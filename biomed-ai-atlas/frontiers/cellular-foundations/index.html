细胞并非一段天然有序的句子。把基因、表达量和空间邻域变成表示，需要尊重测量过程与生物问题。A cell is not a naturally ordered sentence. Representing genes, expression values, and spatial neighborhoods requires attention to measurement and the biological question.

把前沿变成可执行研究Turn the frontier into a runnable study

细胞表征能否越过供体与实验批次？Can a cell representation transfer across donors and batches?

以下是基于本专题原始来源设计的研究方案，列出的指标是计划评价内容，不是已测得的实验结果。The following is a study protocol informed by this topic’s original sources. Metrics are planned evaluations, not claimed experimental results.

最小可复现研究协议A minimum reproducible study protocol
研究问题与可检验主张Question and testable claim固定标签预算后，预训练表征能否在未见供体与实验平台上改善细胞类型或扰动预测？先指定一个任务，再判断所谓“通用”表征支持了哪种迁移。With a fixed label budget, does pretraining improve cell-type or perturbation prediction on unseen donors and platforms? Specify one task first, then state which transfer setting the representation supports.
基线与公平比较Baselines and fair comparison使用相同基因交集与训练供体，比较规范化表达的线性模型、PCA 表征加同类预测头、预训练表征冻结与微调。各方法共享特征过滤规则与标签映射。On identical genes and training donors, compare a linear model on normalized expression, PCA plus a matched prediction head, frozen pretrained features, and fine-tuning. Share feature filtering and label mapping across methods.
数据划分与信息边界Splits and information boundaries先按供体、样本、实验批次分组，再划分细胞。扰动任务分别留出组合、剂量或整种扰动，明确是插值还是新条件外推；预训练中是否出现测试供体也要核对。Group donors, samples, and experimental batches before splitting cells. For perturbation tasks, hold out combinations, doses, or whole perturbations, distinguishing interpolation from new-condition extrapolation. Audit test-donor exposure during pretraining.
消融与受控变化Ablations and controlled changes控制基因数、测序深度、批次协变量和细胞数量；去掉预训练、随机化基因标识、替换缺失基因处理。扰动预测要加入不改变表达的零效应基线。Control gene count, sequencing depth, batch covariates, and cell count. Remove pretraining, randomize gene identifiers, and vary missing-gene handling. Include a zero-effect baseline for perturbation prediction.
终点、单位与区间Endpoints, units, and intervals细胞分类报告按供体汇总的宏平均 F1、少数类型召回与混淆矩阵；扰动预测报告预先指定基因集合的效应误差、差异表达排序与供体间变化。以供体而非单细胞重采样。For classification, report donor-aggregated macro-F1, rare-type recall, and confusion. For perturbations, report effect error on prespecified genes, differential-expression ranking, and between-donor variation. Resample donors rather than individual cells.
失败分析与解释范围Failure analysis and interpretation检查模型是否主要编码平台、文库大小或供体身份。对低表达基因、稀有细胞类型和未见扰动分别分析；总体均值可能被常见细胞和接近零效应的基因主导。Test whether features mainly encode platform, library size, or donor identity. Analyze low-expression genes, rare cell types, and unseen perturbations separately; aggregate means may be dominated by common cells and near-zero-effect genes.

执行前要冻结的检查Checks to freeze before execution

  1. 保存基因词表顺序、计数变换和缺失值规则，避免同名基因对应不同列。Preserve gene-vocabulary order, count transformations, and missing-value rules.
  2. 对测试供体一律不重新拟合规范化或降维参数。Do not refit normalization or dimensionality reduction on test donors.
  3. 报告每个结果对应的独立供体数，而非只报告细胞总数。Report independent donor counts for each result, not only total cell counts.

这一课要理解什么What you will understand

理解基因与细胞表示、批次与生物差异、空间邻域和干预预测的区别，设计按供体与条件外推的验证。Separate gene and cell representations, batch and biological variation, spatial context, and perturbation prediction; design validation that holds out donors and conditions.

基因身份与表达量共同编码Encode gene identity and expression together

单细胞表达矩阵的行是细胞，列是基因，数值受到测序深度、技术噪声和预处理影响。scGPT 将基因身份与表达信息映射到 Transformer 表示，通过预训练后适配细胞注释、多批次整合与扰动预测等任务。与自然语言不同，基因并没有唯一的句子顺序，零表达也不总意味着基因未活跃。比较方法时需要固定基因词表、归一化与输入筛选，加入简单表达特征和常用低维表示作为基线，才能识别预训练本身带来的收益。A single-cell expression matrix indexes cells by rows and genes by columns. Its values depend on sequencing depth, technical variation, and preprocessing. scGPT maps gene identities and expression information into Transformer representations, then adapts pretraining to tasks including cell annotation, batch integration, and perturbation prediction. Unlike language, genes have no unique sentence order, and a zero count does not always imply inactivity. Compare methods using the same gene vocabulary, normalization, and feature-selection procedure. Simple expression features and conventional low-dimensional representations remain important baselines: they establish whether a gain comes from pretraining rather than a different preprocessing pipeline or a larger adaptation budget.

空间关系提供什么新信息What spatial context adds

空间组学保留细胞或采样点的位置，使邻域关系、组织边界与微环境成为可学习对象。图网络可以表示邻接关系，图像编码器可以提供组织形态，但把它们与表达表示结合时，要说明边如何定义以及一个采样点是否混合多个细胞。单细胞基础模型并不自动具有空间推理能力。一个有价值的比较是分别使用表达、坐标、组织图像和三者联合的模型，按切片与供体留出测试，检查空间平滑是否掩盖了真实边界和稀有群体。Spatial omics retains cell or spot locations, making neighborhoods, tissue boundaries, and microenvironments available for learning. Graph models can encode adjacency and image encoders can contribute tissue morphology. Combining these inputs with expression representations requires an explicit definition of graph edges and of whether a spot contains multiple cells. A single-cell foundation model does not automatically acquire spatial reasoning. Compare expression-only, coordinate-only, morphology-only, and combined models with held-out sections and donors. Inspect boundaries and rare populations as well as average prediction quality, because spatial smoothing can improve a global score while erasing precisely the local variation that matters biologically.

从识别状态走向预测干预From recognizing a state to predicting an intervention

细胞类型识别回答“它像什么”，干预预测则回答“改变条件后会怎样”。后者需要区分训练中已见的药物、基因扰动和组合，以及真正未见的条件。随机拆分细胞可能让同一供体、批次或扰动同时进入训练与测试，得到过于容易的任务。更有解释力的设计按扰动、剂量、供体或细胞类型留出，并与未干预均值、简单线性变化等基线比较。预测的基因关联适合形成可检验假设，因果机制还需干预实验进一步确认。Cell annotation asks what a state resembles; perturbation prediction asks what changes when an intervention is applied. The latter requires separating previously observed drugs, genetic perturbations, and combinations from genuinely unseen conditions. A random cell split may put the same donor, batch, or perturbation on both sides, making prediction artificially easy. More informative designs hold out perturbations, doses, donors, or cell types, then compare with untreated means and simple linear-change baselines. Predicted gene associations can generate experimental hypotheses, but association alone does not identify a causal regulatory mechanism. Interventions and appropriately matched controls provide the next layer of evidence.

实验设计：未见供体与未见扰动Study design: unseen donors and perturbations

构造两个独立测试集：一个留出供体但保留已见扰动，另一个留出整个扰动条件。固定预处理，分别报告细胞状态、差异表达与稀有群体的预测效果。若前者表现好而后者退化，说明模型的供体迁移与干预外推能力需要分开描述。Create separate tests for held-out donors under familiar perturbations and for entirely held-out perturbations. Fix preprocessing and assess cell states, differential expression, and rare populations separately. Good donor transfer alongside weak perturbation transfer demonstrates why these capabilities should be reported independently.

再想深一步Take the idea further

嵌入图聚成漂亮的簇，既不能单独证明生物信息完整保留，也不能证明学到了基因调控因果关系。Visually separated embedding clusters alone establish neither preservation of all biological information nor a causal gene-regulatory mechanism.

值得继续研究的问题Questions worth investigating

  1. 预训练表示对新测序平台的收益是否超过简单归一化与批次校正？Do pretrained representations outperform simple normalization and batch correction on a new sequencing platform?
  2. 引入组织邻域后，稀有细胞与边界附近细胞的误差如何变化？How does tissue context affect errors for rare cells and cells near boundaries?
  3. 模型能否预测未见扰动组合，而不是复述训练中的单扰动平均效应？Can the model predict unseen perturbation combinations beyond average single-perturbation effects?

已有进展与下一步Current evidence and next steps

细胞注释与整合已有公开工具和评测；空间推理、未见干预外推与机制发现是可进一步开展实验的方向。Public tools and benchmarks support annotation and integration. Spatial reasoning, unseen-intervention transfer, and mechanism discovery offer directions for further experiments.
