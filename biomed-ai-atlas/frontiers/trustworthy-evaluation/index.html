把前沿变成可执行研究Turn the frontier into a runnable study

置信度与拒答机制，能否在外部人群维持含义？Do confidence and abstention retain their meaning externally?

以下是基于本专题原始来源设计的研究方案，列出的指标是计划评价内容，不是已测得的实验结果。The following is a study protocol informed by this topic’s original sources. Metrics are planned evaluations, not claimed experimental results.

最小可复现研究协议A minimum reproducible study protocol
研究问题与可检验主张Question and testable claim概率校准或集合预测在外部中心、患病率变化与稀有子组中如何变化？先指定校准、覆盖或选择性风险中的一个主目标，不用一个“可信分数”代替全部要求。How do calibration or prediction sets change across sites, prevalence shifts, and rare subgroups? Prespecify calibration, coverage, or selective risk as the primary aim rather than replacing them with one trust score.
基线与公平比较Baselines and fair comparison对同一冻结分类器比较原始分数、温度缩放与预先选定的集合或拒答方法。共享训练与独立校准集，禁止为各方法在测试结果上重新选择阈值。For one frozen classifier, compare raw scores, temperature scaling, and a prespecified set-prediction or abstention method. Share training and independent calibration data; do not select each method’s thresholds using test outcomes.
数据划分与信息边界Splits and information boundaries训练、模型选择、校准和最终测试在患者层面分开。外部中心不参与校准；若研究适配，另设外部校准子集与未使用的外部测试子集，并明确交换性假设。Separate training, model selection, calibration, and final test by patient. Keep external sites outside calibration. For adaptation studies, create distinct external calibration and untouched external test subsets, and state exchangeability assumptions.
消融与受控变化Ablations and controlled changes控制校准集大小、类别比例、设备变化与标签噪声；对原分布与偏移分布分别计算。比较按组与全局校准，但不使用测试组真值回调参数。Vary calibration size, class proportion, device shift, and label noise, evaluating original and shifted distributions separately. Compare groupwise and global calibration without tuning on test-group targets.
终点、单位与区间Endpoints, units, and intervals概率输出报告 Brier 分数、可靠性图和固定分箱规则的 ECE；集合输出报告经验覆盖与集合大小；拒答报告覆盖率、保留样本风险与工作量。所有结果附患者级区间与子组分母。For probabilities, report Brier score, reliability plots, and ECE under fixed bins. For sets, report empirical coverage and set size. For abstention, report retained coverage, risk, and workload. Include patient-level intervals and subgroup denominators.
失败分析与解释范围Failure analysis and interpretation检查高置信错误、少数组覆盖不足、只通过大规模拒答实现低风险，以及偏移后覆盖失效。交换性下的边际覆盖结论不等同于每个患者或每个子组都获得相同保证。Inspect confident errors, subgroup undercoverage, low risk achieved through excessive abstention, and coverage failure under shift. Marginal coverage under exchangeability does not provide the same guarantee for every patient or subgroup.

执行前要冻结的检查Checks to freeze before execution

  1. 主图同时展示风险与保留覆盖率，不能只展示拒答后的准确率。Show risk together with retained coverage rather than only post-abstention accuracy.
  2. 冻结分箱与阈值规则，再读取最终测试标签。Freeze bins and threshold rules before inspecting final test labels.
  3. 记录校准数据与目标使用人群的差异，并将适配分析单独报告。Document differences between calibration and deployment populations and report adaptation separately.

这一课要理解什么What you will understand

把区分能力、校准、亚组误差与实际工作流分开评价，并设计不会把测试集变成调参集的验证计划。Evaluate discrimination, calibration, subgroup errors, and workflow outcomes separately while keeping test data independent of tuning.

同一个模型需要多种证据One model requires several kinds of evidence

区分能力描述阳性与阴性的排序，校准描述概率与观察频率的对应，而阈值决定具体动作的代价。三者不能相互替代：保持样本排序不变的温度缩放可以改变概率，却不会修复排序错误。Guo 等人的研究将现代神经网络的过度自信与校准方法系统化，提供了简单的后处理基线。实际研究应在独立校准数据上确定参数，报告概率质量、灵敏度与特异度，并结合目标患病率解释阳性预测值，避免把所有任务都压缩成一个准确率。Discrimination describes the ordering of positive and negative cases, calibration relates predicted probabilities to observed frequencies, and a threshold turns a score into an action with costs. These properties are not interchangeable. Temperature scaling can change probabilities while preserving sample ordering, so it cannot repair ranking errors. Guo and colleagues systematically studied overconfidence in modern neural networks and provided a simple post-processing baseline. Fit calibration parameters on data kept separate from the final test. Report probability quality, sensitivity, and specificity, and interpret positive predictive value under the intended prevalence rather than reducing every task to accuracy.

平均分数无法覆盖不稳定性An average score does not cover instability

欠确定性研究指出，多个模型可以在同一留出集上表现相近，却在部署条件下行为不同。随机种子、预处理和训练选择都可能改变模型依赖的特征。因此，应将重复训练、跨环境评价与预先定义的压力测试纳入计划，而不只挑最好的一次结果。亚组分析要同时提供样本量和不确定区间，避免把小样本波动当作确定差异。还可以在固定工作量或固定灵敏度下比较模型，让指标对应实际资源约束，而不是只比较无阈值的曲线面积。Underspecification research shows that models with similar held-out performance can behave differently under deployment conditions. Random initialization, preprocessing, and training choices may alter which features a system uses. Repeated training, external environments, and prespecified stress tests therefore belong in the evaluation plan; selecting only the best run hides important variation. Subgroup analyses need sample counts and uncertainty intervals so that small-sample fluctuations are not presented as settled differences. Comparing systems at a fixed workload or sensitivity can also connect evaluation to resource constraints more directly than an area-under-the-curve score alone. Define these comparisons before inspecting the final test outcomes.

评价从模型扩展到人机系统Extend evaluation from the model to the human–AI system

临床转化关注模型与人的共同表现：提示是否改变判断、是否增加复核、能否被正确理解，以及对耗时和结果有何影响。DECIDE-AI 为早期真实临床评价强调使用情境、人因与工作流程的透明报告。研究可以先做回顾性外部验证，再在不影响决策的静默运行中检查数据与延迟，随后按明确方案评估人机协作。每一阶段的结果回答不同问题；算法离线性能提升，需要经过工作流实验，才能说明实际使用是否产生预期收益。Clinical translation concerns the combined system: whether model output changes a decision, adds review work, is understood correctly, or affects time and outcomes. DECIDE-AI emphasizes transparent reporting of context, human factors, and workflow in early live clinical evaluations. A study can progress from retrospective external validation to silent operation that checks data availability and latency without affecting decisions, and then to a defined human–AI evaluation. Each stage answers a different question. An offline algorithmic improvement needs a workflow study before it can support a claim that actual use delivers the intended benefit. The outcome should match the proposed role, such as triage, second reading, or quantitative assistance.

关键公式The key equation

把机制写成公式THE MECHANISM IN AN EQUATION

PPV⁡=Se⁡ πSe⁡ π+(1−Sp⁡)(1−π)\operatorname{PPV}=\frac{\operatorname{Se}\,\pi}{\operatorname{Se}\,\pi+(1-\operatorname{Sp})(1-\pi)}PPV=Seπ+(1−Sp)(1−π)Seπ​

阳性预测值由灵敏度 Se、特异度 Sp 与目标患病率 π 共同决定。即使前两者近似不变，换到低患病率场景后阳性结果的含义也会变化。Positive predictive value depends jointly on sensitivity Se, specificity Sp, and target prevalence π. Even when sensitivity and specificity are approximately stable, a lower-prevalence setting changes the meaning of a positive result.

实验设计：同一工作量下的收益Study design: benefit at the same workload

假设研究目标是分配有限复核资源，先固定每日可复核病例数，再比较模型和现有规则在该工作量下发现的目标病例、遗漏病例与不同亚组覆盖。阈值只用开发数据选择，测试阶段保持锁定。随后用工作流模拟检查排队时间和人工修改量。If the study allocates limited review capacity, fix the number of cases that can be reviewed each day. Compare model and existing-rule case detection, missed cases, and subgroup coverage at that workload. Select thresholds using development data only and lock them for testing. Then use a workflow simulation to examine queues and human corrections.

再想深一步Take the idea further

反复查看测试结果再改阈值、提示词或预处理，会逐渐把测试集变成开发集；最终表现应在保留的独立数据上确认。Repeatedly inspecting test results while changing thresholds, prompts, or preprocessing gradually turns the test set into development data. Confirm final performance on retained independent data.

值得继续研究的问题Questions worth investigating

  1. 模型变化对谁有利、对谁不利，这种差异有多大不确定性？Who benefits or loses from a model change, and how uncertain is that difference?
  2. 在固定复核容量下，收益是否仍然存在？Does the improvement remain under fixed review capacity?
  3. 读者看见概率和证据定位后，判断质量与耗时如何改变？How do probability estimates and localized evidence change reader decisions and time?

已有进展与下一步Current evidence and next steps

校准、外部验证与报告规范已有明确方法；工作流收益必须由对应使用场景的研究测量。作者温度缩放仓库是历史演示代码，已停止维护。Calibration, external validation, and reporting guidelines offer established methods. Workflow benefit must be measured in its intended setting. The authors’ temperature-scaling repository is an unmaintained historical demonstration.
