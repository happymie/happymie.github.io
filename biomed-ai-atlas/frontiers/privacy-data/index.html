以数据为中心的学习与隐私计算Data-centric learning and privacy-preserving computation

模型能力取决于数据如何产生、覆盖谁、如何标注与共享。多中心学习需要同时处理统计异质性与信息保护。Model capability depends on how data is produced, whose cases it covers, and how it is labeled and shared. Multi-site learning must address statistical heterogeneity alongside information protection.

把前沿变成可执行研究Turn the frontier into a runnable study

把隐私预算与任务效用放在同一张实验表Study privacy budget and task utility together

以下是基于本专题原始来源设计的研究方案，列出的指标是计划评价内容，不是已测得的实验结果。The following is a study protocol informed by this topic’s original sources. Metrics are planned evaluations, not claimed experimental results.

最小可复现研究协议A minimum reproducible study protocol
研究问题与可检验主张Question and testable claim在明确攻击者能力与数据分布下，所选隐私机制减少了哪种泄漏，同时付出多少任务效用与计算成本？将访问控制、更新保护与模型输出泄漏分开。Under a defined attacker and data distribution, which leakage does the mechanism reduce, and at what utility and computational cost? Separate access control, update protection, and leakage through model outputs.
基线与公平比较Baselines and fair comparison比较各中心独立模型、普通联邦聚合、仅裁剪更新与加入可核算差分隐私噪声的聚合；若有授权，集中训练仅作为信息上限对照。固定患者、轮次与参与率。Compare local-site models, ordinary federated aggregation, clipping-only aggregation, and aggregation with accounted differential-privacy noise. If authorized, centralized training is an information-upper-bound comparator. Match patients, rounds, and participation.
数据划分与信息边界Splits and information boundaries按患者留出效用测试集；攻击训练、攻击验证与目标模型测试中的对象保持隔离。分别模拟知道模型输出、聚合更新或单个客户端更新的攻击者，而不是混用威胁模型。Hold out patients for utility evaluation. Separate attack training, attack validation, and target-model test identities. Model attackers observing outputs, aggregate updates, or individual client updates separately rather than mixing threat models.
消融与受控变化Ablations and controlled changes固定训练步骤，改变裁剪阈值、噪声与采样率；报告选定会计方法计算的 ε、δ。另关闭安全聚合，区分其对更新可见性的作用与差分隐私对输出分布的约束。With training steps fixed, vary clipping threshold, noise, and sampling. Report ε and δ from a specified accountant. Separately remove secure aggregation to distinguish update visibility from the output-distribution constraint of differential privacy.
终点、单位与区间Endpoints, units, and intervals同时报告任务 AUROC、冻结阈值敏感度、子组效用、隐私预算、成员推断攻击优势与成本。攻击未成功只是该攻击下的观察；差分隐私声明需要对应完整训练流程的数学核算。Report task AUROC, frozen-threshold sensitivity, subgroup utility, privacy budget, membership-inference advantage, and cost. An unsuccessful attack is evidence for that attack only; a differential-privacy claim requires accounting for the full training process.
失败分析与解释范围Failure analysis and interpretation分析少样本中心、罕见类别、非独立同分布客户端与重复参与。记录预算是否因多次调参、重新训练与发布而组合；不要把不上传原始数据视为最终模型不会泄漏的证明。Analyze small sites, rare classes, non-IID clients, and repeated participation. Track composition across tuning, retraining, and releases. Keeping raw data local is not itself proof against leakage from a final model.

执行前要冻结的检查Checks to freeze before execution

  1. 保存会计器输入、采样假设与总步数，使预算可以重新计算。Preserve accountant inputs, sampling assumptions, and step count so the budget can be recomputed.
  2. 训练机制与隐私声明的保护单位一致：记录是按患者、图像还是客户端保护。Match the mechanism and claimed privacy unit: patient, image, or client.
  3. 攻击结果附独立样本数与区间，并与随机猜测对照。Report attack sample counts and intervals against a random-guessing comparator.

这一课要理解什么What you will understand

区分数据质量改进、联邦优化与差分隐私的目标，明确保护单位，并设计包含弱势中心与标注预算的实验。Distinguish data-quality improvement, federated optimization, and differential privacy; define the protected unit and design studies that account for smaller sites and labeling budgets.

先改进学习问题，再扩大数据量Improve the learning problem before scaling the dataset

数据中心方法把样本覆盖、标签定义、重复记录与错误标注作为可优化对象。医学数据中，同一标签可能来自报告抽取、医生共识或随访结果，它们对应不同噪声与时间含义。研究可先建立小规模高质量核查集，再按错误类型决定补标、复核或重采样。主动学习关注“下一批标哪个样本更有价值”，但只选最不确定样本可能持续集中在噪声或少数机构。应按患者与中心追踪标注收益，并比较随机抽样和按亚组补足的简单策略。A data-centric approach treats coverage, label definitions, duplicates, and annotation errors as variables to improve. A medical label extracted from a report differs from one established by clinician consensus or follow-up; their noise and temporal meanings are not interchangeable. A small, carefully audited reference set can guide relabeling, review, and resampling decisions. Active learning asks which examples are worth labeling next, but selecting only the most uncertain observations can repeatedly target noise or a small set of sites. Track labeling gains by patient and center, and compare with random sampling and straightforward subgroup-coverage strategies before attributing improvements to a sophisticated selection rule.

联邦学习改变协作方式Federated learning changes how sites collaborate

FedAvg 让参与方在本地训练，再聚合模型更新，使训练数据可以留在各自环境。医学多中心的困难常在非独立同分布：中心样本量、设备、患病率与标注规则不同，简单按样本数加权可能让大型中心主导目标。可以比较共享模型、局部适配和个性化分支，并把最差中心表现与参与成本单独列出。数据留在本地并不自动保证模型更新没有泄漏；传输加密、访问控制、安全聚合与统计隐私解决不同问题，需要按具体威胁模型组合。Federated averaging trains locally at participating clients and then aggregates model updates, allowing training data to remain in their own environments. Medical centers differ in sample size, devices, prevalence, and labeling rules. Weighting only by sample count may make large sites dominate the objective. Compare a shared model, local adaptation, and personalized components while reporting worst-site performance and participation cost. Keeping data local does not by itself establish that updates reveal no information. Transport encryption, access control, secure aggregation, and statistical privacy address different questions. Their combination should follow an explicit account of who can observe what, rather than treating federation as a universal privacy guarantee.

差分隐私需要明确保护单位Differential privacy needs an explicit protected unit

差分隐私限制相邻数据集产生输出分布的差异。DP-SGD 通过裁剪单样本梯度、加入噪声并累计隐私消耗实现训练机制，但医学场景首先要定义“相邻”：删除一张图像、一次就诊或一个人的全部记录，保护目标并不相同。重复就诊若按独立样本计数，会使患者级解释失真。评测应同时报告保护单位、隐私参数、采样与训练步数，以及整体和亚组效用。下一步研究在于有限预算下如何兼顾稀有病例、小中心与可用的模型性能。Differential privacy bounds how much the output distribution can change between neighboring datasets. DP-SGD clips per-example gradients, adds noise, and accounts for privacy loss over training. In medical data, the first question is what neighboring means: removing one image, one visit, or all records of one person defines different protection. Treating repeated visits as independent examples can invalidate a patient-level interpretation. Report the protected unit, privacy parameters, sampling procedure, and training steps alongside overall and subgroup utility. An important research direction is how to preserve useful performance for rare cases and small centers under a specified privacy budget, instead of reporting a single accuracy–privacy trade-off for the full population.

关键公式The key equation

把机制写成公式THE MECHANISM IN AN EQUATION

Pr⁡[M(D)∈S]≤eϵPr⁡[M(D′)∈S]+δ\Pr[M(D)\in S]\leq e^{\epsilon}\Pr[M(D')\in S]+\deltaPr[M(D)∈S]≤eϵPr[M(D′)∈S]+δ

对任意相邻数据集 D、D′ 和输出集合 S，机制 M 满足此不等式即为 (ε,δ) 差分隐私。相邻关系定义保护对象；参数需要结合训练过程与会计方法解释。For every pair of neighboring datasets D and D′ and output set S, this inequality defines (ε,δ)-differential privacy for mechanism M. The neighboring relation defines what is protected; interpret the parameters with the training procedure and privacy accountant.

实验设计：固定标注与隐私预算Study design: fixed labeling and privacy budgets

用可公开研究的数据模拟多个中心，按机构规模和标签分布保留异质性。固定新增标注数量，比较随机补标、主动学习、普通联邦训练与患者级隐私训练。分别报告各中心效用、稀有类别召回、通信与计算成本，并清楚说明这是模拟多中心条件。Simulate multiple centers from data approved for public research, retaining differences in site size and label distributions. Under a fixed additional-labeling budget, compare random annotation, active learning, ordinary federated training, and patient-level private training. Report per-site utility, rare-class recall, communication, and computation, explicitly describing the setting as a simulation.

再想深一步Take the idea further

“不上传原始数据”与“提供患者级差分隐私”是不同承诺；只给出隐私参数而不说明相邻关系，也无法判断保护范围。Not uploading raw data and providing patient-level differential privacy are different claims. Privacy parameters without a neighboring relation do not specify the protection scope.

值得继续研究的问题Questions worth investigating

  1. 相同补标预算下，覆盖稀有病例比单纯选择高不确定样本更有效吗？Under the same labeling budget, is rare-case coverage more useful than uncertainty selection alone?
  2. 大型中心收益与最差中心收益能否同时提高？Can a method improve both large-site utility and worst-site utility?
  3. 隐私训练的效用损失是否集中在稀有类别或小样本人群？Does the utility loss from private training concentrate in rare classes or smaller populations?

已有进展与下一步Current evidence and next steps

联邦优化与差分隐私已有公开实现；跨机构研究仍需匹配实际的数据治理、标签定义与参与条件。Federated optimization and differential privacy have public implementations. Cross-institution studies must still match actual data governance, label definitions, and participation conditions.
