世界模型与具身医疗研究World models and embodied medical research

当模型需要改变环境，感知、动作表示与反馈就成为一个闭环。能生成逼真视频，与能可靠地预测行动后果，是不同能力。When a model acts on an environment, perception, action representation, and feedback form a closed loop. Generating plausible video and reliably predicting action consequences are different capabilities.

把前沿变成可执行研究Turn the frontier into a runnable study

预测下一帧，与控制下一步，是两种检验Predicting a frame and controlling a step are different tests

以下是基于本专题原始来源设计的研究方案，列出的指标是计划评价内容，不是已测得的实验结果。The following is a study protocol informed by this topic’s original sources. Metrics are planned evaluations, not claimed experimental results.

最小可复现研究协议A minimum reproducible study protocol
研究问题与可检验主张Question and testable claim学习到的状态转移模型能否改善闭环任务完成，而不只是降低视频预测误差？把动作条件、状态可观测性与规划时域明确写入问题。Does a learned transition model improve closed-loop completion rather than only video-prediction error? Specify action conditioning, observability, and planning horizon.
基线与公平比较Baselines and fair comparison在同一模拟任务中比较行为克隆、使用已知状态的规划器与学习世界模型的规划器；共享演示、动作限制、交互预算与奖励定义。已知状态规划器是可观察性对照，不当成同等传感条件。Within one simulated task, compare behavior cloning, a planner with known state, and a learned-world-model planner. Match demonstrations, action limits, interaction budget, and reward definition. The known-state planner is an observability comparator, not an identical sensing condition.
数据划分与信息边界Splits and information boundaries按完整任务轨迹、操作员与场景布局划分，不能随机打散相邻帧。另留出几何、材质、光照或动力学条件，分开检验视觉变化与物理变化。Split complete trajectories, operators, and layouts rather than adjacent frames. Hold out geometry, materials, lighting, or dynamics, and test visual changes separately from physical changes.
消融与受控变化Ablations and controlled changes移除动作条件、缩短或延长滚动预测时域、替换潜在状态、关闭不确定性代价。加入可控延迟与接触参数变化，在模拟环境复核规划是否仍满足同一动作边界。Remove action conditioning, vary rollout horizon, replace latent state, and remove uncertainty costs. Add controlled latency and contact-parameter shifts in simulation, checking that the planner respects the same action limits.
终点、单位与区间Endpoints, units, and intervals开放环报告多步状态与图像误差；闭环报告任务成功、约束违反、累计代价、失败恢复与控制延迟。按完整轨迹重复种子与初态，报告成对差值，而不是把每帧当独立试验。For open loop, report multistep state and image errors. For closed loop, report success, constraint violations, cumulative cost, recovery, and control latency. Repeat seeds and initial states by full trajectory and report paired differences rather than treating frames as independent trials.
失败分析与解释范围Failure analysis and interpretation重点分析误差累积、未建模接触、不可观测状态与规划利用模型漏洞。预测视频看起来平滑不代表动作后果正确；模拟改善也不能直接当成真实医疗设备的有效性结论。Analyze accumulated error, unmodeled contact, hidden state, and planners exploiting model flaws. Smooth predicted video does not establish correct action consequences, and simulated improvement does not establish effectiveness for real medical equipment.

执行前要冻结的检查Checks to freeze before execution

  1. 保存完整初态、动作、状态转移与终态验收脚本，允许逐轨迹重放。Preserve initial state, actions, transitions, and end-state checks for trajectory replay.
  2. 固定控制频率与延迟计时范围，让不同方法的交互预算可比。Fix control frequency and latency boundaries to compare interaction budgets.
  3. 将开放环预测结论与闭环控制结论分别写出。State open-loop prediction and closed-loop control conclusions separately.

这一课要理解什么What you will understand

区分场景重建、模仿学习与世界模型，理解动作坐标与长期误差，并设计面向扰动和恢复的闭环评价。Distinguish scene reconstruction, imitation learning, and world models; understand action coordinates and compounding errors; design closed-loop tests of perturbation and recovery.

从识别图像到观察行动后果From recognizing images to predicting consequences

静态识别模型把观测映射到标签；策略把观测映射到动作；世界模型则学习状态如何在动作作用下变化。DreamerV3 从经验学习潜在动态，并在想象轨迹中训练策略，为研究样本效率和长期规划提供了通用例子。这里的状态可以压缩图像，但还要保留动作相关信息。医学场景中的组织接触、遮挡和器械移动，使这种表示选择更困难。一个场景重建模型即使渲染逼真，也未必理解力学变化，不能直接当作可用于规划的环境模型。A static recognition model maps observations to labels, a policy maps observations to actions, and a world model learns how states evolve under actions. DreamerV3 learns latent dynamics from experience and trains a policy through imagined trajectories, offering a general example for studying sample efficiency and longer-term planning. A compressed state must preserve information relevant to actions, not just image appearance. Tissue contact, occlusion, and instrument motion make this choice especially important in medical research. A scene-reconstruction model may render convincing views while lacking a model of mechanical change. Rendering quality alone therefore does not establish that the representation can support planning.

动作表示常比换大网络更关键Action representation can matter more than model size

Surgical Robot Transformer 研究在达芬奇研究平台上模仿组织操作、持针和打结等基础任务。作者发现近似运动学使绝对位姿动作难以稳定复现，转而使用相对动作，并比较工具与相机相关坐标。其启示是感知与控制之间存在接口：模型输出什么坐标、一次预测多长动作块、何时重新观察，都会改变闭环行为。训练数据也应按完整示范轨迹划分，防止相邻视频帧跨集合泄漏；损失降低还需由未见条件下的任务完成验证。Surgical Robot Transformer studies imitation of tissue manipulation, needle handling, and knot tying on the da Vinci Research Kit. Approximate kinematics make absolute-pose actions difficult to reproduce reliably; the authors investigate relative actions and compare tool- and camera-related coordinate choices. The broader lesson is that perception and control meet through an interface. Output coordinates, the duration of an action chunk, and when the system observes again all affect closed-loop behavior. Split demonstrations by complete trajectories to keep adjacent video frames from leaking across sets. A lower training loss still needs task-completion tests under unseen conditions before it supports a claim of better control.

下一步：接触、扰动与误差恢复Next questions: contact, perturbations, and recovery

具身系统的误差会影响下一步观测，长任务因而可能放大单步偏差。评价不能只用离线动作误差，还应覆盖相机变化、工具重装、物体位姿改变、接触状态和执行延迟。世界模型可用于预测候选动作的后果，但规划可能利用模拟器的漏洞，导致想象成功、真实失败。研究应比较开放环与反馈控制，测量任务完成、作用力、失败恢复和计算延迟，并在明确的实验平台与材料条件下报告结果，让模型能力与演示范围对应。In an embodied system, one error changes the next observation, so long tasks can amplify small one-step deviations. Evaluation must go beyond offline action error to camera changes, tool reinstallation, object placement, contact states, and execution delay. A world model can predict candidate action outcomes, but a planner may exploit model inaccuracies and achieve imagined success without real success. Compare open-loop execution with feedback control and measure completion, forces, failure recovery, and latency. Describe the experimental platform and material conditions precisely. This ties the capability claim to the demonstrated setting and makes it possible to investigate which feedback signals actually improve recovery.

世界模型预测动作 a_t 之后潜在状态 z 的变化，策略 π 根据当前表示选择动作。该式是简化示意；具体系统可能加入历史、随机隐变量、奖励与观测重建。A world model predicts the latent state after action a_t, while policy π selects an action from the current representation. This is a simplified schematic; implementations may include history, stochastic latent variables, rewards, and observation reconstruction.

实验设计：小扰动后的恢复能力Study design: recovery after a small perturbation

在仿真或台架任务中固定初始示范，分别改变相机角度、目标位置与动作延迟。比较一次输出整段动作、定期重规划和持续反馈三种策略，记录成功率、路径偏差、恢复次数与耗时。把无接触移动和接触操作分开，观察模型是否学到了对任务有用的动态。In a simulation or benchtop task, fix the initial demonstrations and vary camera angle, target position, and action delay. Compare a complete open-loop action sequence, periodic replanning, and continuous feedback. Record completion, path deviation, recovery attempts, and time. Analyze free-space motion separately from contact manipulation to test whether the learned dynamics are useful for the task.

再想深一步Take the idea further

逼真的场景重建、低离线动作误差与闭环任务成功是不同指标；任何一种单独提高，都不足以推出其余能力。Realistic scene reconstruction, low offline action error, and closed-loop task completion measure different properties. Improving one does not establish the others.

值得继续研究的问题Questions worth investigating

  1. 潜在状态中需要保留哪些接触信息，才能支持规划？Which contact information must a latent state retain to support planning?
  2. 相对动作在更换工具或相机后是否仍稳定？Do relative actions remain stable after a tool or camera change?
  3. 世界模型的不确定性是否能预测闭环失败并触发有效重规划？Can world-model uncertainty predict closed-loop failure and trigger useful replanning?

已有进展与下一步Current evidence and next steps

世界模型已有通用控制基准，手术模仿学习已有台架任务演示；这些结果对应具体实验条件，不能合并表述为已实现自主临床手术。World models have general-control benchmarks, and surgical imitation learning has benchtop task demonstrations. These are setting-specific results rather than evidence of autonomous clinical surgery.
