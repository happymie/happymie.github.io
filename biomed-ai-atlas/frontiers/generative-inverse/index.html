生成模型与医学逆问题Generative models for medical inverse problems

从少量测量恢复图像，需要让数据约束与学习先验共同起作用，并检查看似合理的细节是否得到测量支持。Recovering an image from limited measurements requires both a measurement constraint and a learned prior, together with tests of whether plausible details are supported by the data.

把前沿变成可执行研究Turn the frontier into a runnable study

漂亮的重建，是否受这次测量支持？Is a convincing reconstruction supported by this measurement?

以下是基于本专题原始来源设计的研究方案，列出的指标是计划评价内容，不是已测得的实验结果。The following is a study protocol informed by this topic’s original sources. Metrics are planned evaluations, not claimed experimental results.

最小可复现研究协议A minimum reproducible study protocol
研究问题与可检验主张Question and testable claim在固定采样算子、噪声水平与推理预算下，生成先验是否改善小结构恢复，同时保持测量一致性？主要对象是同一观测的重建，不是不同图像之间的视觉偏好。With acquisition operator, noise level, and inference budget fixed, does a generative prior recover small structures while retaining measurement consistency? Compare reconstructions of the same observation rather than preferences across unrelated images.
基线与公平比较Baselines and fair comparison比较传统数据一致性加正则化、展开网络与生成后验方法；为每名测试对象使用同一组原始测量。超参数只用独立验证集选择，并记录迭代数、采样步数和精度设置。Compare measurement-consistent regularization, an unrolled network, and a generative posterior method on identical raw measurements for each case. Tune only on validation and record iterations, sampling steps, and numerical precision.
数据划分与信息边界Splits and information boundaries按患者与采集中心留出，序列与相邻切片保持分组。对采样掩码、加速率及噪声做预先定义的内部分布与外部分布测试；不得从同一次完整采集分别构造训练与测试对象。Hold out patients and acquisition sites; keep sequences and neighboring slices grouped. Prespecify in-distribution and shifted sampling masks, acceleration, and noise. Do not construct training and test cases from the same full acquisition.
消融与受控变化Ablations and controlled changes在相同观测上关闭数据一致性、替换学习先验、减少采样步数，并用多个固定种子重复。加入已知位置与幅度的小结构扰动，追踪其测量信号与重建变化。For the same observations, remove data consistency, replace the learned prior, reduce sampling steps, and repeat fixed seeds. Add small structures at known locations and amplitudes to trace their measurement signatures and reconstruction.
终点、单位与区间Endpoints, units, and intervals同时记录归一化测量残差 ||A(x̂)−y||/||y||、像素与结构误差、小结构检出、端到端延迟与显存。多样本分歧按空间位置绘制，不自动称为校准不确定性。Report normalized measurement residual ||A(x̂)−y||/||y||, pixel and structural errors, small-structure detection, end-to-end latency, and memory. Map sample disagreement spatially without automatically interpreting it as calibrated uncertainty.
失败分析与解释范围Failure analysis and interpretation逐例检查罕见结构被抹除、常见纹理被补出、算子失配和噪声失配。若像素指标提高而小结构检出下降，应保留这项取舍，不能只挑最好看的采样结果。Inspect removal of rare structures, insertion of common textures, operator mismatch, and noise mismatch. Retain trade-offs such as better pixel scores but worse small-structure detection; do not select only the most attractive sample.

执行前要冻结的检查Checks to freeze before execution

  1. 为每个病例保存算子版本、采样掩码摘要、噪声设定、种子与残差计算脚本。Store operator version, mask digest, noise setting, seed, and residual-computation code per case.
  2. 小结构扰动实验应包含无扰动配对与空白对照。Include paired unperturbed inputs and blank controls for small-structure experiments.
  3. 时间从读取规定输入开始，到输出可评价结果结束，计入必要采样与数据一致性计算。Time the full specified input-to-evaluable-output path, including required sampling and data-consistency operations.

这一课要理解什么What you will understand

用前向模型与后验分布解释重建，区分视觉逼真、测量一致和任务有效，并构造可复查的比较实验。Explain reconstruction through a forward model and posterior distribution, distinguish visual realism from measurement consistency and task utility, and design an auditable comparison.

测量方程决定什么能被恢复The measurement equation determines what can be recovered

逆问题从测量过程出发：磁共振采集频域信号，CT 采集投影，降采样与噪声使多幅图像可能对应相近测量。正则化或学习先验用于在这些候选中选择合理解，但不能凭空增加实际测量的信息。展开网络把迭代优化步骤写成可训练模块，VarNet 等方案则将数据一致性与神经网络更新结合。阅读架构时，应找出前向算子、噪声假设、采样方式和每一步的数据约束，而不是只比较网络名称或最终图像。An inverse problem begins with how observations are acquired. MRI measures frequency-domain signals, while CT measures projections. Undersampling and noise can make different images produce similar observations. Regularization or a learned prior selects among these candidates, but does not create new measured information. Unrolled networks express iterative optimization as trainable modules; approaches such as VarNet combine neural updates with data consistency. When reading an architecture, identify the forward operator, noise assumptions, sampling pattern, and where the measurement constraint enters each update. Those choices explain the reconstruction problem more precisely than the network name or a visually appealing output alone.

扩散先验如何与观测结合How a diffusion prior meets an observation

扩散模型通过逐步去噪学习图像分布。用于逆问题时，可以在采样过程中加入由观测计算的引导，近似得到给定测量后的图像分布。DPS 针对含噪声的线性与非线性问题，将扩散更新与测量相关梯度结合；它采用后验近似，并非精确求解任意物理系统。同一观测生成多个样本有助于发现不确定区域，但样本相似也可能来自先验偏好。需要同步观察测量残差、不同采样种子、结构保持与下游任务结果。A diffusion model learns an image distribution through progressive denoising. For an inverse problem, observation-derived guidance can be introduced during sampling to approximate the distribution of images given the measurements. Diffusion Posterior Sampling combines diffusion updates with a measurement-related gradient for noisy linear and nonlinear problems. Its posterior is approximate rather than an exact solution for arbitrary acquisition physics. Multiple samples from one observation can help expose ambiguous regions, but agreement among samples may also reflect a shared prior bias. Evaluate measurement residuals, variation across sampling seeds, preservation of relevant structures, and downstream task outcomes together instead of interpreting sample variability as automatically calibrated uncertainty.

前沿在可验证的细节与可承受的计算The frontier is verifiable detail at a usable computational cost

研究可沿两个方向推进：一是让生成细节具有可追溯的观测依据，二是在较少步数下保持稳定重建。流匹配、一致性训练和蒸馏改变了生成路径与计算成本，但加速效果必须放回具体采样率和噪声条件检验。比较时除像素指标外，还可用受控小结构、真实异常和任务读片评价，检查先验是否抹除罕见结构。应固定训练数据与测量算子，分开报告运行时间、显存、测量误差和任务表现，避免单个综合分数掩盖取舍。Two useful research directions are to make generated details traceable to observations and to obtain stable reconstruction with fewer sampling steps. Flow matching, consistency training, and distillation alter generation paths and computational cost, but acceleration must be tested at specified sampling rates and noise levels. Alongside pixel metrics, controlled small structures, real abnormalities, and task-based reading studies can reveal whether a prior removes rare findings. Hold training data and the acquisition operator fixed when comparing methods. Report latency, memory, measurement error, and task performance separately so that a composite score does not hide the trade-off between speed and preservation of meaningful information.

关键公式The key equation

把机制写成公式THE MECHANISM IN AN EQUATION

y=A(x)+ε,p(x∣y)∝p(y∣x) p(x)y=\mathcal{A}(x)+\varepsilon,\qquad p(x\mid y)\propto p(y\mid x)\,p(x)y=A(x)+ε,p(x∣y)∝p(y∣x)p(x)

前向算子 A 把待恢复图像 x 转成测量 y，ε 表示噪声。后验结合观测似然与图像先验；具体噪声模型和算子决定似然，不能只用通用去噪器替代。The forward operator A maps an image x to measurements y, with noise ε. The posterior combines a measurement likelihood with an image prior. The likelihood depends on the acquisition and noise model, not just on a generic denoiser.

实验设计：同一测量的三种答案Study design: three answers to the same measurement

固定一组欠采样测量，比较传统正则化、展开网络和扩散后验采样。使用相同的测试病例，记录测量残差、结构误差、异常检出和推理时间；扩散方法额外报告多次采样的分歧。将所有方法都看不清的小结构单独列出，区分测量不足与方法失败。Use a fixed undersampled measurement set to compare conventional regularization, an unrolled network, and diffusion posterior sampling. For the same cases, record measurement residuals, structural errors, abnormality detection, and inference time. Also report disagreement across diffusion samples. Analyze small structures missed by every method separately to distinguish insufficient measurements from method-specific failures.

再想深一步Take the idea further

更像高质量图像不等于更忠实于该次采集；视觉指标可能奖励由先验补出的常见纹理，同时忽略罕见病变。Looking more like a high-quality image does not establish fidelity to this acquisition; perceptual metrics may reward common textures supplied by a prior while overlooking rare findings.

值得继续研究的问题Questions worth investigating

  1. 哪些结构的恢复主要由测量决定，哪些更多依赖先验？Which recovered structures are determined mainly by measurements, and which rely more strongly on the prior?
  2. 改变采集设备或噪声分布后，快速采样还稳定吗？Does accelerated sampling remain stable after a change in scanner or noise distribution?
  3. 样本间分歧能否预测真实重建错误，而非只反映采样随机性？Can between-sample disagreement predict actual reconstruction errors rather than sampling randomness alone?

已有进展与下一步Current evidence and next steps

展开重建与生成先验都有成熟的研究基线；不同设备、器官和异常下的任务证据需要分别建立。Both unrolled reconstruction and generative priors have established research baselines. Task evidence must be established separately across devices, organs, and abnormalities.
