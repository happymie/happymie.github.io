Percival

把三维 CT 与放射学报告分别编码，用对称对比学习对齐全体扫描的视觉和语言表征，再以冻结图像向量支持检索、零样本分类及独立拟合的疾病与风险模型。Separate encoders align whole-volume CT and radiology-report representations through symmetric contrastive learning. Frozen image embeddings support retrieval, zero-shot classification and separately fitted disease or prognostic models. E1

结构总览ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW

Percival，从结构到机制。Percival: structure to mechanism.

输入INPUT

训练输入配对的三维 CT 与放射学报告。期刊版本将 CT 重采样为 1×1×3 mm，截断 HU 至 −1000～1000 并归一化到 [0,1]，补齐、中心裁剪为 XYZ 轴 352×352×128；图像塔的张量顺序为 B×1×128×352×352。Training takes paired 3D CT volumes and radiology reports. The journal pipeline resamples CT to 1×1×3 mm, clips HU to −1000…1000, normalizes to [0,1], then pads and center-crops to XYZ dimensions 352×352×128. The vision tensor is B×1×128×352×352.

→
输出OUTPUT

图像与文本位于同一表示空间。Tiny、Small、Base 的共享维度分别为 192、384、768；Base 输出 768 维图像或文本向量。相似度用于跨模态检索及提示词分类，独立训练的下游头再输出疾病概率或 Cox 风险分数。Image and text lie in a shared space of 192, 384 or 768 dimensions for Tiny, Small and Base. Base produces a 768-dimensional image or text vector. Similarity supports cross-modal retrieval and prompt classification; separately trained downstream heads produce disease probabilities or Cox risk scores.

关键设计DESIGN DECISION

期刊路线以 AugReg ViT 的三维分块与 CXR-BERT-specialized 组成双塔，并在不同视野、造影条件和临床用途的配对数据上统一训练。The journal route pairs a 3D-patch AugReg ViT with CXR-BERT-specialized and trains jointly across fields of view, contrast settings and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    体数据标准化Volume standardization

    统一朝向、物理间隔、HU 范围和空间尺寸。XYZ 与 PyTorch 的 DHW 顺序要区分；发布配置把 XYZ 尺寸反转后交给视觉塔，预处理后的间隔不能由张量尺寸直接推断。Standardize orientation, physical spacing, HU range and spatial size. Distinguish XYZ from PyTorch DHW: the release reverses XYZ image dimensions before building the vision tower, and tensor size alone does not establish voxel spacing. E1

  2. 02

    三维卷积分块3D convolutional patches

    以空间 XYZ 的 16×16×8 体素为块；实现的 DHW 核与步长为 8×16×16。128×352×352 输入得到 16×22×22=7744 个区域标记，三维卷积将每块映射为 Transformer 特征。Use XYZ patches of 16×16×8 voxels, implemented with DHW kernel and stride 8×16×16. A 128×352×352 volume yields a 16×22×22 grid, or 7,744 regional tokens, projected into Transformer features by a 3D convolution. E1

  3. 03

    位置编码与全局标记Position encoding and global token

    为区域标记加入三维空间位置，前置一个 CLS 全局标记。固定代码对 AugReg 位置编码进行空间适配；完整序列包含 7744 个区域标记与 1 个全局标记。Add 3D spatial position information and prepend a CLS token. The pinned implementation adapts AugReg position embeddings; the full sequence contains 7,744 regional tokens and one global token. E1

  4. 04

    AugReg 视觉 TransformerAugReg vision Transformer

    在完整体数据序列上做多头自注意力。CLS 与所有区域标记双向交换信息，在多层中聚合扫描级结构；这里不是逐切片独立编码后再做层级注意力。Multi-head self-attention operates on the full volume sequence. CLS exchanges information with all regional tokens and aggregates scan-level structure across layers; this is not independent per-slice encoding followed by hierarchical attention. E1

  5. 05

    图像投影与归一化Image projection and normalization

    取最后一层归一化后的 CLS 输出，再以线性头映射到共享维度。Base 为 768 维；计算对比相似度前做 L2 归一化，使内积对应余弦相似度。Take the final normalized CLS output and project it linearly into the shared space, 768 dimensions for Base. L2 normalization before contrastive similarity makes the dot product a cosine similarity. E1

  6. 06

    报告文本编码Report text encoding

    另一条并行路径将报告分词并提供注意力掩码，送入 CXR-BERT-specialized。固定语言塔取第一个标记的最终隐状态，再通过线性投影与对应视觉变体对齐。A parallel path tokenizes the report, supplies an attention mask and encodes it with CXR-BERT-specialized. The pinned language tower takes the first-token final hidden state and linearly projects it to the matching vision variant’s shared dimension. E1

  7. 07

    批内图文相似度矩阵In-batch image–text similarity

    对 B 个扫描与 B 份报告形成 B×B 相似度矩阵。对角线对应真实图文对，其余项是批内负对；分布式训练先汇集多 GPU 特征，扩充对比集合。A batch of B scans and B reports forms a B×B similarity matrix. The diagonal contains matched pairs and off-diagonal entries provide in-batch negatives. Distributed training gathers features across GPUs before constructing the contrastive set. E1

  8. 08

    双向对比目标Symmetric contrastive objective

    同时优化图像找报告与报告找图像，两个方向的交叉熵取平均。相似度的缩放影响 softmax 锐度；公式的除数温度与代码的乘数缩放须按各自约定解释。Optimize image-to-report and report-to-image matching, averaging cross-entropy in the two directions. Similarity scaling controls softmax sharpness; divisor temperature in the formula and multiplier scale in code must be interpreted under their own conventions. E1

  9. 09

    检索与提示词分类Retrieval and prompt classification

    推理时可只编码 CT，并与预先编码的报告或“病变存在／不存在”提示比较。图文向量是匹配表示；双编码器本身不逐词生成报告，也不输出分割掩码。At inference, CT embeddings can be compared with pre-encoded reports or pathology-present/pathology-absent prompts. These are matching representations: the dual encoder itself does not generate report tokens or segmentation masks. E1

  10. 10

    冻结表征后的任务建模Task models on frozen representations

    将图像塔的向量作为独立模型的输入。论文疾病分类使用按解剖视野分别拟合的 L1 逻辑回归，预后使用 ridge Cox 模型；这些拟合目标与图文预训练目标不同。Use vision-tower vectors as inputs to separate models. The paper fits field-of-view-specific L1 logistic regressions for disease classification and ridge Cox models for prognosis. Their fitting objectives differ from image–text pretraining. E1

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

窄解剖视野、单一造影条件或单一临床任务上的训练，限制了 CT 表征覆盖真实扫描差异的能力。Training in narrow anatomical windows, contrast settings or clinical tasks limits coverage of real-world CT variation.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

将全体 CT 的三维视觉编码器与报告文本编码器对齐，让医学报告提供扫描级语义监督。Align a whole-volume 3D vision encoder with a report encoder so that radiology text supplies scan-level semantic supervision.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

同一 CT 表征被用于跨模态检索、胸部病变提示词分类、实验室指标关联、疾病分类和纵向风险研究；后几项通过独立统计或预测模型实现。The same CT representation supports retrieval, thoracic prompt classification, laboratory associations, disease classification and longitudinal risk research. The latter uses separate statistical or predictive models.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 三维块包含局部体素关系，完整序列注意力再连接远距离区域。以给定尺寸计算，区域标记数为 (128÷8)×(352÷16)×(352÷16)=7744，加上 CLS 后共 7745；输入分辨率改变会显著改变注意力计算规模。Volumetric patches encode local voxel relationships, while full-sequence attention links distant regions. At the stated size, (128÷8)×(352÷16)×(352÷16)=7,744 regional tokens plus CLS gives 7,745 tokens. Resolution changes can substantially alter attention cost. E1
  • 两条编码路径在相似度空间相遇，不是在视觉编码中交叉注意全部报告词元。配对监督鼓励扫描与其报告相近，但报告未描述的影像细节不一定得到同等强度监督。The two encoders meet in similarity space rather than cross-attending every report token inside the vision tower. Pair supervision brings a scan closer to its report; details omitted from the report need not receive equally strong supervision. E1
  • 视觉参数约 6M、22M、86M 仅对应 Tiny、Small、Base 视觉塔。它们的共享空间为 192、384、768 维，不是所有变体都采用 768 维，也不能把视觉参数数目当成双塔总参数。The approximate 6M, 22M and 86M parameter counts refer only to Tiny, Small and Base vision towers. Shared dimensions are 192, 384 and 768. Not every variant is 768-dimensional, and vision-only counts are not two-tower totals. E1
  • 期刊预训练使用 436,787 个 CT—报告对、51,966 名参与者。配对验证集为 46,535 个体数据、5,774 名参与者；额外无报告验证 CT 用于图像侧下游分析。组合验证队列按论文报告为 123,603 个 CT、20,599 名参与者，不把子组人数直接相加。Journal pretraining uses 436,787 CT–report pairs from 51,966 participants. Paired validation contains 46,535 volumes from 5,774 participants; additional unpaired CT is used for image-side downstream analyses. Use the reported combined validation cohort of 123,603 CT volumes and 20,599 participants rather than summing subgroup participant counts. E1
  • AdamW、初始学习率 5×10⁻⁵、每 GPU 批量 48、4 张 NVIDIA B200 对应全局有效批量 192。固定 Base YAML 设定 16 个 epoch、clip 损失且不冻结语言塔；这些是所审阅发布配置，不是自选的最优训练建议。AdamW, initial learning rate 5×10⁻⁵, per-GPU batch size 48 and four NVIDIA B200 GPUs yield a global batch size of 192. The pinned Base YAML sets 16 epochs, clip loss and an unfrozen language tower. These are reviewed release settings, not independently optimized recommendations. E1
  • 本页公式采用标准除数温度 τ 的对称对比目标。期刊文字记 τ=10；固定代码的 CLIP 分支在未启用可学习缩放时把余弦相似度乘 10，若启用则使用 exp(logit_scale)。这两种写法不应被直接说成同一个数值温度。The formula here uses the standard divisor-temperature τ convention. Journal prose states τ=10; the pinned CLIP branch instead multiplies cosine similarity by 10 when learnable scaling is disabled, or uses exp(logit_scale) when enabled. The numerical conventions should not be equated without conversion. E1
  • 本文以 2026 年期刊 Article in Press 与固定 v2 实现解释机制。首次预印本于 2025-07-03 公开，期刊于 2026-09-29 发表；早期 v1 的 DeiT、Clinical Longformer、粗块尺寸和 512 维表示不套用到本页 v2。The explanation follows the 2026 journal Article in Press and pinned v2 implementation. The first preprint appeared on 2025-07-03; the journal article appeared on 2026-09-29. Earlier v1 choices—DeiT, Clinical Longformer, coarse patches and 512-dimensional features—are not attributed to this v2 route. E1
  • 复现应同时固定物理间隔、轴顺序、空间裁剪、分块尺寸、所选双塔和投影维度，并按参与者划分数据。图像嵌入归一化与分类器的特征标准化是不同操作，要按具体下游协议处理。Reproduction should pin physical spacing, axis order, crop, patch size, tower choice and projection dimension, with participant-level splitting. Image-embedding L2 normalization and classifier feature standardization are distinct operations governed by the downstream protocol. E1

医学边界Medical scope

  • 跨模态检索比较 CT 与报告的共享向量；提示词分类比较同一病变的存在与不存在描述。它们使用文本相似度，不等同于已经校准的临床患病概率。Cross-modal retrieval compares CT and report vectors; prompt classification compares present/absent descriptions of one pathology. Both use text similarity rather than an already calibrated clinical disease probability. E1
  • 论文外部 CT-RATE 验证保留每个检查一个 CT，共 1,564 个体数据、18 类胸部标签，并区分零样本提示词分类与在 CT-RATE 训练数据上另拟合的线性探测。External CT-RATE evaluation retains one CT per study, totaling 1,564 volumes and 18 thoracic labels. It distinguishes zero-shot prompts from linear probes separately fitted on CT-RATE training data. E1
  • 疾病分类按胸部、腹部／盆腔分别拟合 L1 逻辑回归，使用影像时间附近的疾病状态；纵向预后则以基线之前无对应诊断者为对象，拟合 ridge Cox 风险模型。二者的队列和目标不同。Disease classification fits L1 logistic regression separately for chest and abdomen/pelvis fields of view, targeting disease status near imaging. Longitudinal prognosis instead excludes prior diagnoses of the target condition and fits ridge Cox models. Their cohorts and objectives differ. E1
  • 实验室指标关联和疾病表型分析使用从训练集拟合的 PCA 坐标，并在验证人群中开展发现与复现。统计显著的坐标关联帮助研究表征，但不意味着某个潜变量就是一个独立生物学机制。Laboratory and disease-phenotype association analyses use PCA axes fitted on training embeddings and discovery–replication analyses in validation participants. Significant associations help characterize representations without identifying each latent coordinate as an independent biological mechanism. E1
  • 期刊中的合成实验室数值来自额外三层 MLP，输入完整图像表征及影像与化验的时间差，用被试分组交叉验证；它不是双塔预训练自动输出的检验报告。Synthetic laboratory values in the paper come from an additional three-layer MLP using complete image features and the imaging-to-assay time difference, evaluated with subject-grouped cross-validation. They are not test reports automatically produced by dual-encoder pretraining. E1
  • PMBB 的病例来源、影像视野和造影方式影响表征及验证范围。代码和已发布模型的可获取性，与原始患者影像、报告及生物库数据的访问条件分别处理。PMBB case mix, field of view and contrast administration affect the learned representation and evaluation scope. Availability of code or released models is distinct from access conditions for original patient CT, reports and biobank data. E1
