01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

普通卷积隐式混合通道，缺少输入自适应的通道选择Standard convolutions fuse channels with fixed learned weights, lacking global content-aware channel selection.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

全局汇聚通道统计后用小型门控网络重标定各通道响应Squeeze global spatial statistics per channel and excite a small gating MLP to dynamically recalibrate channel responses.

设计原理Design principles

  • global squeeze 把每通道的全图响应压缩为上下文描述，随后门控网络学习通道依赖。Core Hypothesis: Squeeze global spatial statistics per channel and excite a small gating MLP to dynamically recalibrate channel responses. E0
  • excitation 是输入自适应的软重标定，可插入多种主干而几乎不改变空间尺寸。Architectural Delta: Models inter-channel dependencies explicitly at negligible parameter and compute overhead; plug-and-play across backbones. E0
  • SE 权重只说明模型的通道增益，不提供病灶空间定位，也不自动构成因果解释。Mathematical Formalism: Channel Squeeze, Excitation, and Adaptive Recalibration. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可用于器官分割、病灶分类和多模态 feature-channel 融合，计算开销低。Clinical Translation: Ubiquitous across medical organ segmentation, lesion classification, and multi-sequence MRI channel fusion. E0
  • 小数据中门控可能追随扫描协议或设备通道偏差；解释时应结合空间证据和外部队列。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Canonical channel-attention module; provides global channel weighting rather than spatial localization. E0
