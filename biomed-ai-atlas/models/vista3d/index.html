原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 自动类别分割与点提示交互共享同一个 3D 编码表示。Core Hypothesis: Unify automated multi-organ 3D segmentation, interactive 3D point prompting, and novel-structure zero-shot transfer in one 3D backbone. E0
  • 类别 embedding 负责已知 anatomy，点 prompt 允许指定或修正实例。Architectural Delta: Dual class/point prompt decoder coupled with 3D supervoxel feature distillation from 2D foundation models. E0
  • supervoxel 与 2D foundation 特征蒸馏把区域先验迁入原生体模型。Mathematical Formalism: Unified Class-Token and 3D Point-Prompt Conditioned Volumetric Decoding. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适合把批量自动分割与临床/标注人员的局部纠错放在同一接口。Clinical Translation: Provides a versatile 3D CT annotation acceleration and interactive correction interface. E0
  • 未见结构能力依赖提示且仍需跨机构前瞻验证，不能等同全自动 zero-shot。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Substantial step forward in 3D promptable segmentation; prospective multi-center workflow validation remains ongoing. E0
