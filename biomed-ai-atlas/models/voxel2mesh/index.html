Voxel2Mesh

Voxel2Mesh 不先分割再 marching cubes，而由 3D 体素特征逐级驱动球形模板的 GCN 形变和细分。Deforms a sphere template mesh progressively into a target 3D anatomical surface via Graph Convolutions conditioned on 3D CNN voxel features sampled at mesh vertices. E0

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 从简单球面模板出发，通过图卷积直接预测表面顶点位移。Core Hypothesis: Sample multi-scale 3D U-Net encoder/decoder features directly at current 3D vertex coordinates and deform the mesh coarse-to-fine via GCN blocks. E0
  • 每一级按顶点坐标从 3D CNN 特征图采样，使体素证据驱动 mesh。Architectural Delta: Learned neighborhood voxel sampling + adaptive edge subdivision (unpooling) + normal/Laplacian/edge-length geometric regularizers. E0
  • 逐级 unpool 增加网格分辨率，并以几何正则保持三角面质量。Mathematical Formalism: Learned Neighborhood Sampling of 3D Voxel Features Driving Progressive GCN Mesh Deformation & Unpooling. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可直接输出心脏、肝脏等器官的显式表面，便于形态测量和物理模拟。Clinical Translation: Produces smooth, genus-zero topological meshes for liver, hippocampus, synaptic junctions, and cardiac chambers. E0
  • 固定球形拓扑难表达孔洞或拓扑变化；边界弱、病灶突出和薄结构可能被平滑正则拉回。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Strong explicit 3D surface baseline; fixed sphere topology cannot model organs with holes or variable branching without template modification. E0
