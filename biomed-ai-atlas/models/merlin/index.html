01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

逐切片 2D VLM 无法充分建模体积解剖和检查级上下文Slice-wise 2D VLMs fail to capture 3D volumetric continuity, while report-only supervision misses structured longitudinal EHR outcomes.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

联合 3D CT、EHR diagnosis codes 与报告学习检查级视觉语言表示Train a native 3D convolutional encoder on 15,331 paired abdominal CTs using both free-text reports (Clinical-Longformer) and 1,692 EHR phenotypes.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

代表腹部 CT 主训练域上的原生 3D VLM 与外部大规模评估Supports zero-shot abnormality classification, cross-modal retrieval, 5-year chronic disease risk prediction, and 3D organ segmentation.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • I3D 把二维预训练卷积核沿 z 轴膨胀，在继承初始化的同时让模型直接学习体积解剖。Core Hypothesis: Train a native 3D convolutional encoder on 15,331 paired abdominal CTs using both free-text reports (Clinical-Longformer) and 1,692 EHR phenotypes. E0
  • EHR codes 提供结构化多标签疾病监督，长报告提供自由文本语义，两者从不同角度约束同一 CT encoder。Architectural Delta: Synergistic dual supervision (radiology prose + structured EHR codes) on native 3D volumes, validated across 44,098 external CTs. E0
  • CT–报告共享嵌入支持零样本与检索，保留的 3D feature hierarchy 又可接生成或分割 decoder。Mathematical Formalism: Joint Multi-Task Supervision: 3D CT–Report Contrastive Loss + EHR Phenotype Binary Cross-Entropy. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • Merlin 代表从逐切片 2D VLM 转向原生 3D 检查级建模，并已覆盖外部 CT 验证与多类适配任务。Clinical Translation: Supports zero-shot abnormality classification, cross-modal retrieval, 5-year chronic disease risk prediction, and 3D organ segmentation. E0
  • 训练主域仍以腹部 CT 和回顾性 EHR 为核心；代码标签偏差、报告生成事实性与前瞻工作流安全需分别验证。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Among the most thoroughly externally validated 3D CT foundation models; evidence remains retrospective. E0
