AUTOMAP

AUTOMAP 的标志是大规模全连接层直接承担 domain transform，而非先做 FBP/IFFT 再用 CNN 后处理。Learns the sensor-to-image domain transform directly via large fully connected layers mapping flattened raw measurements to an image grid followed by sparse convolutional refinement. E0

以全连接域变换层加卷积网络端到端学习测量域到图像域映射Frame tomographic reconstruction as a data-driven manifold mapping between sensor and image domains parameterized by dense fully connected layers.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 不显式编码解析逆变换，而以稠密层学习测量坐标到图像坐标的全局映射。Core Hypothesis: Frame tomographic reconstruction as a data-driven manifold mapping between sensor and image domains parameterized by dense fully connected layers. E0
  • 域变换后用卷积利用自然/医学图像的局部结构进行细化。Architectural Delta: Unified end-to-end neural domain transformation across arbitrary k-space trajectories and projection geometries on small grids. E0
  • 通过端到端配对监督把成像逆问题写成单一可微网络。Mathematical Formalism: Fully Connected Sensor-to-Image Domain Transform Manifold Learning. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 历史上证明 MRI、CT 等测量域到图像域可直接学习，推动 learned reconstruction。Clinical Translation: Historically provocative demonstration of learned sensor-to-image inversion in low-resolution MRI and PET. E0
  • 全连接层参数随测量和图像尺寸急剧增长，且扫描几何改变后难复用；没有显式数据一致性保证。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Historical curiosity rather than a scalable baseline: O(N⁴) fully connected parameters cannot scale to clinical 512²/3D grids and discard known Fourier/Radon physics. E0
