原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • GSC 在 Mamba 展平前显式编码局部三维空间关系。Core Hypothesis: Apply gated 3D spatial convolutions followed by tri-directional Mamba scans across forward, reverse, and inter-slice axes. E0
  • Tri-oriented Mamba 分别沿深度、高度和宽度传播长程信息。Architectural Delta: TSMamba block (GSC + Tri-oriented Mamba) paired with uncertainty-weighted multi-scale skip refinement. E0
  • FUE 对多尺度 skip 做不确定性筛选，避免无条件复用所有 encoder 特征。Mathematical Formalism: Tri-Oriented 3D Volumetric Selective Scan (Depth / Height / Width). E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适合 whole-volume 长关系与多尺度分割并重的 3D 研究。Clinical Translation: Representative peer-reviewed 3D medical Mamba architecture for brain tumor and colorectal segmentation. E0
  • 三轴 scan、FUE 和卷积 stem 都是性能来源，消融不能只比较 Mamba 有无。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Worth reproducing as a 3D SSM reference; still requires broader multi-center and boundary-level validation. E0
