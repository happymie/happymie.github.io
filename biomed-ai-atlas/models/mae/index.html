01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

对比学习依赖强增强和大 batch，ViT 预训练计算昂贵Contrastive SSL depended heavily on hand-crafted view augmentations, while full-token ViT pretraining was computationally expensive.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

高比例遮挡图像 patch，仅让重型 encoder 处理可见 token，再轻量重建Exploit high spatial redundancy in images by dropping 75% of patches before the encoder and reconstructing raw normalized pixels.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

成为 2D、3D MRI/CT 与病理预训练的基础方法Workhorse self-supervised pretraining method for 3D CT/MRI volumes, gigapixel pathology, retinal OCT, and biosignals.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 自然图像存在高空间冗余，因此高遮蔽率迫使 encoder 学习对象结构而非局部复制。Core Hypothesis: Exploit high spatial redundancy in images by dropping 75% of patches before the encoder and reconstructing raw normalized pixels. E0
  • 遮蔽 token 不进入 encoder，计算集中在可见内容，形成重 encoder、轻 decoder 的非对称设计。Architectural Delta: Asymmetric encoder–decoder design (encoder processes only 25% visible tokens → >3× compute reduction) + high masking ratio. E0
  • 重建损失只监督不可见 patch，避免模型把能力浪费在已知像素的恒等映射。Mathematical Formalism: Masked Patch Pixel Mean-Squared Error Computed Strictly on Masked Tokens. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 在 CT/MR 中可扩展为 3D block 遮蔽，但遮蔽尺度必须小心保留微小病灶与薄层结构。Clinical Translation: Workhorse self-supervised pretraining method for 3D CT/MRI volumes, gigapixel pathology, retinal OCT, and biosignals. E0
  • 像素重建容易偏向低频解剖；加入器官感知、频域或多模态目标可提高临床语义密度。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Core SSL infrastructure; pixel-level MSE emphasizes fine texture and intensity, shining brightest under downstream fine-tuning. E0
