BME-X

BME-X 不是通用 MRI encoder，而是先预测组织标签、再用组织感知 DU-Net 生成增强图像的两阶段恢复模型。Two-stage brain MRI enhancement foundation model that first estimates tissue probability maps and then conditions a Dual U-Net (DU-Net) to restore harmonized images. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

MRI 增强模型通常按 degradation 或扫描仪分别训练，且增强质量与下游价值脱节MRI restoration networks were typically trained per degradation or scanner, often smoothing tissue boundaries needed by downstream tools.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

先预测组织标签，再用 tissue-aware enhancement network 统一结构脑 MRI 的运动校正、超分、去噪和扫描仪协调化Pre-estimate anatomical tissue segmentation probabilities to explicitly guide a tissue-aware enhancement network across diverse degradations.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

面向结构脑 MRI 的多任务图像增强，而非多器官通用编码器Serves as a universal structural brain MRI pre-processing and harmonization front-end improving downstream FreeSurfer/segmentation consistency.

设计原理Design principles

  • 先把复杂退化问题转成较稳定的组织分类，再以组织图约束增强。Core Hypothesis: Pre-estimate anatomical tissue segmentation probabilities to explicitly guide a tissue-aware enhancement network across diverse degradations. E0
  • 低质量图像与软组织标签共同输入第二个 DU-Net，避免只追求纹理锐化。Architectural Delta: Couples anatomical tissue priors with unified motion correction, super-resolution, denoising, and multi-site scanner harmonization. E0
  • 一个预训练模型统一运动校正、超分、去噪、harmonization 与 3T→7T-like 增强。Mathematical Formalism: Tissue-Probability-Conditioned Dual-Domain Brain MRI Restoration & Harmonization. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 增强价值用组织分割、配准、诊断和组织体积偏差共同验证，而不只看 PSNR。Clinical Translation: Serves as a universal structural brain MRI pre-processing and harmonization front-end improving downstream FreeSurfer/segmentation consistency. E0
  • 模型专用于结构脑 MRI；组织先验错误可能把异常解剖拉向常见形态，病灶保真需单独检查。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Representative restoration-oriented MRI foundation model; must be audited to ensure subtle focal lesions are not harmonized away. E0
