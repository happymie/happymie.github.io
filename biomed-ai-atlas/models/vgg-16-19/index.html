01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

早期 CNN 使用较大卷积核且结构异质，深度扩展规律不清Early CNNs relied on heterogeneous large kernels (11×11, 7×7) with unclear scaling rules for network depth.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

用连续 3×3 卷积和周期性池化把网络规律地加深到 16/19 层Replace large kernels with homogeneous stacks of 3×3 convolutions interleaved with 2×2 max-pooling up to 16–19 weight layers.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

长期作为医学影像迁移学习、感知损失和编码器基线Widely used for early medical transfer learning, perceptual feature losses, and 2D encoder initialization.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 连续堆叠 3×3 卷积，以多个非线性和较少参数获得等效大感受野。Core Hypothesis: Replace large kernels with homogeneous stacks of 3×3 convolutions interleaved with 2×2 max-pooling up to 16–19 weight layers. E0
  • 每个 stage 通过 max-pool 降采样并增加通道，形成规则层级特征。Architectural Delta: Stacked 3×3 filters capture equivalent large receptive fields with fewer parameters and deeper non-linearities. E0
  • 网络是 plain feed-forward CNN，无 residual shortcuts；超深优化和全连接参数量因此受限。Mathematical Formalism: Stacked 3×3 Kernels Emulating Large Effective Receptive Fields. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可作为迁移学习、感知损失和传统医学 2D encoder 的历史参照。Clinical Translation: Widely used for early medical transfer learning, perceptual feature losses, and 2D encoder initialization. E0
  • 参数量与显存效率弱于现代残差/轻量主干，且 ImageNet 纹理偏置不能替代医学域预训练。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Historical reference and perceptual loss extractor; heavy FC parameters and lack of residual paths limit modern primary use. E0
