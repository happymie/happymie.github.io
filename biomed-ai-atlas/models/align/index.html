ALIGN

ALIGN 的主要贡献不是新交互模块，而是简单 EfficientNet/BERT 双塔在十亿级未精洗图文对上的规模化训练。Demonstrates that a simple EfficientNet + BERT dual encoder trained on over one billion noisy web image–alt-text pairs learns robust cross-modal representations. E0

  • 对称批内对比损失同时训练图搜文与文搜图。Mathematical Formalism: Billion-Scale Noisy Image–Alt-Text Bidirectional Contrastive Learning. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可迁移为影像—报告双塔，用于零样本标签提示和跨模态检索。Clinical Translation: Methodologically inspired harvesting large-scale noisy hospital PACS archives and scientific figure-caption corpora. E0
  • 医学数据规模远小于网页语料，且同义报告会形成假负样本，需要语义软标签或患者级采样修正。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Web-scale VLM milestone; in clinical medicine, uncurated label/report noise and privacy leakage require far stricter governance. E0
