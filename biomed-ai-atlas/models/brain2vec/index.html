Brain2Vec

用三维遮蔽自编码学习 T1 脑 MRI 表征。预训练重建被遮蔽体块，应用时提取 768 维全脑嵌入，或以编码器初始化下游模型。A 3D masked autoencoder learns T1 brain MRI representations. Masked-volume reconstruction trains an encoder that supplies 768-dimensional embeddings or initializes downstream models. E1

结构总览ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW

Brain2Vec，从结构到机制。Brain2Vec: structure to mechanism.

输入INPUT

经去颅骨、线性配准与强度标准化的单通道 T1 脑 MRI，采样为 128×128×128 体素。作者预处理与模型读取器共同定义输入空间。A single-channel T1 brain MRI after skull stripping, linear registration and intensity normalization, resampled to 128×128×128 voxels. Preprocessing and the loader define the input space.

→
输出OUTPUT

预训练输出体块重建与遮蔽损失；表征模式输出 768 维 CLS 嵌入。分类、脑龄回归需要各自训练的下游头；解码器另用于潜变量操纵研究。Pretraining produces patch reconstructions and masked loss; representation mode yields a 768-dimensional CLS embedding. Classification and brain-age regression use separately trained heads. Decoder experiments probe latent changes.

关键设计DESIGN DECISION

用稀疏可见体块训练重型编码器，以完整序列训练较轻解码器；下游表征关闭遮蔽。Train a heavy encoder on visible patches and a lighter decoder on the complete sequence; disable masking for embeddings.

  1. 01

    对齐三维输入Align the 3D input

    先去颅骨并配准到共同模板，再缩放与标准化。解剖位置、体素网格和强度空间决定后续表征的可比性。Skull stripping and atlas registration precede resizing and normalization, aligning anatomy, voxel grids and intensity space. E1

  2. 02

    划分三维体块Embed 3D patches

    8×8×8 非重叠体块将 128³ 体积化为 4096 个位置。作者用核与步长均为 8 的 Conv3d 投影到 768 维，并加入三维位置编码。An 8³ patch grid creates 4096 positions. A stride-8 Conv3d projects patches to 768 dimensions with 3D positional encoding. E1

  3. 03

    随机遮蔽 75%Mask 75% at random

    预训练仅保留 1024 个可见体块，加入 CLS 标记后送入编码器。原始顺序索引用于解码阶段恢复位置。Pretraining retains 1024 visible patches plus CLS. Stored indices restore original positions during decoding. E1

  4. 04

    编码可见上下文Encode visible context

    基础模型使用 12 层、12 头 ViT。自注意力联合建模可见体块，编码器不接收被遮蔽位置的占位标记。The base model uses twelve ViT layers and twelve heads, attending over visible patches without masked-position placeholders. E1

  5. 05

    恢复完整位置Restore the full grid

    将编码结果投影到 512 维，补入共享可学习遮蔽标记，按 ids_restore 还原 4096 个体块位置并加入解码位置编码。Project to width 512, insert learned mask tokens and use ids_restore to recover all 4096 positions. E1

  6. 06

    重建体块体素Reconstruct patch voxels

    8 层、16 头解码器处理完整体块序列；线性预测头为每块输出 512 个体素值。目标是标准化强度空间中的重建。An eight-layer, sixteen-head decoder and linear head predict 512 normalized voxel values per patch. E1

  7. 07

    只在遮蔽处计分Score masked patches only

    按体块求体素均方误差，再只对遮蔽位置求平均。可见体块提供上下文，却不进入这一遮蔽重建损失的分母。Average voxel MSE per patch, then average only masked positions. Visible patches provide context without entering that denominator. E1

  8. 08

    完整输入提取嵌入Extract a full-input embedding

    表征推理关闭遮蔽。作者 inference.py 执行 forward_encoder(mask_ratio=0.0)，再读取 features[:,0,:]，得到基础模型的 768 维 CLS 表征。Inference disables masking and selects features[:,0,:] from forward_encoder: the base model’s 768-dimensional CLS representation. E1

  9. 09

    选择下游学习方式Choose a transfer route

    冻结编码器后训练 SVM／SVR，或用预训练参数初始化 ViT 再微调。两条路线的训练对象、输出与评价协议分别记录。Train SVM/SVR models on frozen embeddings, or initialize and fine-tune a ViT. These routes have separate objectives and evaluation protocols. E1

  10. 10

    检查解剖解释Examine anatomical interpretation

    论文结合注意力、区域体积相关性与潜变量操纵。解码后的区域变化可帮助研究表征；相关性、注意力与预测因果机制需分别理解。The paper combines attention, regional correlations and latent manipulation. Reconstructed changes aid interpretation while these forms of evidence remain distinct. E1

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

标注有限的三维 MRI 任务难以逐个从头训练，预设区域体积又只覆盖特定解剖描述。Limited labels impede task-by-task 3D MRI training; predefined regional volumes cover selected anatomical descriptions.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

从遮蔽体块重建学习三维结构，再把编码器迁移到脑龄与分类任务。Learn 3D structure by reconstructing masked patches, then transfer the encoder to brain age and classification.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

统一预处理后的 T1 脑 MRI，支持冻结嵌入和编码器微调两条路线。Standardized T1 brain MRI supports frozen features and encoder fine-tuning.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 三维体块的 512 个体素先被投影到 768 维；特征宽度与空间体块大小是两个参数。A patch contains 512 voxels before projection to width 768; spatial patch size and feature width are separate parameters. E1
  • 75% 是预训练的体块遮蔽比例。4096 个空间位置保留 1024 个可见体块，再加入 CLS；提取嵌入时使用完整输入。The 75% masking ratio belongs to pretraining: 1024 of 4096 patches remain, plus CLS. Embedding extraction uses the complete input. E1
  • 编码器仅处理可见体块；解码器补齐遮蔽标记并恢复位置。两者的序列长度、宽度和计算责任不同。The encoder sees visible patches; the decoder inserts placeholders and restores positions, with different lengths and widths. E1
  • 三维正弦余弦位置编码保留各轴空间信息；重排后的体块必须按位置索引恢复。Three-dimensional sinusoidal positions retain axis information; shuffled patches must return to their indexed locations. E1
  • 损失先平均每块的体素误差，再对遮蔽块求平均。输入已标准化，重建值属于该强度空间。Voxel error is averaged within each patch and then across masked patches in normalized intensity space. E1
  • 基础模型的编码器为 12 层、768 维；代码也提供 large 与 huge 构造器，本页参数固定在论文基础模型。This page fixes the paper’s base encoder at twelve layers and width 768, distinguishing the additional large and huge code constructors. E1
  • 下游 SVM／SVR 消费冻结嵌入；ViT 微调则更新相应网络参数。冻结路线和微调路线应分别配置训练、验证与测试。Frozen SVM/SVR features and ViT fine-tuning require separate train, validation and test configurations. E1
  • 注意力可显示关注区域，区域相关性连接解剖描述，潜变量操纵考察解码变化；三类分析共同帮助理解表征。Attention, regional correlations and latent manipulation offer complementary views of learned representations. E1
  • 作者讨论了训练数据偏向特定研究和较年长人群的问题。外部队列、扫描协议和年龄覆盖是迁移评估的重要部分。Study composition, older participant coverage and acquisition protocols matter when evaluating transfer to external cohorts. E1

医学边界Medical scope

  • 输入核对：检查去颅骨、配准方向、128³ 网格与强度标准化，再比较同一编码器的嵌入。Input checks: verify skull stripping, registration, the 128³ grid and normalization before comparing embeddings. E1
  • 脑龄回归：区分冻结嵌入加 SVR 与微调 ViT，分别报告年龄覆盖、误差和独立队列表现。Brain age: distinguish frozen-feature SVR from fine-tuned ViT; report age coverage and external-cohort error. E1
  • 分类应用：疾病与风险因素标签各有定义；判别分数、阈值、召回率和精确率一同阅读。Classification: read task definitions, thresholds, recall and precision alongside discrimination scores. E1
  • 研究划分：纵向扫描按参与者分组；预训练、下游训练及外部验证的数据角色分别记录。Study splits: group longitudinal scans by participant and record pretraining, downstream and external-validation roles. E1
  • 解释实验：区域体积与潜变量的相关性可供研究，潜变量改变后的重建不作为患者病程模拟。Interpretation: regional correlations and reconstructed latent changes are research analyses, rather than patient-trajectory simulations. E1
