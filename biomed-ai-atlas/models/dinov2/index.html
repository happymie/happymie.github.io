01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

早期 SSL 的数据治理与大规模训练配方难扩展、难复现Prior self-supervised models degraded on uncurated web noise and often required full end-to-end fine-tuning for dense tasks.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

在清洗的 LVD-142M 上结合 DINO、iBOT 与 KoLeo 训练可复用视觉特征Curate a balanced, deduplicated pretraining corpus via embedding retrieval and train a scalable ViT family with composite self-distillation.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

直接支撑 UNI、Virchow 等病理 foundation encoderForms the direct algorithmic backbone of modern pathology foundation encoders (UNI, Virchow, Prov-GigaPath) and RAD-DINO.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 数据策展是结构的一部分：去重和与目标域相似的重平衡直接决定基础表征覆盖面。Core Hypothesis: Curate a balanced, deduplicated pretraining corpus via embedding retrieval and train a scalable ViT family with composite self-distillation. E0
  • DINO 图像目标、iBOT patch 目标和 KoLeo 分散正则共同约束语义、稠密局部性与特征多样性。Architectural Delta: Automated data curation pipeline + DINO + untied-head iBOT + KoLeo sphere uniformity regularizer + high-resolution short adaptation. E0
  • EMA teacher 与跨尺寸蒸馏把大模型的稳定目标迁移到多种容量的无标签 student。Mathematical Formalism: Composite DINO Image Loss + iBOT Patch MIM Loss + KoLeo Uniformity Regularizer. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • DINOv2 是强通用初始化，但 LVD-142M 并非医学策展集，细粒度病理和三维成像仍存在域差。Clinical Translation: Forms the direct algorithmic backbone of modern pathology foundation encoders (UNI, Virchow, Prov-GigaPath) and RAD-DINO. E0
  • 医学部署应分别验证冻结特征、全量微调和参数高效适配，不能把自然图像零样本性能等同于临床泛化。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Gold-standard frozen visual feature extractor; natural-image weights still benefit from in-domain medical adaptation for 3D/grayscale. E0
