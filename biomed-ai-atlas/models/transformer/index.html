原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • scaled dot-product attention 用当前输入动态生成 token 间聚合权重。Core Hypothesis: Replace recurrence and convolution entirely with parallel multi-head scaled dot-product attention and position encodings. E0
  • 多头机制并行学习不同关系，位置编码补回顺序信息。Architectural Delta: Content-dependent Q/K/V all-pair affinity matrices, multi-head subspace projection, residual LayerNorm, and position-wise FFN. E0
  • encoder–decoder cross-attention 将源表示与自回归目标生成解耦。Mathematical Formalism: Scaled Dot-Product Multi-Head Self-Attention. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 它是 ViT、医学 VLM 和长生理序列模型的共同注意力母体。Clinical Translation: Foundational operator underlying Vision Transformers, medical VLMs, multimodal EHR fusion, and biosignal foundation models. E0
  • 标准全局 attention 的 N×N 矩阵在高分辨率 3D 中通常必须窗口化或降采样。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Universal deep learning infrastructure; direct application to dense 3D voxels is bottlenecked by O(N²) attention complexity. E0
