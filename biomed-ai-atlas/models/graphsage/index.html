GraphSAGE

GraphSAGE 学的是可应用到新节点的聚合器，并用固定邻居采样实现归纳式 mini-batch GNN。Inductive graph representation learning that samples fixed-size K-hop neighborhoods and applies learnable aggregator functions (mean, LSTM, pooling) to generalize to unseen nodes. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

谱 GCN 偏传导式且全邻居计算难扩展到新节点和大图Full-batch transductive GCNs required holding the entire graph Laplacian in memory and could not embed unseen test nodes without retraining.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

通过采样固定数量邻居并聚合其特征生成节点嵌入Learn aggregator functions over uniformly sampled fixed-size k-hop neighborhoods while explicitly concatenating a node's own previous state with its aggregated neighborhood.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

用于患者相似图、细胞图、血管树和大规模病理图Enables scalable inductive training on million-cell whole-slide pathology graphs and dynamic clinical EHR cohorts.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 学习邻居聚合函数，而不是为训练图中的每个节点单独记 embedding。Core Hypothesis: Learn aggregator functions over uniformly sampled fixed-size k-hop neighborhoods while explicitly concatenating a node's own previous state with its aggregated neighborhood. E0
  • 固定 fan-out 采样把大图训练转为可控 mini-batch 计算。Architectural Delta: Mini-batch neighborhood sampling bounding memory footprint + explicit self-feature concatenation preserving node identity against over-smoothing. E0
  • 自身与邻居表示显式拼接，使中心信息与局部上下文分工。Mathematical Formalism: Fixed-Size Neighborhood Sampling & Concatenated Self-vs-Neighbor Inductive Aggregation. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适用于患者相似图、病理细胞图、血管树和大规模生物网络。Clinical Translation: Enables scalable inductive training on million-cell whole-slide pathology graphs and dynamic clinical EHR cohorts. E0
  • 采样会丢失稀有关键邻居，且临床图边的构造误差与群体偏差会被多层传播。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Essential scalable inductive GNN; uniform random neighbor sampling can miss rare diagnostic hub cells if degree distributions are skewed. E0
