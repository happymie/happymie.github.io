01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

标准自注意力对像素数呈二次复杂度，难处理大图Even windowed spatial self-attention restricted receptive fields or incurred heavy memory overhead on megapixel medical scans.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

用通道维 transposed attention 和门控深度卷积前馈网络恢复高分辨率图像Compute cross-covariance attention across feature channels (C×C instead of HW×HW) after applying 3×3 depthwise convolutions for local spatial context.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

适合高分辨率病理、CT/MRI 去噪和运动伪影校正Well-suited for high-resolution digital pathology restoration, whole-slice MRI motion correction, and low-dose CT denoising.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 把注意力矩阵转到通道维，避免随像素数二次增长。Core Hypothesis: Compute cross-covariance attention across feature channels (C×C instead of HW×HW) after applying 3×3 depthwise convolutions for local spatial context. E0
  • Q/K/V 与 FFN 都嵌入 depthwise 卷积，让全局通道交互保留局部空间归纳偏置。Architectural Delta: O(HW · C²) linear spatial scaling via MDTA + element-wise gated depthwise convolutional FFN controlling fine detail propagation. E0
  • 层级 encoder-decoder、同尺度 skip 和原尺度 refinement 协同恢复细节。Mathematical Formalism: Linear-Complexity Channel-Wise Transposed Attention (MDTA) + Gated Depthwise FFN (GDFN). E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 对高分辨率病理、CT/MRI 去噪和运动伪影校正具有较好计算可扩展性。Clinical Translation: Well-suited for high-resolution digital pathology restoration, whole-slice MRI motion correction, and low-dose CT denoising. E0
  • MDTA 是通道统计而非物理数据一致性；定量医学重建仍需显式测量约束或残差验证。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Compute-efficient high-resolution Transformer restorer; must be audited for quantitative intensity preservation. E0
