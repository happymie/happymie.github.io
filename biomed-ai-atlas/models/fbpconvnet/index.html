01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

解析稀疏 CT 重建存在条纹，而直接端到端学习难扩展Analytical FBP suffers from severe streaking under sparse views, while traditional iterative MBIR takes minutes to hours per volume.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

先做解析 FBP，再用 residual U-Net/CNN 去除稀疏视角伪影Exploit the fact that normal-operator streaking artifacts in 2D CT have a translational convolutional structure well matched to a multi-scale residual U-Net.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 先用已知 CT 物理得到可解释的 FBP 初值，再把学习限制为图像域残差校正。Core Hypothesis: Exploit the fact that normal-operator streaking artifacts in 2D CT have a translational convolutional structure well matched to a multi-scale residual U-Net. E0
  • U-Net 的多尺度感受野能识别由稀疏角度造成的全局条纹，同时长跳连保留局部结构。Architectural Delta: Fast analytical FBP inversion + residual U-Net learning the difference between streaked FBP and full-view ground truth. E0
  • 该模型没有展开迭代和反馈式数据一致性；视觉改善不保证 A(x_hat) 与实测投影一致。Mathematical Formalism: Analytical FBP Initialization Followed by Residual Convolutional Artifact Subtraction. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适合作为稀疏视角或低剂量 CT 的快速后处理基线，部署链路短。Clinical Translation: Canonical, fast image-domain post-processing baseline for sparse-view and low-dose CT reconstruction. E0
  • 对训练分布外的金属、截断或新几何，残差网络可能删去病灶或补出结构，必须回投影审计。Evidence & Safety Boundary: High practical speed and historical impact, but enforces zero projection-domain data consistency at inference—risking hallucinated or smoothed subtle lesions. E0
