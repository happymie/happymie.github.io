01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

仅靠加深或加宽网络的计算收益递减且设计空间复杂Scaling depth or width alone yielded diminishing returns and required intricate per-branch hyperparameter tuning.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

在残差块中并行聚合等拓扑分支，以 cardinality 控制表示容量Aggregate C parallel identical-topology transformations inside a residual bottleneck, introducing cardinality C as a third scaling axis.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

常用于 2D/3D 医学分类、检测和分割编码器Frequently adopted in 2D/3D medical classification, nodule detection, and volumetric encoder backbones.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 除深度和宽度外，引入 cardinality 作为表示容量维度。Core Hypothesis: Aggregate C parallel identical-topology transformations inside a residual bottleneck, introducing cardinality C as a third scaling axis. E0
  • 等拓扑分支的 split-transform-merge 可由 grouped 3×3 convolution 紧凑实现。Architectural Delta: Formulates split–transform–merge cleanly as grouped convolution with minimal engineering overhead. E0
  • 残差捷径保持优化稳定，分组卷积控制在相近计算量下增加变换多样性。Mathematical Formalism: Cardinality-Controlled Aggregated Residual Transformations. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可替换 2D/3D 医学分类、检测和分割编码器中的普通 ResNet bottleneck。Clinical Translation: Frequently adopted in 2D/3D medical classification, nodule detection, and volumetric encoder backbones. E0
  • 分组数、每组宽度和 3D 内存需共同调节；架构优势不能替代强数据增强与外部验证。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Grouped/depthwise convolution is now a permanent staple of modern CNNs. E0
