01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

显式 likelihood 生成模型难训练且样本常过度平滑Pixel-wise L1/L2 and maximum-likelihood generative models averaged over uncertain modes, producing blurry textures.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

generator 与 discriminator 进行对抗式分布学习Pit a generator G against an adversarial discriminator D trained to distinguish real samples from synthesized ones.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 判别器估计真实与生成分布的可分性，生成器通过判别梯度隐式学习数据分布而无需显式 likelihood。Core Hypothesis: Pit a generator G against an adversarial discriminator D trained to distinguish real samples from synthesized ones. E0
  • 训练是二人 minimax 博弈；理想均衡处生成分布等于真实分布，判别器无法区分二者。Architectural Delta: Implicit density learning via Jensen-Shannon divergence minimization yielding sharp, high-frequency visual samples. E0
  • 判别器相当于可学习损失，会持续适应当前生成错误，而不是固定的像素距离。Mathematical Formalism: Two-Player Minimax Adversarial Value Function Between Generator G and Discriminator D. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可用于合成影像、增强和跨模态映射，但视觉逼真不代表病灶、剂量或定量值真实。Clinical Translation: Catalyzed medical cross-modality synthesis (MR-to-CT), low-dose perceptual enhancement, and synthetic cohort augmentation. E0
  • mode collapse 和不稳定训练会隐性减少病种多样性；隐私重复与罕见病例覆盖必须审计。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Foundational generative paradigm; training instability, mode collapse, and anatomically unfaithful hallucinations pose severe clinical risks. E0
