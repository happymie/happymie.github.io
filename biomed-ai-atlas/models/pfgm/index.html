PFGM++

PFGM++ 用可调增广维度 D 的 Poisson 电场统一 PFGM 与 diffusion，而不是固定高斯前向噪声过程。Embeds N-dimensional data on the z=0 hyperplane of an N+D augmented space and learns the high-dimensional Poisson electric field lines, unifying PFGM (D=1) and Diffusion (D→∞). E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

原始 PFGM 与 diffusion 的关系和可扩展调节机制不清Diffusion models rely on Gaussian kernels with thin tails, whereas original PFGM (D=1) was difficult to scale to large conditional architectures.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

在增广空间用高维电场/Poisson flow 连接数据分布与简单先验Generalize the augmented electrostatic dimension from D=1 to arbitrary D, proving that as D→∞ and r=σ√D, the Poisson kernel converges to Gaussian diffusion.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

提供医学生成与逆问题的新型 score/flow 先验选择Evaluated on low-dose CT denoising and medical image generation as a more perturbation-robust drop-in alternative to score models.
