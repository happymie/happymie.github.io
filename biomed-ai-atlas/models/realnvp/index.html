01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

一般生成网络缺精确似然，早期 flow 的可扩展性和可逆设计有限GANs and VAEs lacked exact tractable likelihood evaluation, while autoregressive density models were slow to sample.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

用仿射 coupling layers 构造可逆网络并精确计算 log-determinantSplit dimensions in each coupling layer so half remain unchanged while scaling and translating the other half via unconstrained neural networks s(·), t(·).

  • coupling 层只变换一半变量，使逆映射有闭式表达。Core Hypothesis: Split dimensions in each coupling layer so half remain unchanged while scaling and translating the other half via unconstrained neural networks s(·), t(·). E0
  • 三角 Jacobian 将行列式化为缩放项之和，可做精确最大似然。Architectural Delta: Triangular Jacobian with closed-form sum-of-logs determinant + exact single-pass forward density estimation and inverse sampling. E0

  • 可作为医学图像精确密度估计、生成和可逆重建先验的基础。Clinical Translation: Applied to invertible medical image priors, exact-likelihood out-of-distribution detection, and bijective registration. E0
