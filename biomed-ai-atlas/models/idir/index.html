IDIR

IDIR 将位移场本身作为坐标神经场，并在测试图像对上直接优化，而不是由训练好的网络一次预测 flow。Parameterizes the continuous 3D deformation field φ(x) = x + u_θ(x) for a single image pair directly as a coordinate-based Implicit Neural Representation (SIREN MLP) optimized at test time. E0

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 不在离散 voxel 网格直接存位移，而用坐标 MLP 参数化连续场。Core Hypothesis: Represent the displacement vector field u_θ(x) as a periodic-activation coordinate MLP optimized directly on the test pair via stochastic coordinate sampling. E0
  • 每个图像对独立优化网络参数，因此无需训练集或预训练 checkpoint。Architectural Delta: Grid-free continuous parameterization + exact analytical Jacobian/hyperelastic/bending-energy derivatives via automatic differentiation. E0
  • 同一隐式函数可在任意坐标求值，并通过可微插值由图像相似度监督。Mathematical Formalism: Per-Pair Continuous Coordinate MLP Deformation Field Optimization with Analytical Jacobian Regularization. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适合个体化 MRI/CT 配准、连续场分析和需要任意分辨率查询的场景。Clinical Translation: Used for high-precision lung 4D-CT landmark tracking and patient-specific registration without training datasets. E0
  • 逐病例迭代较慢且可能落入局部最优；连续 MLP 本身不保证 diffeomorphism，需显式拓扑正则。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Representative implicit neural registration method; per-case optimization takes seconds to minutes rather than milliseconds. E0
