01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

RNN 难训练，CNN 与 Transformer 难稳定覆盖超长序列RNNs suffer from vanishing gradients and sequential training bottlenecks, while Transformers scale quadratically O(N²) on long sequences.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

用结构化状态矩阵把长程递推等价为可高效计算的长卷积Initialize state matrix A with HiPPO orthogonal polynomial projections and decompose it into diagonal plus low-rank form for fast kernel evaluation.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 结构化状态矩阵把长期记忆、数值稳定和可计算性同时纳入参数化。Core Hypothesis: Initialize state matrix A with HiPPO orthogonal polynomial projections and decompose it into diagonal plus low-rank form for fast kernel evaluation. E0
  • 同一线性系统既可作为长卷积并行训练，也可递推执行。Architectural Delta: Mathematically unifies continuous-time ODEs, O(1)-step autoregressive recurrence, and parallel FFT convolution training. E0
  • 训练通常为次二次/近 O(N log N) 路径，不应与 Mamba 的选择性线性 scan 混为一谈。Mathematical Formalism: S4 Structured State-Space Kernelization via Cauchy/Woodbury Identity. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适合 EEG、ECG 等长生理序列的高效长程建模研究。Clinical Translation: Provides the mathematical foundation for long-horizon EEG, ECG, waveform modeling, and subsequent visual/3D SSMs. E0
  • 把 2D/3D 影像展平给 S4 仍需额外空间扫描设计，原模型不天然理解体拓扑。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Foundational structured SSM; linear time-invariant (LTI) dynamics lack content-dependent selection until Mamba. E0
