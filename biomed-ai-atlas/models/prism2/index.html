PRISM2

PRISM2 明确提供双嵌入：Perceiver 的通用 base embedding，以及 Phi-3 隐状态导出的诊断 embedding。Pairs a Perceiver slide encoder over Virchow2 tile embeddings with a Phi-3 LLM trained on single-turn clinical dialogues derived from pathology reports, exposing dual embeddings. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

两阶段 patch/slide/LLM 管线信息瓶颈多且难统一适配Two-stage MIL + LLM pathology pipelines suffered from rigid task heads and disconnected visual vs. linguistic representations.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

将 Virchow2 tile embeddings 输入 Perceiver slide encoder，再连接 Phi-3 进行临床对话式推理Connect a Perceiver slide aggregator to a clinical LLM (Phi-3) via conversational report supervision, extracting both visual and LLM-contextualized embeddings.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

以临床对话作为监督和病理全切片推理接口，不以部署聊天代理为目标Designed as a promptable whole-slide diagnostic representation and conversational reasoning engine rather than a patient-facing chatbot.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • Virchow2 负责局部 tile，Perceiver 把超长序列压成可迁移 base embedding。Core Hypothesis: Connect a Perceiver slide aggregator to a clinical LLM (Phi-3) via conversational report supervision, extracting both visual and LLM-contextualized embeddings. E0
  • Stage 1 让同一 slide 表示同时满足 BioGPT 对比对齐与 Phi-3 自回归生成。Architectural Delta: Exposes dual reusable interfaces (Perceiver base embedding + LLM last-token diagnostic embedding) alongside promptable zero-shot inference. E0
  • Stage 2 冻结 slide encoder，仅用报告改写的单轮临床对话强化 prompt-based inference。Mathematical Formalism: Dual-Interface Perceiver Base Slide Embedding & Phi-3 Diagnostic Hidden-State Embedding. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • base embedding 更适合 biomarker、生存等迁移；diagnostic embedding 更偏癌症诊断语义。Clinical Translation: Designed as a promptable whole-slide diagnostic representation and conversational reasoning engine rather than a patient-facing chatbot. E0
  • 临床对话是丰富监督形式，不表示模型被设计为多轮部署聊天代理；生成输出仍可能错误。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Emerging 2025/2026 frontier preprint; requires independent multi-center replication and pathologist workflow validation. E0
