DDNM

DDNM 用 A†y 固定 range-space 信息，只让 diffusion denoiser 生成 null-space 分量。Decomposes reverse diffusion intermediate estimates x̂_{0|t} via the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse A† into a fixed measurement range-space component A†y and a generated null-space component (I − A†A)x̂_{0|t}. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

通用扩散先验直接采样不满足观测，任务专用训练成本高Unconditional diffusion samples violate measurement constraints, whereas backpropagating likelihood gradients through U-Nets (DPS) is memory-heavy.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

把扩散采样分解为满足线性测量的范围空间部分与可生成的零空间部分Directly replace the range-space component of each step's denoised estimate x̂_{0|t} with A†y, leaving the diffusion model to synthesize only the null-space component.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

可用于 MRI、CT、去模糊和缺失数据的扩散逆问题研究Used in zero-shot MRI reconstruction, sparse CT restoration, super-resolution, and inpainting.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 测量确定的范围空间分量由 A†y 直接替换。Core Hypothesis: Directly replace the range-space component of each step's denoised estimate x̂_{0|t} with A†y, leaving the diffusion model to synthesize only the null-space component. E0
  • 扩散模型只负责填充 (I−A†A) 所描述的零空间自由度。Architectural Delta: Backpropagation-free zero-shot linear inverse solver with a noise-aware extension (DDNM+) handling additive measurement noise. E0
  • 数据一致校正在每个逆扩散步插入，因此不需为每个退化算子重新训练网络。Mathematical Formalism: Range–Null Space Decomposition: x̄_{0|t} = A†y + (I − A†A)x̂_{0|t}. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可研究 MRI 欠采样、CT 缺失投影和去模糊等线性逆问题的零样本扩散先验。Clinical Translation: Used in zero-shot MRI reconstruction, sparse CT restoration, super-resolution, and inpainting. E0
  • 医学算子往往含噪、非线性或无精确伪逆；预训练自然图像先验也可能在 null space 生成错误解剖。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Clean mathematical decomposition of range vs. null space; highlights directly that null-space anatomy is synthesized purely by the prior. E0
