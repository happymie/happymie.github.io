MedNeRF

MedNeRF 把 NeRF 置于 GAN 式跨病例生成先验中，以 shape/appearance latent 从单 X-ray 合成视角，而非逐场景多视图优化。Adapts GIRAFFE/GRAF 3D-aware generative radiance fields to X-ray projections, disentangling 3D shape and appearance latents to synthesize continuous-angle CT projections from a single X-ray. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

单视图到 3D 高度欠定，原始 NeRF 依赖多视图且建模可见光Single-view X-ray to 3D reconstruction lacked multi-view supervision, while 2D GANs could not enforce 3D viewpoint consistency.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

以 GAN 条件先验和 NeRF 式场表示从单张 X-ray 生成视角可控的 CT 投影Train a 3D-aware conditional generative radiance field on 2D X-ray projections with a multi-scale PatchGAN discriminator, then invert latent codes (z_s, z_a) from a single test X-ray.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

直接任务是单 X-ray 的 3D-aware projection synthesis；数据增强与表示学习属于潜在用途Explored for single-view chest and knee X-ray 3D-aware view synthesis and data augmentation.
