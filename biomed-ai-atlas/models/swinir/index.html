01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

CNN 恢复网络局部感受野限制长程依赖，全局 attention 成本过高Pure CNN restoration networks had content-independent local kernels, while naive ViTs lost fine pixel boundaries and translation equivariance.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

以浅层卷积、深层残差 Swin blocks 和重建 head 统一多类图像恢复Combine local shifted-window self-attention (for content-adaptive long-range self-similarity) with residual convolutional layers for translational stability.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

常用于低剂量 CT、MRI、显微图像去噪与超分辨率迁移Widely adopted backbone for MRI super-resolution, low-dose CT/PET denoising, and microscopy image restoration.

设计原理Design principles

  • 浅层卷积保留低频基底，深层 Transformer 只需学习复杂残差。Core Hypothesis: Combine local shifted-window self-attention (for content-adaptive long-range self-similarity) with residual convolutional layers for translational stability. E0
  • shifted-window attention 以局部窗口成本获得跨窗口非局部建模。Architectural Delta: Shallow feature CNN + deep RSTB cascade + long global skip connection achieving state-of-the-art PSNR across restoration benchmarks. E0
  • 统一 RSTB 主干，按超分、去噪或去伪影切换重建 head。Mathematical Formalism: Residual Swin Transformer Block (RSTB) Combining Shifted-Window Attention & Conv Inductive Bias. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适合高分辨率病理、低剂量 CT、MRI 和显微图像恢复迁移。Clinical Translation: Widely adopted backbone for MRI super-resolution, low-dose CT/PET denoising, and microscopy image restoration. E0
  • 自然图像感知指标提升不保证 HU、ADC 或病灶边界保真，需配测量一致性和下游任务评价。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Strong modern restoration backbone; remains an image-space mapping unless embedded inside an unrolled physics data-consistency loop. E0
