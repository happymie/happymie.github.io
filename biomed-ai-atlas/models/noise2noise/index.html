Noise2Noise

使用同一信号的两次独立无偏噪声实现进行 noisy-to-noisy 监督，训练阶段不需要干净靶图。Trains a denoising network using pairs of independent noisy realizations (y_1, y_2) of the same underlying anatomy without ever observing clean ground truth. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

clean target 昂贵或无法采集Acquiring noiseless ground-truth scans (e.g., high-dose CT or long-exposure microscopy) is often clinically unethical or physically impossible.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

用同一潜在图像的两次独立、条件无偏 noisy observations 进行监督Exploit the statistical property that replacing a clean L2 regression target x with an independent zero-mean noisy target y_2 leaves the optimal Bayes estimator unchanged.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

可用于拆分 PET counts、MRI k-space 或可重复低剂量测量Directly applicable to splitting list-mode PET counts, multi-NEX MRI averages, dose-split CT projections, and cryo-EM / fluorescence microscopy.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 在平方损失下，只要目标噪声条件无偏，noisy target 与 clean target 具有相同的总体最优预测。Core Hypothesis: Exploit the statistical property that replacing a clean L2 regression target x with an independent zero-mean noisy target y_2 leaves the optimal Bayes estimator unchanged. E0
  • 两个观测必须对应同一潜在信号；独立性是去除目标噪声而不学习它的关键。Architectural Delta: Eliminates the need for clean training targets whenever two independent noisy measurements of static anatomy can be acquired or split. E0
  • Noise2Noise 改变监督数据契约，而不是规定某一种网络主干。Mathematical Formalism: Equivalence of Expected L2 Risk Under Independent Zero-Mean Noisy Targets E[y_2|x] = x. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可从 PET list-mode 计数拆分、MRI 重复采样或多次低剂量采集构造训练对。Clinical Translation: Directly applicable to splitting list-mode PET counts, multi-NEX MRI averages, dose-split CT projections, and cryo-EM / fluorescence microscopy. E0
  • 患者运动、生理变化、造影剂/摄取变化会破坏同一潜在 x 假设，导致解剖或病灶偏差。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Enduring mathematical principle; breaks down if inter-scan patient motion occurs or if noise realizations share correlated bias. E0
