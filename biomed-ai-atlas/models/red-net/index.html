RED-Net

相较普通 autoencoder，RED-Net 在每个镜像卷积—反卷积层之间建立对称加法 skip，而不只保留少数 U-Net 尺度跳连。Deep symmetric convolutional–deconvolutional autoencoder connected by layer-by-layer additive residual skip connections at every mirrored depth. E0

设计原理Design principles

  • encoder 用深卷积获取大感受野，decoder 用反卷积恢复图像。Core Hypothesis: Pair every convolutional encoding layer with a mirrored deconvolutional decoding layer linked by an element-wise additive residual skip. E0
  • 同尺度对称 skip 以加法传递高频细节并缩短反向传播路径。Architectural Delta: Preserves full spatial resolution options and shortens backpropagation paths across 10–30+ layer restoration autoencoders. E0
  • 网络直接学习退化到 clean 的端到端映射，可按任务调整深度与输出残差。Mathematical Formalism: Symmetric Mirrored Convolution–Deconvolution Autoencoder with Dense Additive Skips. E0

  • 为低剂量 CT、MRI 去噪与残差式重建提供经典深层 encoder-decoder 模板。Clinical Translation: Popularized residual encoder–decoder (RED-CNN) architectures as a primary baseline for low-dose CT image-domain denoising. E0
