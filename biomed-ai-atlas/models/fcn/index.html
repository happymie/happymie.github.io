01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

分类网络只能给图级标签，逐 patch 推理低效且边界粗Classification CNNs produced only image-level labels; sliding-window patch classification was redundant and boundary-coarse.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

将分类 CNN 的全连接层卷积化，端到端输出像素级预测Transform fully connected layers into convolutions and append transposed-convolution upsampling for end-to-end pixel-wise training.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

是 U-Net 等医学 encoder-decoder 分割网络的直接祖先Direct progenitor of U-Net and all modern medical encoder–decoder segmentation networks.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 把分类网络完全卷积化后，可一次前向计算整图 dense prediction。Core Hypothesis: Transform fully connected layers into convolutions and append transposed-convolution upsampling for end-to-end pixel-wise training. E0
  • FCN-16s/8s 用浅层 skip score 补偿深层下采样造成的定位损失。Architectural Delta: Learned deconvolution combined with multi-scale coarse-to-fine skip score fusion (FCN-32s → FCN-16s → FCN-8s). E0
  • learned deconvolution 学习上采样核，但浅深特征只做分数相加，表达仍较粗。Mathematical Formalism: FCN-8s Multi-Stride Score Map Skip Fusion. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 它是 U-Net 等医学 encoder–decoder 的直接概念祖先。Clinical Translation: Direct progenitor of U-Net and all modern medical encoder–decoder segmentation networks. E0
  • 对精细边界和小结构，FCN 的稀疏 skip 与粗上采样通常不如对称解码器。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Foundational dense-prediction paradigm; asymmetric score-only upsampling is superseded by symmetric U-Net decoders. E0
