01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

高质量 diffusion 需要许多 solver stepsEven accelerated ODE/DDIM samplers required 10–50 sequential neural evaluations, limiting real-time intraoperative or streaming use.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

把同一 probability-flow ODE 轨迹上的任意点直接映射到相同 clean endpointEnforce that any two adjacent points on the same PF-ODE trajectory map to the exact same clean endpoint under an EMA target network.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

对实时医学重建和交互生成有直接吸引力Highly attractive for real-time medical reconstruction, interactive synthesis, and low-latency clinical workflows.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 同一 probability-flow ODE 轨迹上的所有状态应映射到同一个干净端点。Core Hypothesis: Enforce that any two adjacent points on the same PF-ODE trajectory map to the exact same clean endpoint under an EMA target network. E0
  • 可由 score-based diffusion 蒸馏，也可通过 consistency training 独立学习；两种训练证据需区分。Architectural Delta: Supports single-step generation (1 NFE) as well as zero-shot multi-step iterative refinement trading compute for quality. E0
  • 推理跳过逐步 ODE/SDE 数值积分，一步速度来自端点映射而非更粗的普通 diffusion solver。Mathematical Formalism: Trajectory Self-Consistency f_θ(x_{t_{n+1}}, t_{n+1}) = f_{θ^-}(x_{t_n}, t_n) with Boundary Condition f_θ(x_ε, ε) = x_ε. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适合对延迟敏感的交互重建、床旁生成或大体积推理探索。Clinical Translation: Highly attractive for real-time medical reconstruction, interactive synthesis, and low-latency clinical workflows. E0
  • 医学 raw-data 条件化、少步病灶保真和后验校准证据仍少，不能从自然图像速度结果外推临床等效。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Important speed frontier; raw-data medical inverse benchmarks and quantitative bias audits at 1–2 NFEs remain limited. E0
