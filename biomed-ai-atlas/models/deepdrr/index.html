DeepDRR

DeepDRR 不是纯 GAN 渲染器，而是 3D 材料分解网络、解析材料/能谱 ray tracing、学习散射和解析噪声的组合 forward model。Physics-based X-ray fluoroscopy simulator combining CNN 3D tissue material decomposition, analytical energy-dependent Beer–Lambert ray-casting, learned Rayleigh/Compton scatter, and quantum noise. E0

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 材料分解由深网络完成，但 primary attenuation 仍由解析射线追踪计算。Core Hypothesis: Decompose CT voxels into air/soft-tissue/bone/metal via a 3D CNN, integrate analytical mass attenuation across energy spectra, and add CNN-estimated scatter + Poisson noise. E0
  • 用神经网络近似昂贵 Monte Carlo 的低频散射成分。Architectural Delta: Hybrid physics + deep learning forward model that allows models trained purely on synthetic CT projections to transfer zero-shot to real clinical C-arm X-rays. E0
  • 最后按曝光物理注入噪声，使合成 X-ray 不只是无噪投影。Mathematical Formalism: Polychromatic Beer–Lambert Material Attenuation Integral + Learned Scatter & Quantum/Electronic Noise. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可批量生成具有已知 3D 姿态和器械标签的 fluoroscopy，用于 2D/3D 配准与术中模型训练。Clinical Translation: Foundational simulation engine for training intraoperative 2D/3D registration, surgical tool tracking, and pelvic/spinal landmark detection. E0
  • 材料网络、能谱、散射和噪声任一失配都会造成 synthetic-to-real gap，必须用真实设备逐协议验证。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Core medical projective simulation infrastructure; fidelity still depends on CT slice thickness and accurate C-arm spectrum modeling. E0
