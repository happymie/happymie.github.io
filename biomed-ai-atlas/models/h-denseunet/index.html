01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

纯 2D 缺跨切片信息，纯 3D 在高分辨率 CT 上显存和数据需求过高Pure 2D networks miss z-axis continuity, whereas pure 3D networks cannot fit deep high-resolution encoders in GPU memory.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

先以 2D DenseUNet 提取丰富切片内特征，再用 3D DenseUNet 融合体上下文Cascade a deep 2D DenseUNet for high-resolution in-plane representation with a 3D DenseUNet for volumetric context.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 2D DenseUNet 以较低显存学习高分辨率切片纹理，3D DenseUNet 补充跨切片解剖连续性。Core Hypothesis: Cascade a deep 2D DenseUNet for high-resolution in-plane representation with a 3D DenseUNet for volumetric context. E0
  • 2D-to-3D 重排与 hybrid fusion 让两种上下文在统一体坐标中联合使用。Architectural Delta: End-to-end joint Hybrid Feature Fusion (HFF) reconciling 2D transferability with 3D volumetric continuity. E0
  • dense connections 强化特征复用和梯度流，但会增加 activation memory，尤其在 3D 分支。Mathematical Formalism: Hybrid 2D Intra-Slice and 3D Inter-Slice Dense Feature Fusion. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 针对肝脏与肿瘤 CT 分割设计，是高分辨体数据中平衡 2D 细节和 3D 上下文的代表。Clinical Translation: Landmark hybrid 2.5D/3D architecture for volumetric CT liver and tumor segmentation. E0
  • 对非轴向各向异性、其他器官和现代 patch-based 3D 配置的优势需重新验证，不能直接外推。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Historically strong hybrid design; modern patch-based 3D nnU-Net and MedNeXt pipelines offer simpler reproducibility. E0
