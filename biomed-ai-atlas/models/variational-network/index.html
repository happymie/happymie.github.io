01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

手工 regularizer 和 potential 表达能力有限Compressed-sensing MRI with fixed wavelet/TV regularizers reconstructed slowly and introduced staircasing or over-smoothing.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

展开变分梯度下降，学习滤波器、势函数并显式使用多线圈 forward modelUnfold K=10 gradient descent steps of a variational energy combining learned convolutional filter-bank potentials with exact multi-coil k-space data consistency.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

在 accelerated MRI 中已有临床工作流速度和质量证据First unrolled MRI reconstruction architecture validated in clinical reader studies to achieve 4× accelerated knee MRI acquisition.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 从变分目标的梯度下降推导每一级，而不是把重建当作无结构的图像到图像回归。Core Hypothesis: Unfold K=10 gradient descent steps of a variational energy combining learned convolutional filter-bank potentials with exact multi-coil k-space data consistency. E0
  • 多线圈 forward model PFS 与伴随 S*F*P* 保证采样残差以正确几何进入更新。Architectural Delta: End-to-end training of stage-specific filter kernels, spline activation derivatives (potentials), and data-consistency step weights. E0
  • 滤波器和势函数由数据学习，替代固定的稀疏先验，同时保持有限级可解释展开。Mathematical Formalism: Unrolled Variational Gradient Descent with Multi-Coil SENSE Forward Operator A and Adjoint A*. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适用于给定采样 mask 和可靠 coil maps 的加速多线圈 MRI，可把重建速度固定在 K 级前向。Clinical Translation: First unrolled MRI reconstruction architecture validated in clinical reader studies to achieve 4× accelerated knee MRI acquisition. E0
  • 序列、场强、厂商或灵敏度估计改变时，学习到的正则与数据项权重可能失配。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Foundational clinical unrolled MRI milestone; shallow Fields-of-Experts regularizers were later deepened into U-Net cascades in E2E-VarNet. E0
