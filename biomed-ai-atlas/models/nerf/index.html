01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

离散 voxel/mesh 分辨率固定，novel-view fidelity 受限Discrete voxel grids scaled cubically in memory, while triangle meshes struggled to optimize complex topology via gradient descent.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

连续坐标 MLP 表示密度和视角相关颜色，并沿射线可微体渲染Encode continuous spatial coordinates with high-frequency sinusoids so a compact MLP memorizes a continuous volumetric density and radiance field.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

启发稀疏投影 attenuation fields、内镜场建模和医学 INRCatalyzed medical Implicit Neural Representations (INRs), sparse-view CBCT attenuation fields (NAF), and surgical endoscopy scene reconstruction.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 用连续坐标 MLP 表示场景，而非固定分辨率 voxel/mesh；任意位置都可查询。Core Hypothesis: Encode continuous spatial coordinates with high-frequency sinusoids so a compact MLP memorizes a continuous volumetric density and radiance field. E0
  • 位置编码提供高频基函数，使 MLP 能拟合细节；方向条件只进入颜色分支。Architectural Delta: Fourier positional encoding overcoming MLP spectral bias + hierarchical coarse-to-fine stratified ray sampling. E0
  • 可微 alpha-compositing 将像素误差反传到整条射线的密度与颜色。Mathematical Formalism: Classical Emission–Absorption Volume Rendering Integral Along Camera Rays. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可启发内镜新视角、医学隐式场和稀疏投影场表示。Clinical Translation: Catalyzed medical Implicit Neural Representations (INRs), sparse-view CBCT attenuation fields (NAF), and surgical endoscopy scene reconstruction. E0
  • 原始 NeRF 的可见光发射—吸收 RGB 模型不是 X-ray 线积分物理，不能直接解释为衰减系数或 HU。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Foundational neural field paradigm; visible-light occlusion/emission rendering must be rewritten into Beer–Lambert transmission for X-ray/CT. E0
