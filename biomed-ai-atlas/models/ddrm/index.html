01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

每种线性退化都需重新训练专用模型Supervised restoration required retraining a separate model whenever the degradation operator, blur kernel, or undersampling mask changed.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

利用线性退化算子 A 的 SVD 构造 diffusion restoration posteriorTransform the reverse diffusion process into the Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) basis of H, conditioning spectral coefficients directly on observed measurements and noise level.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

同一 prior 可研究 MRI、去模糊、超分辨和部分 CT 线性逆问题Applicable to super-resolution, deblurring, inpainting, and compressed sensing MRI where the forward operator admits tractable spectral decomposition.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 在 A 的奇异向量基中，测量约束可按每个 singular direction 与噪声水平解析处理。Core Hypothesis: Transform the reverse diffusion process into the Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) basis of H, conditioning spectral coefficients directly on observed measurements and noise level. E0
  • 无条件 diffusion prior 与退化算子解耦，同一 prior 可用于多种线性恢复任务。Architectural Delta: Zero-shot plug-and-play linear inverse solver requiring only 20 DDIM-style steps and zero task-specific retraining. E0
  • T 个逆扩散步骤构成恢复迭代，但不是学习优化器式的 stage-by-stage unrolling。Mathematical Formalism: Spectral-Space Conditional Reverse Diffusion Along Singular Vectors of Operator H. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适合具有可计算 SVD 或快速谱分解的线性 MRI、去模糊和超分辨研究。Clinical Translation: Applicable to super-resolution, deblurring, inpainting, and compressed sensing MRI where the forward operator admits tractable spectral decomposition. E0
  • 大规模 CT 显式 SVD 通常不可行，非线性/Poisson 成像也不满足其解析条件化假设。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Important zero-shot inverse solver milestone; computing explicit full SVD is intractable for large 3D cone-beam CT or non-linear forward operators. E0
