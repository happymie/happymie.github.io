Vox2Cortex

Vox2Cortex 将 3D U-Net 特征在顶点处采样，并以 GCN 同时形变四个互相关联的皮层表面。Jointly deforms four topologically coupled brain cortical hemisphere templates (left/right white and pial surfaces) in seconds using 3D U-Net voxel features and Residual GCNs. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

传统皮层重建流程耗时且多阶段，通用 mesh 模型难满足嵌套皮层拓扑Classical FreeSurfer cortical surface reconstruction required 4–8 hours of CPU optimization per scan, while voxel segmenters failed to prevent self-intersections in tight sulci.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

由 3D MRI 体素特征驱动多表面 mesh deformation，同时预测皮层内外表面Drive 4 coupled template meshes simultaneously through multiple Euler-like deformation steps conditioned on multi-scale 3D CNN features sampled at vertex coordinates.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 用 3D U-Net 提供体素域多尺度证据，用 GCN 维护显式皮层 mesh 拓扑。Core Hypothesis: Drive 4 coupled template meshes simultaneously through multiple Euler-like deformation steps conditioned on multi-scale 3D CNN features sampled at vertex coordinates. E0
  • 同时形变左右半球的 white 与 pial 表面，而非独立逐表面重建。Architectural Delta: Inter- and intra-surface geometric constraints preventing white/pial self-intersection + 1,000× speedup (<20 seconds vs. hours in FreeSurfer). E0
  • 粗到细顶点更新和表面几何正则使输出可直接用于皮层测量。Mathematical Formalism: Coupled Multi-Surface Cortical Mesh Deformation via Continuous ODE-like GCN Steps & Curvature Regularization. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 面向脑 MRI white/pial surface extraction，可直接计算皮层厚度和表面形态。Clinical Translation: High-speed neuroimaging baseline for cortical thickness analysis, Alzheimer's atrophy tracking, and large biobank processing. E0
  • 模板拓扑和训练人群先验可能限制异常皮层、术后缺损或严重畸形；需检查自交、拓扑和亚毫米偏差。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Domain-specialized explicit surface landmark; severe brain tumors or strokes that disrupt normal cortical topology remain challenging. E0
