01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

纯卷积的显式全局关系有限Pure 3D CNN encoders have restricted explicit global receptive fields across large volumetric scans.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

以 3D patch token 的 ViT encoder 接 U 形 CNN decoderTokenize 3D volumes into non-overlapping 3D patches processed by a ViT encoder, projecting intermediate layers into a 3D CNN decoder.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

把全局 Transformer encoder 系统引入 3D 医学分割Pioneered global Transformer encoders for 3D multi-organ CT/MRI segmentation.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • ViT encoder 让远隔体 patch 可通过 self-attention 直接交换信息。Core Hypothesis: Tokenize 3D volumes into non-overlapping 3D patches processed by a ViT encoder, projecting intermediate layers into a 3D CNN decoder. E0
  • 不同 Transformer 深度充当 U-Net 式 skip 层级，而非只用最终 CLS 表示。Architectural Delta: Multi-depth reshape-and-deconvolution skip pathways bridging sequence-level ViT tokens back to spatial voxel grids. E0
  • CNN decoder 重新注入局部上采样和高分辨率定位偏置。Mathematical Formalism: Multi-Depth 3D ViT Token Projection into Convolutional Decoder Stages. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适合需要远程器官关系的 3D 分割研究，但通常依赖足够数据或预训练。Clinical Translation: Pioneered global Transformer encoders for 3D multi-organ CT/MRI segmentation. E0
  • patch tokenization 与全局 O(N²) attention 会压缩小病灶并限制可用体素分辨率。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Important hybrid milestone, though flat 3D patch attention incurs high quadratic compute and data hunger from scratch. E0
