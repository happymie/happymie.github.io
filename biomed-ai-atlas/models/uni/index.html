01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

ImageNet 或小规模 TCGA encoder 跨组织迁移弱ImageNet-pretrained encoders and small TCGA-only models generalized poorly across diverse tissue types and rare cancers.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

在超过一亿病理 patches 上用 DINOv2 训练 ViT-L 通用 tile encoderScale DINOv2 self-supervised pretraining to >100M non-public diagnostic tissue tiles across 20 major tissue types (100,000+ WSIs).

原论文及后续比较报告较强的病理 label efficiency 和跨任务迁移Widely adopted frozen tile feature extractor for cancer subtyping, biomarker prediction, ROI retrieval, and cell segmentation.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 把病理基础模型训练单元设为组织 tile，以超大规模无标签 WSI 转化为可管理的视觉样本。Core Hypothesis: Scale DINOv2 self-supervised pretraining to >100M non-public diagnostic tissue tiles across 20 major tissue types (100,000+ WSIs). E0
  • DINOv2 式图像级自蒸馏与 patch 遮蔽蒸馏同时学习形态语义和局部组织结构。Architectural Delta: Massive non-TCGA clinical tissue diversity + DINOv2/iBOT recipe evaluated systematically across 34 clinical pathology tasks. E0
  • 预训练 encoder 与任务聚合器解耦，同一 tile 表征可用在线性探针、ROI 和 WSI 级任务。Mathematical Formalism: UNI Joint Tile-Level DINO Self-Distillation & Masked Patch iBOT Distillation. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 强 tile encoder 不自动解决 WSI 级空间推理；聚合方法、取样倍率和 tile 数仍决定病例级性能。Clinical Translation: Widely adopted frozen tile feature extractor for cancer subtyping, biomarker prediction, ROI retrieval, and cell segmentation. E0
  • 染色、扫描仪和组织来源偏移需独立验证，且患者级/切片级划分必须防止近重复泄漏。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Mature pathology tile foundation model; slide-level diagnosis still requires training an MIL or slide aggregator on top. E0
