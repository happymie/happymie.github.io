原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 通过多任务联合训练把器官、模态和形变统计汇总到单一共享注册器，而非每任务单独训练。Core Hypothesis: Unify diverse public 3D registration cohorts (brain, lung, abdomen, knee; CT & MRI) under a single GradICON training objective. E0
  • 保留 GradICON 的双向复合梯度约束，使规模扩展不丢失核心几何正则。Architectural Delta: Demonstrates that a single frozen registration weight set achieves strong out-of-the-box zero-shot accuracy on unseen anatomical domains and fast one-shot adaptation. E0
  • foundation model 在此指跨训练任务 zero-shot/可微调能力，不表示覆盖所有临床域。Mathematical Formalism: Multi-Dataset Composite Similarity & Gradient Inverse Consistency Foundation Training. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可作为多器官、多模态注册的统一初始化或基线，减少每个新任务重新训练的成本。Clinical Translation: First credible, open-weight foundation model for 3D medical deformable image registration. E0
  • 未覆盖的儿科、术后、植入物或极端病理仍可能失效；需按目标域报告 landmark、Jacobian 和病理保持。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Rapidly advancing registration foundation baseline; extreme cross-modality contrasts (e.g., ultrasound-to-MRI) still benefit from domain adaptation. E0
