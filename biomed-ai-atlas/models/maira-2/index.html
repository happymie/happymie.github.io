01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

纯文本报告难追溯到影像证据，模型错误不易审计Un-grounded text reports cannot be visually audited on the image, and single-image inputs ignore longitudinal interval change.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

在报告生成时同时输出与叙述实体对应的图像区域定位Condition the LLM on full longitudinal clinical context (multi-view + prior study/report) while emitting spatial bounding-box tokens inline with findings.

  • 普通报告、grounded 报告和 phrase grounding 进行多任务训练。Mathematical Formalism: Longitudinal Multi-View Conditioned Autoregressive Generation of Interleaved Text & Box Tokens. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 定位框使报告句子能够回指当前正位胸片区域，便于人工核验。Clinical Translation: Sets the standard for auditable, spatially grounded adult chest X-ray report drafting and human-in-the-loop review. E0
  • 框只覆盖可局灶定位的发现，正常解剖、弥漫性改变和错误推理仍不能因此自动验证。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Major step toward verifiable clinical VLMs; accurate box localization still requires mandatory radiologist verification. E0
