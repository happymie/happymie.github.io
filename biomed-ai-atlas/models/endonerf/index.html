EndoNeRF

EndoNeRF 以时间条件 deformation MLP 把动态组织映射到 canonical NeRF，并显式利用手术器械 mask。Reconstructs deformable soft-tissue scenes from single-viewpoint stereo robotic endoscopy by pairing a canonical static NeRF with a time-conditioned deformation field and tool occlusion masks. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

静态 NeRF 无法处理组织形变、器械遮挡和稀疏内镜视角Robotic surgical scenes violate static NeRF assumptions due to non-rigid tissue deformation, smoke/specularities, and severe surgical instrument occlusion.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

用可变形 NeRF 联合建模内镜场景外观、几何和手术组织动态Warp 3D points at time t via a displacement MLP Δx(x, t) into a shared canonical radiance field while masking out rays occluded by surgical instruments.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

用于微创手术场景重建、导航和动态视图合成Pioneered neural field 3D soft-tissue reconstruction for DaVinci robotic prostatectomy and minimally invasive surgery.

设计原理Design principles

  • 把动态手术场景分成时间形变场和共享 canonical radiance field。Core Hypothesis: Warp 3D points at time t via a displacement MLP Δx(x, t) into a shared canonical radiance field while masking out rays occluded by surgical instruments. E0
  • 所有时刻先逆变形到同一规范空间，再查询密度和颜色。Architectural Delta: Tool-mask-guided importance ray sampling + stereo depth cue supervision + canonical-plus-deformation spatiotemporal factorization. E0
  • 器械 mask 引导射线采样，避免把临时遮挡建模成组织。Mathematical Formalism: Time-Conditioned Deformation Field Δx(x, t) Mapping Dynamic Tissue to a Canonical NeRF + Tool-Guided Ray Sampling. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可用于微创手术软组织重建、导航视图合成和动态场景分析。Clinical Translation: Pioneered neural field 3D soft-tissue reconstruction for DaVinci robotic prostatectomy and minimally invasive surgery. E0
  • 镜面反射、烟雾、快速非刚性运动和器械 mask 误差会破坏亮度恒定与 canonical 对应假设。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Landmark surgical NeRF baseline; cannot handle topological tissue cutting/resection and requires minutes of offline optimization (later accelerated by EndoGaussian). E0
