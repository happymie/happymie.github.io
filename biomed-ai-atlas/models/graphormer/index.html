Graphormer

Graphormer 不做局部消息传递堆叠，而在全局 attention logits 中显式加入最短路径和边特征偏置。Enables global all-pair Transformer self-attention over graphs by injecting degree centrality encodings into nodes and shortest-path spatial + edge-feature biases into attention logits. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

普通 Transformer 忽略图拓扑，消息传递 GNN 长程传播易过平滑Message-passing GNNs are bounded by the 1-WL isomorphism test and suffer from over-squashing across long graph bottlenecks.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

把最短路径距离、节点中心性和边特征作为 attention bias 注入 TransformerAllow every node to attend to every other node globally while encoding graph topology directly as additive biases inside the softmax attention matrix.

适用于分子、细胞相互作用、脑网络和患者关系图Excels on molecular property prediction, drug discovery, protein structure, and compact brain region connectome graphs (~100–500 ROIs).

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 保留全局 self-attention，但用图结构编码补回 Transformer 缺少的拓扑归纳偏置。Core Hypothesis: Allow every node to attend to every other node globally while encoding graph topology directly as additive biases inside the softmax attention matrix. E0
  • 中心性编码作用于单节点，最短路径距离和边编码作用于节点对。Architectural Delta: Centrality encoding + Shortest-Path Distance (SPD) spatial bias + multi-hop edge feature encoding along shortest paths. E0
  • virtual graph token 作为可学习全局汇聚器，避免手工 mean pooling。Mathematical Formalism: Graph Structural Bias Injection into Self-Attention Logits: (q_i k_j^T)/√d + b_{φ(v_i,v_j)} + c_{ij}. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适用于分子、脑连接组、细胞相互作用和患者关系图等全局依赖任务。Clinical Translation: Excels on molecular property prediction, drug discovery, protein structure, and compact brain region connectome graphs (~100–500 ROIs). E0
  • 全局注意力随节点数二次增长，且最短路径/构图规则可能把技术性边误当生物学关系。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Leading Graph Transformer; O(N²) all-pair attention and shortest-path computation prohibit direct application to 100,000-node WSI cell graphs. E0
