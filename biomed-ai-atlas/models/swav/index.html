原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 不同视图不直接做实例对比，而是互相预测在线聚类分配。Core Hypothesis: Compute online soft cluster assignments over learnable prototypes using Sinkhorn-Knopp equipartition and predict view A's code from view B. E0
  • Sinkhorn 约束批内原型使用近似均衡，降低全部样本落入单一原型的坍塌。Architectural Delta: Online balanced clustering + multi-crop augmentation strategy enabling memory-efficient small-batch SSL training. E0
  • 多裁剪让局部区域与全局图像共享语义，同时控制计算量。Mathematical Formalism: Swapped Cross-View Prediction of Sinkhorn-Balanced Prototype Codes. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可把病理局部 patch、放射图像局部视野与整图放进统一自监督训练。Clinical Translation: Applied to large-scale unlabeled histopathology patch clustering and radiographic representation learning. E0
  • 均衡 prototype 是优化约束而非真实病种患病率，不能把原型直接解释为临床类别。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Foundational clustering-based SSL method; multi-crop strategy became standard in DINO and DINOv2. E0
