  • 预训练 decoder 只服务恢复任务，真正复用单位是 3D encoder。Mathematical Formalism: Restorative 3D Autoencoding Across Composite Volumetric Corruptions. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 它奠定直接在 3D 医学体数据上做领域自监督的路线。Clinical Translation: Established the foundational paradigm for native 3D self-supervised pretraining in volumetric radiology. E0
