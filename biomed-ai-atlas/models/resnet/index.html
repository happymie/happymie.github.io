原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 残差块学习相对恒等映射的修正，而不是每层重新构造完整表示。Core Hypothesis: Reformulate layers to learn residual perturbations y = x + F(x) relative to an identity reference. E0
  • 恒等捷径提供不经过权重层的梯度通路，缓解深层优化退化。Architectural Delta: Parameter-free identity shortcuts and 1×1 projection shortcuts enable stable optimization beyond 100+ layers. E0
  • projection shortcut 只在空间或通道维度变化时匹配张量形状。Mathematical Formalism: Additive Residual Branch with Identity Shortcut Mapping. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 3D ResNet、Residual U-Net 与 nnU-Net ResEnc 都复用其块级优化机制。Clinical Translation: Foundational building block for 3D ResNet, Residual U-Net, and nnU-Net ResEnc across medical modalities. E0
  • 残差捷径解决深层优化，不等同于 U-Net 用于恢复定位信息的长跳连。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Mandatory foundational backbone and fair baseline across 2D and 3D biomedical tasks. E0
