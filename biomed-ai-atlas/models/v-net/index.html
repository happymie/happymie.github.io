02 / 核心想法Core idea

3D residual FCN 直接优化 Dice 型目标Combine a 3D residual convolutional encoder–decoder with a differentiable soft Dice loss.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

推动 Dice 型目标在医学体分割中的普及Popularized Dice-family losses (Dice + CE) as the standard training objective for medical volumetric segmentation.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 残差 3D 编解码器同时缓解深层优化与体数据上下文建模困难。Core Hypothesis: Combine a 3D residual convolutional encoder–decoder with a differentiable soft Dice loss. E0
  • strided convolution 让下采样本身可学习。Architectural Delta: Strided 3D convolutions replacing pooling, block-wise residual shortcuts, and direct overlap optimization. E0
  • Dice 型目标以集合重叠归一化前景规模，缓解极端类别不平衡。Mathematical Formalism: Direct Optimization of Differentiable Volumetric Dice Overlap. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • V-Net 是理解 3D residual segmentation 与 Dice-family loss 的经典节点。Clinical Translation: Popularized Dice-family losses (Dice + CE) as the standard training objective for medical volumetric segmentation. E0
  • Dice 高并不保证边界距离、拓扑或小病灶检出，仍应联合 HD95/NSD 和 lesion-wise 指标。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Classic 3D baseline; Dice loss alone remains insensitive to boundary Hausdorff outliers and topological breaks. E0
