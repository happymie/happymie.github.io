01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

global 图文 embedding 难定位细粒度征象Global image–report embeddings collapse spatial coordinates, failing to localize focal findings like small pneumothoraces or nodules.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

同时对齐整图-报告和局部影像区域-报告词语Align individual report words with attention-weighted combinations of local convolutional sub-regions alongside global study alignment.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

支持胸片检索、少标注分类/分割和 phrase groundingEnables zero-shot phrase grounding, text-to-image retrieval, and label-efficient chest X-ray segmentation/classification.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 全局对比保证一份报告与整幅影像语义匹配，局部对齐进一步把词语落到影像区域。Core Hypothesis: Align individual report words with attention-weighted combinations of local convolutional sub-regions alongside global study alignment. E0
  • 词到区域 attention 是软对应，不需要边界框即可从报告学习潜在定位。Architectural Delta: Joint global contrastive loss + word-to-region attention-weighted local contrastive objective without requiring bounding-box annotations. E0
  • 局部和全局目标互补：前者保留细粒度病灶线索，后者稳定检查级语义。Mathematical Formalism: Word-Attended Local Region Context Aggregation & Local–Global Contrastive Loss. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可利用报告中的病灶词实现弱监督定位，但 attention 热图不是经过校准的病灶边界。Clinical Translation: Enables zero-shot phrase grounding, text-to-image retrieval, and label-efficient chest X-ray segmentation/classification. E0
  • 否定、正常描述和非视觉性临床信息需在文本处理或评估中单独审计。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Established local grounding in medical VLMs; soft attention heatmaps remain coarse and do not guarantee causal localization. E0
