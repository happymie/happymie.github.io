3D Gaussian Splatting

显式优化各向异性 3D Gaussians，并通过屏幕空间 splatting 与自适应增密实现实时 radiance rendering。Represents 3D scenes explicitly as millions of anisotropic 3D Gaussians (position, covariance, opacity, spherical harmonics) rendered in real time via differentiable tile-based rasterization. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

NeRF 每射线 MLP 查询使训练与渲染较慢Continuous coordinate MLPs in NeRF required querying hundreds of points along every ray, bottlenecking real-time rendering and interactive editing.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

以显式各向异性 3D Gaussians 和 visibility-aware splatting 表示场景Replace implicit continuous MLPs with explicit 3D Gaussian primitives projected directly to 2D screen space via closed-form EWA covariance splatting.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 以显式各向异性 Gaussian primitives 替代每射线 MLP 查询，交换部分内存以换取实时 rasterization。Core Hypothesis: Replace implicit continuous MLPs with explicit 3D Gaussian primitives projected directly to 2D screen space via closed-form EWA covariance splatting. E0
  • 3D covariance 经相机投影成为屏幕椭圆，并通过可微 alpha compositing 参与图像损失。Architectural Delta: Anisotropic 3D covariance optimization (R S S^T R^T) + adaptive clone/split/prune density control + fast tile-based GPU sorting rasterizer. E0
  • 增密和剪枝让表示容量随场景细节自适应，而非固定 voxel 分辨率。Mathematical Formalism: Projective 3D-to-2D Covariance Splatting & Front-to-Back Alpha Compositing. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可启发内镜/术野新视角、动态解剖可视化和医学 X-ray Gaussian renderer。Clinical Translation: Rapidly adapted to surgical endoscopy 3D reconstruction (EndoGaussian) and radiative X-ray Gaussian tomography (R2-Gaussian). E0
  • 原始 RGB opacity compositing 不保证 HU、attenuation 或患者解剖真实性；医学应用必须重写物理和定量验证。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Genuine real-time rendering breakthrough; discrete Gaussian primitives can still produce needle-like artifacts under sparse views. E0
