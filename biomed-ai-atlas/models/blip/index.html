BLIP

区别于纯双塔，BLIP 用共享 MED 同时承担对齐、跨注意力匹配和因果生成，并把 captioner-filter 作为数据循环的一部分。Unifies vision–language understanding and generation via a Multimodal Mixture of Encoder–Decoder (MED) coupled with a CapFilt data bootstrapping loop. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

网页图文噪声大，理解与生成模型往往分开训练Pure dual encoders (CLIP) cannot generate free text, while noisy web captions degrade generative language modeling.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

以统一 encoder-decoder 架构结合图文对比、匹配和生成目标Share weights across a multimodal encoder–decoder trained jointly on contrastive, matching, and generative objectives while bootstrapping clean captions.
