01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

FCN 上采样边界粗，生物医学标注稀缺FCN upsampling lost fine boundary localization, while annotated biomedical datasets were small.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

对称 encoder-decoder 与同尺度长跳连联合语义和精确定位Pair a contracting semantic encoder with a symmetric expanding decoder linked by same-scale feature concatenation skips.
