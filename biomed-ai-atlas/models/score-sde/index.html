Score-SDE

以连续 score field 同时定义 reverse-time SDE、predictor-corrector sampler 与同边缘的 probability-flow ODE。Unifies discrete diffusion and score matching into a continuous-time Stochastic Differential Equation (SDE) with a corresponding deterministic Probability-Flow ODE. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

离散 diffusion 和 score matching 缺少统一连续框架SMLD (score matching) and DDPM (diffusion) were developed as separate discrete heuristics lacking a unified continuous mathematical theory.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

以连续时间 score network 统一 reverse SDE、probability-flow ODE 与采样器Cast forward corruption as a continuous Itô SDE whose reverse-time SDE and deterministic PF-ODE depend solely on the time-dependent score ∇_x log p_t(x).

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

为 MRI/CT posterior sampling 和 likelihood guidance 提供理论接口Provides the rigorous continuous-time mathematical foundation for MRI/CT score-based posterior reconstruction.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 连续时间 SDE 统一了离散 diffusion 与多噪声尺度 score matching。Core Hypothesis: Cast forward corruption as a continuous Itô SDE whose reverse-time SDE and deterministic PF-ODE depend solely on the time-dependent score ∇_x log p_t(x). E0
  • 反向 SDE 的随机路径和 PF-ODE 的确定性路径使用同一 score，并具有相同时间边缘分布。Architectural Delta: Unified VE/VP/sub-VP SDE taxonomy + Predictor-Corrector (PC) samplers + exact log-likelihood computation + controllable inverse conditional sampling. E0
  • predictor-corrector 将数值积分与每尺度 MCMC 校正组合，但质量和计算量均依赖步长策略。Mathematical Formalism: Continuous-Time Forward/Reverse SDE & Marginal-Equivalent Probability-Flow ODE. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可把测量 likelihood gradient 加入 reverse dynamics，用于 MRI/CT posterior sampling 与不确定性研究。Clinical Translation: Provides the rigorous continuous-time mathematical foundation for MRI/CT score-based posterior reconstruction. E0
  • score prior、噪声 SDE 和真实测量 likelihood 任一失配都会使样本不再代表可信后验。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Foundational mathematical framework; practical medical inverse solving still depends on accurate likelihood score approximations. E0
