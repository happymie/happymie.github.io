TransMorph

TransMorph 用层级 Swin encoder 取代纯 CNN 配准编码器，同时保留 U-Net 式 decoder 和可微 warping。Replaces the convolutional encoder in VoxelMorph with a 3D hierarchical Swin Transformer encoder while retaining a convolutional U-Net decoder and Spatial Transformer warping. E0

设计原理Design principles

  • Swin 的层级窗口注意力在可控成本下捕获远距离解剖对应。Core Hypothesis: Leverage 3D Swin Transformer blocks in the encoder to capture larger effective receptive fields for cross-volume matching. E0
  • Transformer encoder 提供多尺度 skip，卷积 decoder 恢复稠密局部位移。Architectural Delta: Hybrid 3D Swin encoder + CNN skip decoder + diffeomorphic/Bayesian variants benchmarked across brain MRI and CT. E0
  • Spatial transformer 把形变放回图像域，以相似度和场正则进行无监督训练。Mathematical Formalism: 3D Shifted-Window Transformer Correspondence Encoding + Differentiable Deformation Decoding. E0
