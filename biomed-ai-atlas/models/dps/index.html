01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

早期 diffusion inverse 方法难处理 noisy 或 nonlinear operatorsProjection-based diffusion solvers failed under heavy measurement noise or non-linear operators without closed-form SVD.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

把 learned score prior 与可微 measurement likelihood gradient 结合Approximate p(y|x_t) ≈ p(y | x̂_0(x_t)) using Tweedie's formula and take a gradient step ∇_{x_t} ||y − A(x̂_0(x_t))||² at every reverse diffusion step.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

可用于 MRI、CT、PET 等测量模型耦合的 posterior samplingWidely used in accelerated multi-coil MRI, sparse-view CT, phase retrieval, and PET posterior sampling research.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 用 Tweedie/去噪估计把不可直接求得的 noisy-state likelihood 近似为对 x0_hat 的测量残差。Core Hypothesis: Approximate p(y|x_t) ≈ p(y | x̂_0(x_t)) using Tweedie's formula and take a gradient step ∇_{x_t} ||y − A(x̂_0(x_t))||² at every reverse diffusion step. E0
  • 可微 A 使同一无条件 prior 能接入不同线性或非线性 inverse problems。Architectural Delta: Handles general noisy linear and non-linear differentiable forward operators without SVD or task-specific retraining. E0
  • 每个逆时间步都执行 score 与 data-consistency guidance，属于采样迭代而非固定 K 级学习优化器。Mathematical Formalism: Prior Score Coupled with Measurement Likelihood Gradient via Tweedie Denoised Estimate x̂_0. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可为 MRI、CT、PET 等显式测量模型生成多个候选样本，用于研究条件不确定性。Clinical Translation: Widely used in accelerated multi-coil MRI, sparse-view CT, phase retrieval, and PET posterior sampling research. E0
  • guidance 权重、噪声似然和 A 的误差会改变样本分布；多样性不等于经过校准的临床后验。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Core diffusion inverse baseline; requires backpropagation through the score network at every step (high memory/latency) and sensitive guidance-scale tuning. E0
