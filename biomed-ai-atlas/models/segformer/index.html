01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

Transformer 分割器常依赖重型 decoder 与固定位置编码，跨分辨率泛化受限Early Transformer segmenters relied on heavy decoders and fixed interpolated positional embeddings that degraded across resolutions.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

MiT 层级编码器结合轻量全 MLP decoder 融合多尺度特征Pair a hierarchical multi-scale Transformer encoder (using 3×3 depthwise convolutions for positional cues) with a lightweight all-MLP decoder.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • MiT 用层级多尺度编码器解决平坦 ViT 对 dense prediction 不友好的问题。Core Hypothesis: Pair a hierarchical multi-scale Transformer encoder (using 3×3 depthwise convolutions for positional cues) with a lightweight all-MLP decoder. E0
  • efficient attention 通过缩减 K/V 控制高分辨率计算，Mix-FFN 的 depthwise Conv 提供局部位置线索。Architectural Delta: Overlapping patch merging, spatial-reduction self-attention, Mix-FFN without fixed positional encodings, and MLP multi-scale fusion. E0
  • 解码器只做各尺度 MLP 投影、上采样和融合，把大部分表示能力放在 encoder。Mathematical Formalism: Position-Encoding-Free Mix-FFN & Multi-Scale All-MLP Aggregation. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适合作为 2D 内镜、病理、超声和一般医学分割的轻量 Transformer 基线。Clinical Translation: Popular lightweight Transformer baseline for 2D endoscopy, dermatology, ultrasound, and histopathology segmentation. E0
  • 2D 成功不能直接外推到完整 3D 体；重叠 patch、缩减比例和上采样需按小病灶尺寸验证。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Strong 2D dense-prediction baseline; extending sequence-reduced attention to 3D still requires careful memory engineering. E0
