原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 先用无标签 tile 学局部组织形态，再用 slide 弱标签学全片模式。Core Hypothesis: Combine a pretrained hybrid CNN-Transformer tile encoder with a weakly supervised WSI attention aggregator conditioned on anatomical site. E0
  • attention 聚合在大量实例中选择与病例标签相关的区域。Architectural Delta: Two-stage tile + slide pretraining across 19 anatomical sites with broad multi-center external validation. E0
  • 解剖部位上下文与多中心预训练共同提升跨癌种复用。Mathematical Formalism: Anatomical-Site-Conditioned Attention MIL Whole-Slide Aggregation & Contrastive Supervision. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 同一 WSI 表示可迁移到癌种识别、分子标志物和生存风险。Clinical Translation: Applied to cancer detection, tumor origin identification, genomic profile inference, and survival risk stratification. E0
  • 弱监督注意力不是病理学因果定位，回顾性预后关联也不等同于治疗决策效益。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Demonstrates broad multi-center retrospective validation; prognostic risk scores still require prospective trial verification. E0
