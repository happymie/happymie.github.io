原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 对比目标学习可检索、可零样本分类的全局图文几何；caption 目标迫使视觉 tokens 保留生成所需细节。Core Hypothesis: Pretrain a ViT vision encoder, text encoder, and multimodal caption decoder on 1.17M curated human histopathology image–caption pairs. E0
  • multimodal decoder 通过 cross-attention 在每个生成 token 时读取病理图像，而不只依赖单一全局向量。Architectural Delta: Unifies CLIP-style dual-encoder retrieval/zero-shot transfer with CoCa-style generative captioning in computational pathology. E0
  • 人类病理描述比通用网络 alt-text 更贴近领域概念，使开放词汇表征具有专业语义。Mathematical Formalism: Joint Contrastive Image–Text Alignment & Cross-Attended Generative Caption Loss. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 同一 backbone 可连接检索、零样本和生成，但生成内容必须以图像证据和不确定性机制约束。Clinical Translation: Enables zero-shot cancer subtyping, text-to-tile search, WSI zero-shot MI-Zero classification, and pathology captioning. E0
  • 病理 caption 覆盖和语言风格会限定开放词汇边界；罕见病种不能仅凭通用相似度宣称可靠。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Top-performing multimodal pathology foundation model; performance is bounded by educational/caption terminology coverage. E0
