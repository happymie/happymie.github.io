原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 同一实例的两种增强定义正样本，batch 内其他实例同时充当负样本。Core Hypothesis: Maximize agreement between differently augmented views of the same sample in a projected latent space while repelling all other batch samples. E0
  • 强数据增强决定要学习的不变性，温度参数控制正负相似度分布的锐度。Architectural Delta: Composition of strong augmentations, learnable non-linear MLP projection head g(·), and temperature-scaled cosine contrastive loss. E0
  • 把对比目标放在投影空间可避免直接扭曲供下游使用的 encoder 表征。Mathematical Formalism: Normalized Temperature-Scaled Cross-Entropy (NT-Xent) Contrastive Loss. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 增强必须尊重医学语义：左右翻转、颜色扰动或强裁剪可能改变侧别、染色或病灶标签。Clinical Translation: Sparked patient-aware, multi-view, and slice-proximity contrastive learning across radiology and pathology. E0
  • 大 batch 负样本需求在 3D 影像中代价高；同患者切片还可能成为假负样本。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Foundational contrastive SSL paradigm; requires large batch sizes and careful medical augmentation design to avoid false negatives. E0
