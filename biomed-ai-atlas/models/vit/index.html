Vision Transformer (ViT)

非重叠 patch 线性 token 化、CLS 汇聚与全局 Transformer encoder，完全移除卷积主干。Splits images into non-overlapping patches, linearly projects them into tokens with positional embeddings, and processes them via a pure global Transformer encoder. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

Transformer 缺少二维图像输入接口，视觉模型长期依赖卷积Standard Transformers could not directly ingest pixel grids due to quadratic cost, leaving computer vision reliant on CNNs.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

把图像切成 patch token 并使用标准 Transformer encoderTreat non-overlapping 16×16 image patches as sequence tokens and apply a standard isotropic Transformer encoder.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 把固定大小图像块直接视为 token，使纯 Transformer 可在视觉任务中工作。Core Hypothesis: Treat non-overlapping 16×16 image patches as sequence tokens and apply a standard isotropic Transformer encoder. E0
  • 全局 self-attention 从第一层起建立任意远 patch 间的内容依赖，而不依赖卷积感受野递增。Architectural Delta: Linear patchify stem, learnable 1D positional embeddings, and global self-attention from the very first layer. E0
  • CLS token 通过多层注意力汇聚整幅图像，预训练规模与正则化决定其数据效率。Mathematical Formalism: Non-Overlapping Patch Tokenization & Global Self-Attention Encoding. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 病理切片 patch、眼底图和二维放射影像可直接 token 化，但小病灶可能在粗 patch 中被平均。Clinical Translation: Dominant backbone for large-scale self-supervised pathology (UNI, Virchow) and multimodal medical foundation models. E0
  • 医学小数据场景通常需要自然图像或领域自监督预训练，且高分辨率会使全局注意力显存按 N² 增长。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Excels under massive pretraining (MAE, DINOv2, CLIP); underperforms CNNs when trained from scratch on small medical cohorts. E0
