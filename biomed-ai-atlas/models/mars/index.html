MARS

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

单序列、单器官预训练难处理临床 MRI 协议和对比度异质性Single-sequence or single-organ MRI models brittlely fail when clinical pulse sequences, TR/TE parameters, or contrasts vary.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

在大规模多序列 MRI 上解耦 anatomy-invariant 表征与 sequence-specific variationExplicitly factorize 3D MRI representations into an anatomy-invariant structural branch and a sequence-specific contrast branch guided by acquisition metadata.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 3D Swin 共享主干，但显式拆出解剖结构与 MRI 序列变化两类 latent。Core Hypothesis: Explicitly factorize 3D MRI representations into an anatomy-invariant structural branch and a sequence-specific contrast branch guided by acquisition metadata. E0
  • 结构/序列对比和部位/采集参数预测让两个支路承担不同信息。Architectural Delta: Pretrained on ~160,000 3D MRI scans via cross-sequence synthesis, masked reconstruction, and contrastive disentanglement. E0
  • 遮挡重建与受 sequence latent 调制的合成任务补充体素级生成约束。Mathematical Formalism: Anatomy–Sequence Disentanglement Conditioned on MRI Acquisition Parameters. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 同一预训练权重可接分割、分类、配准、进展预测与报告生成适配器。Clinical Translation: Adapts across 44 diverse downstream MRI tasks spanning segmentation, diagnosis, deformable registration, progression, and reporting. E0
  • 44 项结果来自异质任务和不同 heads，不能把总体胜出直接解释为单一临床场景已前瞻验证。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Broad multi-task scope reported by authors; independent external replication under standardized baselines remains necessary. E0
