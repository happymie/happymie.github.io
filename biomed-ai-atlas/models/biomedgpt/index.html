02 / 核心想法Core idea

以统一序列到序列框架处理生物医学图像、文本和多种输出格式Map vision patches, text subwords, and spatial locations into a shared discrete vocabulary processed by a unified BART/OFA-style encoder–decoder.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 把不同模态离散成 token，以相同 encoder—decoder 接口处理。Core Hypothesis: Map vision patches, text subwords, and spatial locations into a shared discrete vocabulary processed by a unified BART/OFA-style encoder–decoder. E0
  • 把分类、生成、问答和定位重写为带任务提示的 seq2seq。Architectural Delta: Multi-task, multi-specialty pretraining + instruction fine-tuning across vision, language, VQA, captioning, and detection. E0
  • 多领域多任务联合预训练使参数在放射、病理、皮肤和文本任务间共享。Mathematical Formalism: Unified Sequence-to-Sequence Autoregressive Modeling Over Multimodal Biomedical Tokens. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 一个模型可复用到多种生物医学理解和生成任务，降低每任务重新设计 head 的成本。Clinical Translation: Generalist biomedical foundation baseline spanning radiology, pathology, ophthalmology, dermatology, and biomedical NLP. E0
  • 统一 token 不代表统一证据强度；跨模态任务的数据规模、标注质量和安全验证仍须逐项报告。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Representative generalist architecture; specialist models often retain an edge on high-stakes quantitative or 3D tasks. E0
