Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN)

以因果、指数扩张的 1D 卷积残差块并行覆盖长历史，而不使用循环状态。Stacks 1D causal convolutions with exponentially increasing dilation factors d = 2^l inside residual blocks to cover long historical receptive fields in parallel. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

RNN 串行计算且有效记忆长度不稳定Recurrent networks (LSTM/GRU) could not parallelize across time during training and exhibited unstable effective memory horizons.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

用 causal dilated 1D convolution 与 residual blocks 建模长序列Combine strict causal padding (no future leakage) with exponentially dilated 1D convolutions (d=1, 2, 4, 8…) and residual shortcuts.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

成为 ECG/EEG 等生理信号强、便宜、稳定的 baselineHighly competitive, fast, and stable baseline for continuous ECG, EEG seizure detection, PPG, and EMG envelope decoding.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 用因果卷积保证在线预测不泄漏未来，用 dilation 在较少层数下覆盖长时间范围。Core Hypothesis: Combine strict causal padding (no future leakage) with exponentially dilated 1D convolutions (d=1, 2, 4, 8…) and residual shortcuts. E0
  • 所有时间位置可并行计算，避免 RNN 的逐步隐藏状态依赖。Architectural Delta: Full temporal parallelization during training + exact, deterministic receptive field calculation R = 1 + Σ (k−1)·d_l + low streaming memory. E0
  • 残差块稳定深层训练，但可见历史范围由结构预先固定，不会按样本动态选择远程依赖。Mathematical Formalism: Causal Exponentially Dilated 1D Convolution Preserving Temporal Ordering. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 是 ECG/EEG 等等间隔生理信号的高吞吐强基线，尤其适合流式事件检测。Clinical Translation: Highly competitive, fast, and stable baseline for continuous ECG, EEG seizure detection, PPG, and EMG envelope decoding. E0
  • 不规则采样、跨小时缺口或依赖超出感受野时，需要时间编码、重采样或更长上下文机制。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Practical first-line waveform baseline; receptive field is fixed by network depth/dilation and weights remain static across inputs. E0
