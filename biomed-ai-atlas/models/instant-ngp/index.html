Instant-NGP

Instant-NGP 的速度来自可学习多分辨率 hash-grid encoding 和空空间跳跃，而不是更深的 NeRF MLP。Replaces fixed sinusoidal positional encodings with a multi-resolution hierarchy of learnable hash tables paired with a tiny fused-CUDA MLP, accelerating neural fields by 100×–1000×. E0

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

坐标 MLP 的 Fourier features 训练慢，细节与速度难兼顾Deep coordinate MLPs (8 layers × 256 channels) were computationally expensive to evaluate millions of times per optimization step.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

用多分辨率哈希网格编码坐标并以小 MLP 表示场景Store trainable feature vectors directly in compact multi-resolution hash tables indexed by voxel corners, letting a tiny 2-layer MLP resolve hash collisions.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 多个空间尺度的可学习哈希表同时存储粗结构和细节。Core Hypothesis: Store trainable feature vectors directly in compact multi-resolution hash tables indexed by voxel corners, letting a tiny 2-layer MLP resolve hash collisions. E0
  • 哈希编码把表示容量放在局部特征表中，因此后端 MLP 可以很小。Architectural Delta: O(1) multi-scale hash lookup + implicit gradient-based collision resolution + occupancy grid empty-space skipping. E0
  • occupancy skipping 与 GPU 融合内核显著缩短训练和渲染。Mathematical Formalism: Multi-Resolution Spatial Hash-Grid Trilinear Interpolation & Tiny MLP Readout. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可加速 CBCT attenuation field、医学 INR、配准场或内镜场景优化。Clinical Translation: Core acceleration engine inside NAF (CBCT), implicit deformable registration, and real-time surgical scene neural fields. E0
  • 哈希碰撞和快速拟合可能隐藏局部伪影；定量衰减场需与投影残差和物理单位共同验证。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Essential coordinate-encoding infrastructure; high-frequency hash levels can fit measurement noise if not regularized. E0
