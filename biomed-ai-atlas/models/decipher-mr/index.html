Decipher-MR

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

3D MRI 标签稀缺且传统视觉预训练难吸收放射学语义3D MRI lacks massive manual labels, while naive global report alignment ignores which series or organ corresponds to which report sentence.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

先以 DINOv2 式视觉自监督学习 3D MRI 表示，再做 report-guided contrastive alignmentPretrain a 3D ViT via self-distillation on ~200,000 MRI series, then align series embeddings with organ-parsed radiology report segments.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

用于 3D MRI 检索、分类和跨任务迁移Supports zero-shot 3D MRI retrieval, disease classification, and label-efficient segmentation/grading across organs.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • Stage 1 独立预训练 3D 视觉 encoder 和医学文本 encoder。Core Hypothesis: Pretrain a 3D ViT via self-distillation on ~200,000 MRI series, then align series embeddings with organ-parsed radiology report segments. E0
  • 视觉侧用 DINOv2 student—teacher 与 iBOT 遮挡 patch 学全局和局部表示。Architectural Delta: Native 8×8×8 3D patch ViT + 3D DINOv2/iBOT visual SSL + organ-gated cross-modal contrastive learning. E0
  • Stage 2 将 3D ViT 与 PubMedBERT 投影到 512 维共享空间，按器官和 study 规则做图文对比。Mathematical Formalism: 3D Self-Distilled ViT + Organ-Aware Multi-Sequence MRI-to-Report Contrastive Alignment. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 原生 3D token 与报告弱监督共同支持 MRI 检索、分类、分割和异常定位。Clinical Translation: Supports zero-shot 3D MRI retrieval, disease classification, and label-efficient segmentation/grading across organs. E0
  • 报告是 study 级而 series 是局部输入，配对噪声和文本泄漏必须通过同 study 采样、外部集与无文本消融检验。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Recent 3D MRI VLM preprint; pretraining weights/code are not fully public, requiring independent verification. E0
