MediSyn

在公开医学图文对上微调 Stable Diffusion 1.4 的条件去噪 U-Net，冻结文本编码器与 VAE，用同一个潜扩散模型根据文本生成跨专科二维医学图像。MediSyn fine-tunes the conditional denoising U-Net of Stable Diffusion 1.4 on public medical image–text pairs while freezing the text encoder and VAE, using one latent diffusion model for text-guided 2D medical image synthesis across specialties. 结构总览ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW

MediSyn，从结构到机制。MediSyn: structure to mechanism.

输入INPUT

生成时输入描述模态、解剖部位或疾病的文本提示与随机高斯潜变量，去噪器还接收扩散时间步；训练时使用医学图像与由模态信息、既有类别标签组成的配对文本。Generation takes a text prompt describing modality, anatomy or disease, together with a Gaussian latent sample; the denoiser also receives the diffusion timestep. Training uses medical images paired with captions assembled from modality information and existing class labels.

→
输出OUTPUT

与文本提示条件对应的二维合成医学图像；作者演示采用512×512像素输出。A synthetic 2D medical image conditioned on the text prompt, with 512×512 output in the author demonstration.

关键设计DESIGN DECISION

通用性来自跨专科数据与训练设计，而非更换一套全新主干。作者比较六种基础模型及微调配置后，选定冻结 VAE 和文本编码器、仅更新 U-Net 的 SD1.4 配置作为 MediSyn。Generality comes from the cross-specialty data and training design rather than a completely new backbone. After comparing six model/training configurations, the authors select SD1.4 with a frozen VAE and text encoder and a fine-tuned U-Net as MediSyn.

    医学图像—文本对Medical image–text pairs

    汇集六个专科、十种成像模态。将已有类别标签与模态信息写成文本；尽可能先按患者划分训练、验证与测试数据，再进入生成模型训练。Six specialties and ten imaging modalities supply paired examples. Captions describe existing class labels and modality information; patient-level splitting is used wherever possible before model training. E1

  2. 02

    冻结 VAE 编码器Frozen VAE encoder

    训练阶段把真实图像编码为较小的空间潜表示。这个编码器保持冻结，图像压缩不是本次医学微调的主要学习位置。During training, a frozen VAE encoder maps real images to a smaller spatial latent representation. The image compressor is retained rather than being the main target of medical fine-tuning. E1

  3. 03

    冻结 CLIP 文本编码器Frozen CLIP text encoder

    从 CLIP ViT-L 取出的文本编码器把提示转成条件表示。发布配置的交叉注意力条件维度为768；最终选定的 MediSyn 配置不更新该编码器。A text encoder taken from CLIP ViT-L embeds the prompt. The released configuration uses a cross-attention condition dimension of 768, and the selected MediSyn configuration keeps this encoder frozen. E1

  4. 04

    潜空间加噪与时间步Latent corruption and timestep

    向训练图像的潜表示加入高斯噪声；带噪潜变量与时间步共同定义当前去噪问题。生成时从随机高斯潜变量开始，而不是先输入某位患者的图像。Gaussian noise corrupts an encoded training image. The noisy latent and timestep define the denoising problem; generation instead starts from a Gaussian latent sample rather than a patient image. E1

  5. 05

    医学微调条件 U-NetMedical fine-tuning of the conditional U-Net

    U-Net 预测噪声，文本通过中间层交叉注意力参与计算。固定发布配置使用4通道潜输入/输出、64的样本空间尺寸、每块2层，以及320、640、1280、1280的块通道宽度。The U-Net predicts noise, with text entering through intermediate cross-attention. The pinned release configuration specifies four input/output latent channels, sample size 64, two layers per block and block widths of 320, 640, 1280 and 1280. E1

  6. 06

    噪声预测均方误差Noise-prediction MSE

    将预测噪声与实际加入的噪声比较，以均方误差更新 U-Net。训练中10%的提示被替换为空文本，为推理时的无分类器引导提供无条件分支。Mean squared error compares predicted noise with the noise actually added, updating the U-Net. Ten percent of training prompts are replaced with empty text to support classifier-free guidance at inference. E1

  7. 07

    文本引导的迭代采样Text-guided iterative sampling

    复用已训练 U-Net，在逐步变化的时间步上去噪潜变量。作者演示将其装入 Stable Diffusion 1.4 管线，采用空负提示与7.0的引导尺度；这些是演示配置，不是所有任务的最优值。The trained U-Net repeatedly denoises the latent over successive timesteps. The author demo inserts it into a Stable Diffusion 1.4 pipeline and uses an empty negative prompt with guidance scale 7.0; these are demo settings rather than universal optima. E1

  8. 08

    冻结 VAE 解码器Frozen VAE decoder

    将最后的去噪潜表示映射回像素空间。VAE 解码器在图像合成阶段使用，仍保留基础模型的预训练参数。The frozen VAE decoder maps the final denoised latent back to pixels, retaining the base model's pretrained parameters during image synthesis. E1

  9. 09

    合成图像与下游评估Synthetic image and downstream evaluation

    输出二维医学图像，分别评估视觉分布、图文对应和下游分类用途。图像逼真度、生成多样性与对真实数据训练的帮助是不同的评价问题。The output is a 2D medical image. Distributional appearance, text alignment and downstream classifier utility are assessed separately; fidelity, diversity and usefulness for learning from real data are different evaluation questions. 01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

为每个专科、模态和部位维护独立生成模型，增加训练、部署与反复加载权重的成本；依赖私有训练数据也不便复现。MediSyn 研究能否让一个模型学习多种医学图像分布。Maintaining a generator for every specialty, modality and anatomical region adds training, deployment and repeated model-loading costs, while private training data limits reproducibility. MediSyn asks whether one model can learn diverse medical image distributions.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

保留 Stable Diffusion 1.4 的潜扩散框架，在统一的医学图文数据上只微调 U-Net。VAE 负责像素与潜表示之间的映射，冻结的文本编码器提供提示条件。Retain the latent diffusion framework of Stable Diffusion 1.4 and fine-tune only its U-Net on a unified medical image–text collection. The VAE maps between pixels and latents, while a frozen text encoder supplies prompt conditions.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

用已有模态信息和类别标签构建提示，覆盖胃肠、放射、病理、外科、皮肤与眼科。用途是可控合成与低数据条件下的数据增强，学习目标仍是生成图像。Prompts use existing modality information and class labels across gastroenterology, radiology, pathology, surgery, dermatology and ophthalmology. The aim is controlled synthesis and augmentation in limited-data settings, with image generation remaining the learning objective.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 潜空间承担扩散计算，像素空间主要由 VAE 处理。训练时编码真实图像并加入噪声；生成时从高斯潜变量开始，二者共享条件噪声预测器。Diffusion operates in latent space while the VAE handles the pixel representation. Training encodes and corrupts real images; generation starts with Gaussian latents, sharing the same conditional noise predictor. E1
  • 文本不是最终图像的标签预测结果，而是控制生成分布的条件。交叉注意力让 U-Net 的中间特征利用提示中的模态与医学语义。Text is a condition on the generated distribution rather than a diagnostic label predicted from the output. Cross-attention lets intermediate U-Net features use modality and medical semantics from the prompt. E1
  • 最终发布路线只微调 U-Net；VAE 与 CLIP ViT-L 文本编码器保持冻结。论文还比较 SDXL 和 BiomedCLIP 文本编码器变体，不能把所有变体都称为已发布的 MediSyn 配置。The selected release fine-tunes only the U-Net while freezing the VAE and CLIP ViT-L text encoder. SDXL and BiomedCLIP text-encoder configurations are also compared in the paper and should not all be presented as the released MediSyn configuration. E1
  • 第4.2节描述的训练设计为15个epoch、有效批量128、SD学习率5×10⁻⁵、Bfloat16计算与10%空提示。损失比较预测噪声和加入噪声，生成图像并不需要逐像素配对的目标图像。Section 4.2 specifies 15 epochs, effective batch size 128, SD learning rate 5×10⁻⁵, Bfloat16 computation and 10% empty prompts. The objective compares predicted and added noise; generation does not require a paired target image at inference. E1
  • 研究使用1,260,826个图文对，其中训练1,014,781、验证122,463、测试123,582；尽可能按患者约80∶10∶10划分。公开可获取不等于每个源数据集都允许任意再分发，数据仍保留各自使用条件。The study curates 1,260,826 pairs, comprising 1,014,781 training, 122,463 validation and 123,582 test pairs, using approximately 80∶10∶10 patient-level splits wherever possible. Public availability does not give every source dataset unrestricted redistribution rights. E1
  • 本条的机制和原图对应 arXiv v7（2026-04-21），首次预印本公开于2024-05-16；期刊论文于2026-10-16发表。作者公开的是 U-Net 权重、配置与推理演示，本条不把它描述为完整公开训练工程。The mechanism and original figure here follow arXiv v7, dated 2026-04-21. The first preprint appeared on 2024-05-16 and the journal article on 2026-10-16. The authors release a U-Net, configuration and inference demonstration; this is not described here as a complete published training project. E1

医学边界Medical scope

  • 覆盖六个专科和十种成像模态：CT、X光、MRI、超声、内镜、显微、眼底、OCT、皮肤镜与临床照片。研究的是二维图像合成，不应据此推定完整三维体数据或任何未列出的模态。The six specialties span ten modalities: CT, X-ray, MRI, ultrasound, endoscopy, microscopy, fundoscopy, OCT, dermoscopy and clinical photography. The work studies 2D synthesis rather than establishing full 3D volume generation or support for unlisted modalities. E1
  • 论文用多种评估分别检查合成质量、加载与推理成本、医生对视觉真实性及图文对应的判断、训练图像近邻差异，以及低数据条件下的分类增强。每种评估回答的问题不同。Separate evaluations examine synthetic quality, loading/inference cost, physician judgments of realism and text alignment, differences from training-image neighbors, and classifier augmentation with limited real data. These evaluations answer different questions. E1
  • 对皮肤镜和机器人辅助前列腺切除图像，MediSyn 的 FID 更低但 MS-SSIM 更高；肌骨X光方向相反。不能把“跨专科”简化为所有任务的保真度与多样性都同时改善。In dermoscopy and robot-assisted prostatectomy images, MediSyn has lower FID but higher MS-SSIM than the task-specific counterpart; musculoskeletal X-rays show the reverse pattern. Cross-specialty training does not imply simultaneous fidelity and diversity improvements for every task. E1
  • 论文给出的失败例包括：虽见过MRI和胸部X光，仍不能据此生成可靠的胸部MRI；此前未见过的疾病描述也可能得到医学上不准确的图像。The paper gives a concrete failure example: seeing MRI and chest X-rays does not enable reliable chest MRI generation. Previously unseen disease descriptions can also yield medically inaccurate images. E1
  • 合成图像与训练近邻不同，不等于形式化隐私保证。训练模态不平衡、类别间差异与临床准确性仍需在所需用途上分别验证。Differences from nearest training images do not establish a formal privacy guarantee. Modality imbalance, class-dependent behavior and clinical accuracy still require evaluation for the intended use. E1

结论与出处Claims and sources

基础架构Architecture

MediSyn 采用 Stable Diffusion 1.4 的 VAE、CLIP ViT-L 文本编码器与交叉注意力 U-Net；最终配置只微调 U-Net。MediSyn uses the VAE, CLIP ViT-L text encoder and cross-attention U-Net of Stable Diffusion 1.4; only the U-Net is fine-tuned in the selected configuration.
