Rectified Flow

01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

弯曲生成轨迹需要更多 ODE steps 才能准确积分Curved ODE transport trajectories accumulate high discretization error under large Euler step sizes.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

从 source-target coupling 学直线速度场，并用 reflow 进一步拉直轨迹Drive transport paths as close to straight lines as possible by regressing the constant displacement (x_1 − x_0) and iteratively re-pairing (z_0, ẑ_1) via reflow.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

为少步医学生成与 translation 提供速度路线Provides a principled mathematical route for real-time few-step medical domain translation and generative modeling.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 用端点线性插值监督 velocity，使学习到的流尽量沿短而直的运输轨迹。Core Hypothesis: Drive transport paths as close to straight lines as possible by regressing the constant displacement (x_1 − x_0) and iteratively re-pairing (z_0, ẑ_1) via reflow. E0
  • reflow 以模型自身生成的端点重新配对并重训，逐轮降低轨迹弯曲和交叉。Architectural Delta: Simple ODE velocity formulation + Reflow procedure that provably reduces transport cost and straightens trajectories for 1-step distillation. E0
  • 训练不需要 score；速度场的直线性直接关系到粗步长 ODE 的离散误差。Mathematical Formalism: Linear Interpolation Velocity Matching & Iterative Reflow Trajectory Straightening. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 对需要交互速度的医学图像生成、translation 或条件重建具有少步潜力。Clinical Translation: Provides a principled mathematical route for real-time few-step medical domain translation and generative modeling. E0
  • 轨迹更直只保证数值积分更容易，不保证微小病灶、定量强度或患者身份信息保持。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Genuine algorithmic speed advantage; reflow distillation can subtly smooth high-frequency diagnostic textures if not audited. E0
