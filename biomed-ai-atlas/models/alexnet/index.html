    输入图像Input Image

    RGB 图像经随机裁剪、翻转与均值处理进入网络。RGB image enters the network after random cropping, horizontal flipping, and mean subtraction. E0

  2. 02

    11×11 Conv11×11 Conv

    大步幅卷积快速降采样，ReLU 取代易饱和激活。Large-stride convolution rapidly downsamples spatial resolution while ReLU replaces saturating activations. E0

  3. 03

    Pool + LRNPool + LRN

    重叠最大池化压缩空间；原版使用局部响应归一化。Overlapping max-pooling compresses spatial dimensions; original formulation applies Local Response Normalization (LRN). E0

  4. 04

    5×5 分组卷积5×5 Grouped Conv

    原始双 GPU 实现按通道分组计算第二层卷积。Original dual-GPU implementation partitions channels into two parallel groups for second-layer convolution. E0

  5. 05

    3×3 Conv 堆叠Stacked 3×3 Convs

    连续三层卷积组合中层纹理与高层形状证据。Three consecutive 3×3 convolutional layers compose mid-level textures and high-level shape cues. E0

  6. 06

    FC6–FC7FC6–FC7

    两层大容量全连接层，训练时以 dropout 抑制共适应。Two high-capacity fully connected layers regularized via dropout during training to suppress co-adaptation. E0

  7. 07

    分类头Classification Head

    线性层把高维特征映射到固定类别 logits。Linear classifier projects high-dimensional features onto closed-set class logits. E0

  8. 08

    SoftmaxSoftmax

    将 logits 归一化为封闭类别概率。Normalizes raw logits into closed-set categorical probabilities via Softmax. E0

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 局部卷积与权重共享把图像结构先验写入参数化方式。Core Hypothesis: End-to-end GPU-trained deep convolutional classifier scaling capacity via ReLU, dropout, and aggressive data augmentation. E0
  • ReLU、GPU 并行和数据增强共同使大规模深监督训练成为现实。Architectural Delta: 5 convolutional layers + 3 fully connected layers integrating multi-GPU execution, ReLU non-linearity, dropout, and crop/color augmentations. E0
  • dropout 主要约束大容量全连接层，并不替代外部验证。Mathematical Formalism: Cross-Channel Local Response Normalization (LRN) in Original AlexNet. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 可作为理解早期 ImageNet 到医学 2D 迁移的历史起点，但今天不应作为强主干。Clinical Translation: Catalyzed transfer of generic ImageNet CNN representations across 2D medical imaging classification and detection. E0
  • 早期大步幅与多次下采样容易抹去微小病灶，医学使用应审计输入分辨率。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Historical milestone; superseded as a competitive medical backbone. E0
