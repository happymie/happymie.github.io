01 / 上一代瓶颈Prior limitation

通用 LLaVA 缺医学术语和科研图像知识，人工指令数据昂贵General LLaVA lacked biomedical terminology and figure interpretation skills, while manual clinician instruction datasets were unavailable.

02 / 核心想法Core idea

将生物医学视觉编码器通过投影连接到 LLM，并用合成指令数据微调Project BiomedCLIP/ViT visual tokens into an LLM and fine-tune on multi-turn biomedical instructions synthesized from PubMed captions.

03 / 医学适配Medical adaptation

用于生物医学图像问答、解释与研究辅助Widely adopted open-source baseline for biomedical VQA, conversational research assistance, and MLLM benchmarking.

原理与医学适配Principles and medical adaptation

设计原理Design principles

  • 先把预训练视觉特征映射进语言模型 embedding 空间。Core Hypothesis: Project BiomedCLIP/ViT visual tokens into an LLM and fine-tune on multi-turn biomedical instructions synthesized from PubMed captions. E0
  • 先做图像—图注概念对齐，再用医学指令数据训练对话遵循。Architectural Delta: Fast 15-hour two-stage curriculum: Stage 1 linear projection concept alignment on PMC-15M + Stage 2 end-to-end synthetic instruction tuning. E0
  • 保持 LLaVA 的简单线性连接结构，以数据构造而非复杂 fusion 获得领域能力。Mathematical Formalism: Linear/MLP Visual Projection + Two-Stage Biomedical Autoregressive Instruction Tuning. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 适合生物医学图像问答、论文图像解释与医学教学研究。Clinical Translation: Widely adopted open-source baseline for biomedical VQA, conversational research assistance, and MLLM benchmarking. E0
  • PMC 图注偏研究图表而非临床全流程，回答可靠性和患者级安全性必须单独验证。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Foundational open medical MLLM; synthetic caption-derived instructions cannot substitute for verified clinical DICOM ground truth. E0
