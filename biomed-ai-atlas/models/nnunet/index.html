设计原理Design principles

  • 模型配置由数据集 fingerprint 和显存约束推导，而非固定网络图。Core Hypothesis: Automate preprocessing, network topology, patch/batch sizing, sampling, training schedule, and post-processing directly from dataset statistics. E0
  • 把预处理、采样、增强、loss、推理和后处理纳入同一可复现对象。Architectural Delta: Couples rule-based domain heuristics (spacing, anisotropy, modality normalization) with empirical cross-validation model selection. E0
  • 通过交叉验证选择配置与后处理，减少单次 split 和手工调参偏差。Mathematical Formalism: Dataset-Fingerprint Rule Engine & 5-Fold Sliding-Window Ensemble. E0

医学边界Medical scope

  • 它是大多数新医学分割方法应在相同数据条件下比较的系统级强基线。Clinical Translation: Serves as the undisputed reproducible infrastructure across multi-organ and multi-modality medical segmentation benchmarks. E0
  • 其优势不能被简单归因于 U-Net block；公平消融必须锁定完整 pipeline。Evidence & Safety Boundary: Mandatory first-line baseline against which any newly proposed segmentation architecture must be benchmarked. E0
