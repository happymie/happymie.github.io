去噪与图像恢复Denoising & Restoration

2017 · IEEE Transactions on Image Processing

DnCNN

深 CNN 回归噪声残差，再与输入显式相减，而非直接回归 clean image。Trains a deep plain CNN with BatchNorm and ReLU to regress the additive noise residual R_θ(y) ≈ n rather than the clean image, recovering x̂ = y − R_θ(y).
去噪与图像恢复Denoising & Restoration＋
2018 · ICML

Noise2Noise

使用同一信号的两次独立无偏噪声实现进行 noisy-to-noisy 监督，训练阶段不需要干净靶图。Trains a denoising network using pairs of independent noisy realizations (y_1, y_2) of the same underlying anatomy without ever observing clean ground truth.
去噪与图像恢复Denoising & Restoration＋
2019 · ICML

Noise2Self

通过 J-invariant 遮挡使单幅 noisy image 同时提供输入与 held-out 监督目标。Formalizes single-image self-supervised denoising via J-invariant functions whose prediction for pixel subset J does not depend on the observed noisy values in J.
去噪与图像恢复Denoising & Restoration＋
2019 · CVPR

Noise2Void

Noise2Void 通过输入操纵构造 blind spot，只从单张 noisy 图像回归其被隐藏中心，而非需要成对噪声或 clean target。Implements practical single-image blind-spot denoising by randomly masking center pixels via neighborhood replacement so a standard U-Net predicts held-out pixels.
去噪与图像恢复Denoising & Restoration＋
2016 · NeurIPS

RED-Net

相较普通 autoencoder，RED-Net 在每个镜像卷积—反卷积层之间建立对称加法 skip，而不只保留少数 U-Net 尺度跳连。Deep symmetric convolutional–deconvolutional autoencoder connected by layer-by-layer additive residual skip connections at every mirrored depth.
去噪与图像恢复Denoising & Restoration＋
2021 · ICCV Workshops

SwinIR

SwinIR 把多个带卷积残差的 RSTB 置于浅层卷积和任务重建头之间，以移窗注意力服务高分辨率恢复。Sandwiches multiple Residual Swin Transformer Blocks (RSTBs, each ending in a 3×3 convolutional residual) between a shallow CNN extractor and a reconstruction head.
去噪与图像恢复Denoising & Restoration＋
2022 · CVPR

Restormer

Restormer 用 MDTA 在通道维做转置注意力，并以 GDFN 门控深度卷积替代普通 Transformer FFN。Replaces spatial N×N self-attention with Multi-Dconv Head Transposed Attention (MDTA) across channels (C×C) paired with a Gated-Dconv Feed-Forward Network (GDFN).
