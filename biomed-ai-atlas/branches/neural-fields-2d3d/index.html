X2CT-GAN

双正交 2D 编码器将特征提升并融合到 3D，再以体素重建和 3D adversarial loss 联合监督。Lifts features from two orthogonal (bi-planar PA + lateral) 2D chest X-rays into 3D via backprojection-inspired feature expansion and cross-view fusion supervised by 3D voxel + GAN losses.
2D→3D 与神经辐射场2D-to-3D & Neural Fields＋
2020 · ECCV

NeRF

位置编码坐标 MLP 预测连续密度与视角相关颜色，再沿相机射线做可微体渲染。Represents a continuous 3D scene as a 5D coordinate MLP (x,y,z,θ,φ) → (σ, c) with Fourier positional encoding, optimized via differentiable emission–absorption volume rendering.
2D→3D 与神经辐射场2D-to-3D & Neural Fields＋
2022 · MICCAI

NAF

以 hash-encoded 坐标网络表示 X-ray attenuation，并用 Beer–Lambert 重投影误差逐病例优化。Rewrites NeRF for cone-beam CT by replacing view-dependent emission–absorption with an isotropic X-ray linear attenuation field μ(x) optimized via Beer–Lambert reprojection error and hash encoding.
2D→3D 与神经辐射场2D-to-3D & Neural Fields＋
2023 · ACM Transactions on Graphics / SIGGRAPH

3D Gaussian Splatting

显式优化各向异性 3D Gaussians，并通过屏幕空间 splatting 与自适应增密实现实时 radiance rendering。Represents 3D scenes explicitly as millions of anisotropic 3D Gaussians (position, covariance, opacity, spherical harmonics) rendered in real time via differentiable tile-based rasterization.
2D→3D 与神经辐射场2D-to-3D & Neural Fields＋
2021 · CVPR

pixelNeRF

pixelNeRF 将每个 3D 点与其投影像素的 CNN 特征绑定，使 NeRF 从单场景优化器变为跨场景条件模型。Conditions a coordinate NeRF MLP on pixel-aligned 2D CNN feature vectors projected from 1 or a few input views, turning NeRF from a per-scene optimizer into a feed-forward cross-scene prior.
2D→3D 与神经辐射场2D-to-3D & Neural Fields＋
2022 · ACM Transactions on Graphics / SIGGRAPH

Instant-NGP

Instant-NGP 的速度来自可学习多分辨率 hash-grid encoding 和空空间跳跃，而不是更深的 NeRF MLP。Replaces fixed sinusoidal positional encodings with a multi-resolution hierarchy of learnable hash tables paired with a tiny fused-CUDA MLP, accelerating neural fields by 100×–1000×.
2D→3D 与神经辐射场2D-to-3D & Neural Fields＋
2022 · IEEE EMBC

MedNeRF

MedNeRF 把 NeRF 置于 GAN 式跨病例生成先验中，以 shape/appearance latent 从单 X-ray 合成视角，而非逐场景多视图优化。Adapts GIRAFFE/GRAF 3D-aware generative radiance fields to X-ray projections, disentangling 3D shape and appearance latents to synthesize continuous-angle CT projections from a single X-ray.
2D→3D 与神经辐射场2D-to-3D & Neural Fields＋
2022 · MICCAI

EndoNeRF

EndoNeRF 以时间条件 deformation MLP 把动态组织映射到 canonical NeRF，并显式利用手术器械 mask。Reconstructs deformable soft-tissue scenes from single-viewpoint stereo robotic endoscopy by pairing a canonical static NeRF with a time-conditioned deformation field and tool occlusion masks.
2D→3D 与神经辐射场2D-to-3D & Neural Fields＋
2026 · MICCAI 2026

PhyDiCT

在固定预训练三维扩散先验上交替施加投影观测约束与去噪更新；文本报告、几何和预训练权重都是显式条件。A frozen text-conditioned 3D diffusion prior is guided by differentiable projection consistency during inference; reports, geometry and pretrained weights are explicit conditions.
