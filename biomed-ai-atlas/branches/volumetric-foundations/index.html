Merlin

以 I3D ResNet-152 原生编码完整 CT，并用 EHR phenotype prediction 与 Clinical Longformer 报告对比进行多源多任务预训练。Native 3D abdominal CT foundation model encoding whole volumes with an I3D ResNet-152 supervised jointly by EHR diagnosis codes (phenotypes) and radiology reports.

2025 · Nature Biomedical Engineering

BME-X

BME-X 不是通用 MRI encoder，而是先预测组织标签、再用组织感知 DU-Net 生成增强图像的两阶段恢复模型。Two-stage brain MRI enhancement foundation model that first estimates tissue probability maps and then conditions a Dual U-Net (DU-Net) to restore harmonized images.

2026 · Nature Biomedical Engineering

Prima

Prima 的核心链路是 32×32×4 子体积 VQ-VAE token、sequence ViT、study ViT，再以神经放射报告做 CLIP 对齐。Hierarchical clinical brain MRI study foundation model chaining 32×32×4 sub-volume VQ-VAE tokenization, a sequence-level ViT, and a study-level ViT aligned with radiology reports.

2026 · Nature Biomedical Engineering

MARS

MARS 用 3D Swin 加结构/序列双支路、元数据与对比约束，并通过重建/合成把 anatomy-invariant 与 sequence-specific 信息解耦。Multi-sequence 3D MRI foundation model pairing a 3D Swin backbone with dual structure/sequence branches and acquisition metadata to disentangle anatomy from contrast.

2026 · npj Digital Medicine

Decipher-MR

Decipher-MR 明确采用 8³ patch 的 3D ViT-Base、DINOv2/iBOT 视觉预训练、PubMedBERT MLM，再做器官感知的 MRI-report CLIP 对齐。3D MRI vision–language foundation model combining an 8³-patch 3D ViT-Base (pretrained via 3D DINOv2/iBOT) with PubMedBERT MLM and organ-aware MRI–report CLIP alignment.
