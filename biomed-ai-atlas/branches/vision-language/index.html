图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models

2021 · ICML

CLIP

完全分离的图像/文本双塔，通过批内 N×N 对称对比目标对齐，并用文本 prompt 充当开放词表分类器。Aligns separate image and text encoders in a shared embedding space via symmetric in-batch N×N contrastive loss, turning text prompts into zero-shot classifiers.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2023 · ICML

BLIP-2

Q-Former 的 learnable queries 作为冻结图像 encoder 与冻结 LLM 之间唯一可训练的视觉信息瓶颈。Bridges a frozen pretrained image encoder to a frozen LLM using a lightweight Querying Transformer (Q-Former) with learnable query tokens as an information bottleneck.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2022 · MLHC

ConVIRT

将自然生成的医学图像–报告对输入全局双塔，通过双向跨模态对比预训练医学视觉 encoder。Pretrains medical visual encoders directly from naturally occurring paired radiology images and free-text reports via bidirectional contrastive loss.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2021 · ICCV

GLoRIA

在全局图文对比之外，以词为 query 对 CNN region grid 做软 attention 并训练局部区域–词对齐。Combines global image–report contrastive alignment with word-queried soft attention over local CNN image grid regions for fine-grained phrase grounding.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2022 · ECCV

BioViL

以放射报告专用 CXR-BERT 提升文本语义，再联合学习检查级图文对比与短语–视觉 patch grounding。Pairs a radiology-specialized text encoder (CXR-BERT with RSM masked pretraining) with joint global and local patch–phrase contrastive alignment.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2025 · NEJM AI

BiomedCLIP

以 PMC-15M 广域生物医学 figure–caption 语料、ViT 视觉塔和 PubMedBERT 文本塔训练领域 CLIP。Domain-specific CLIP trained on PMC-15M (15 million biomedical figure–caption pairs from PubMed Central) using a ViT vision tower and PubMedBERT text tower.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2021 · ICML

ALIGN

ALIGN 的主要贡献不是新交互模块，而是简单 EfficientNet/BERT 双塔在十亿级未精洗图文对上的规模化训练。Demonstrates that a simple EfficientNet + BERT dual encoder trained on over one billion noisy web image–alt-text pairs learns robust cross-modal representations.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2022 · ICML

BLIP

区别于纯双塔，BLIP 用共享 MED 同时承担对齐、跨注意力匹配和因果生成，并把 captioner-filter 作为数据循环的一部分。Unifies vision–language understanding and generation via a Multimodal Mixture of Encoder–Decoder (MED) coupled with a CapFilt data bootstrapping loop.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2024 · CVPR

Florence-2

核心不是增加多个 head，而是用任务提示和位置 token 将 caption、检测、grounding、分割全部改写为 seq2seq。Reformulates captioning, object detection, phrase grounding, and segmentation into a single prompt-driven sequence-to-sequence generation task with quantized coordinate tokens.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2022 · EMNLP

MedCLIP

MedCLIP 的关键不是更深双塔，而是用医学知识构造任意图像—文本间的软语义配对。Replaces hard binary 1-to-1 image–report pairing with a soft semantic similarity matrix derived from clinical entity extraction, enabling unpaired contrastive learning.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2023 · Machine Learning for Health / PMLR

Med-Flamingo

区别于单一投影接 LLM，Med-Flamingo 用 Perceiver 固定视觉 latent，并在多层冻结 LLM 中反复注入门控跨注意力。Compresses variable-number medical images via a Perceiver Resampler and injects them into a frozen LLM through tanh-gated cross-attention layers for few-shot in-context learning.

2023 · NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks

LLaVA-Med

其贡献主要是将 LLaVA 两阶段训练移到生物医学域：PMC 图注概念对齐后再做合成医学指令微调。Adapts the LLaVA visual-instruction-tuning paradigm to biomedicine via two-stage training: PMC figure–caption alignment followed by GPT-4-synthesized biomedical instruction tuning.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2025 · Nature Communications

RadFM

RadFM 的模型特异性在于统一 2D/3D 视觉 encoder 与 Perceiver 扫描聚合，再接生成式 LLM。Unifies 2D radiographs and 3D volumetric scans (CT/MRI) via a 2D/3D ViT + Perceiver 3D aggregator feeding into a generative radiology LLM.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2023 · arXiv preprint

MAIRA-1

MAIRA-1 不是通用图文预训练模型，而是冻结胸片 RAD-DINO、使用四层 adapter，并完整微调 Vicuna-7B 的专用报告生成器。Specialist chest X-ray report generator pairing a frozen CXR-specific RAD-DINO visual encoder, a 4-layer MLP adapter, and a fully fine-tuned Vicuna-7B LLM.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2024 · arXiv preprint

MAIRA-2

相较 MAIRA-1，MAIRA-2 增加多视图/既往/报告上下文，并把报告句子与生成式 bounding-box token 绑定。Extends MAIRA-1 with multi-view (AP/PA + lateral), prior study/report longitudinal context, and interleaved quantized bounding-box tokens for grounded reporting.
图文对齐与医学 VLMVision-Language Models＋
2024 · Nature Medicine

BiomedGPT

BiomedGPT 以模态无关、任务无关的 encoder—decoder token 化接口统一生物医学视觉、语言和多模态输出。Modality- and task-agnostic encoder–decoder Transformer unifying biomedical images, clinical text, and bounding boxes into a discrete token sequence interface.
