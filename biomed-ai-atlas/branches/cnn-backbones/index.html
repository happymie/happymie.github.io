AlexNet

以 GPU、ReLU、dropout 和深卷积组合首次规模化训练 ImageNet 级深 CNN。First large-scale ImageNet-grade deep CNN trained end-to-end by combining dual-GPU parallelism, non-saturating ReLU activations, dropout regularization, and stacked convolutions.

ResNet

显式 identity shortcut 与残差相加 y=x+F(x)。Explicit identity shortcut connections combined with additive residual learning y = x + F(x).

DenseNet

每层对所有前层输出做通道 concat，而非仅连接相邻层或做残差相加。Every layer concatenates feature maps from all preceding layers along the channel axis rather than summing adjacent residuals.

ConvNeXt

7×7 depthwise spatial mixer 加 inverted bottleneck 的现代纯卷积块。Modernized pure ConvNet block pairing a 7×7 depthwise spatial mixer with an inverted channel bottleneck.

2015 · ICLR

VGG-16/19

以高度同质的 3×3 plain-convolution blocks 规律加深到 16/19 层，无残差连接。Homogeneous stacking of 3×3 plain convolutional blocks systematically deepened to 16–19 layers without residual shortcuts.

2015 · CVPR

GoogLeNet / Inception-v1

在每个 Inception module 中并行执行 1×1、降维后 3×3/5×5 和 pooling 分支，再通道拼接。Parallel multi-scale branches (1×1, bottlenecked 3×3, 5×5 convolutions, and pooling) concatenated within each Inception module.

2017 · CVPR

ResNeXt

在 residual bottleneck 中以 cardinality 控制的 grouped 3×3 convolution 聚合多个等拓扑变换。Aggregates multiple homogeneous residual branches via grouped 3×3 convolutions governed by a cardinality hyperparameter.

2018 · CVPR

SENet

全局平均池化 squeeze 后经 bottleneck MLP 产生每通道 sigmoid 门，对卷积特征逐通道重标定。Global average pooling (Squeeze) followed by a lightweight bottleneck MLP (Excitation) producing per-channel sigmoid gates.

EfficientNet

以 NAS 设计的 MBConv+SE 基线配合 depth/width/resolution 三维 compound scaling。NAS-discovered MBConv+SE baseline scaled jointly across depth, width, and input resolution via a compound coefficient φ.
