AlexNet · 2012

以 GPU、ReLU、dropout 和深卷积组合首次规模化训练 ImageNet 级深 CNN。First large-scale ImageNet-grade deep CNN trained end-to-end by combining dual-GPU parallelism, non-saturating ReLU activations, dropout regularization, and stacked convolutions.

问题：手工特征占主导，深 CNN 计算昂贵且易饱和或过拟合Question: Hand-crafted features dominated vision; deep CNNs were computationally prohibitive and prone to saturating gradients or severe overfitting.

机制：GPU 训练的深层卷积分类器，以 ReLU、dropout 和增强扩大可训练规模Mechanism: End-to-end GPU-trained deep convolutional classifier scaling capacity via ReLU, dropout, and aggressive data augmentation.

ResNet · 2016

显式 identity shortcut 与残差相加 y=x+F(x)。Explicit identity shortcut connections combined with additive residual learning y = x + F(x).

问题：网络加深后出现优化退化，训练误差反而上升Question: Deepening plain networks triggered optimization degradation where training error paradoxically increased with depth.

机制：用恒等捷径学习残差映射 y=x+F(x)Mechanism: Reformulate layers to learn residual perturbations y = x + F(x) relative to an identity reference.

Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition · E0 / 待核对

DenseNet · 2017

每层对所有前层输出做通道 concat，而非仅连接相邻层或做残差相加。Every layer concatenates feature maps from all preceding layers along the channel axis rather than summing adjacent residuals.

问题：深层网络中特征传播与梯度传递受阻，并会重复学习相似表示Question: Deep networks suffered from attenuated feature propagation and redundant re-learning of low-level representations.

机制：每层接收全部前层特征的拼接并以 growth rate 控制宽度Mechanism: Feed each layer the channel-wise concatenation of all prior layers within a block while bounding width via a fixed growth rate k.

Densely Connected Convolutional Networks · E0 / 待核对

ConvNeXt · 2022

7×7 depthwise spatial mixer 加 inverted bottleneck 的现代纯卷积块。Modernized pure ConvNet block pairing a 7×7 depthwise spatial mixer with an inverted channel bottleneck.

问题：Transformer 与 CNN 的比较混入了训练配方和层级设计差异Question: Comparisons between Vision Transformers and CNNs conflated self-attention with modern training recipes and macro-architecture design.

机制：以现代训练配方和大核 depthwise 卷积重构纯卷积层级主干Mechanism: Modernize a pure hierarchical ConvNet using Transformer-era training recipes and large-kernel depthwise convolutions.
