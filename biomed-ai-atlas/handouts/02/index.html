FCN · 2015

FC→Conv 的整图 dense prediction，加 pool3/pool4 分数级 skip fusion。Converts image-level FC layers into 1×1 convolutions for arbitrary-size dense prediction with pool3/pool4 skip score fusion.

问题：分类网络只能给图级标签，逐 patch 推理低效且边界粗Question: Classification CNNs produced only image-level labels; sliding-window patch classification was redundant and boundary-coarse.

机制：将分类 CNN 的全连接层卷积化，端到端输出像素级预测Mechanism: Transform fully connected layers into convolutions and append transposed-convolution upsampling for end-to-end pixel-wise training.

Fully Convolutional Networks for Semantic Segmentation · E0 / 待核对

U-Net · 2015

对称 U 形多尺度解码器与 encoder–decoder 同尺度 concat long skips。Symmetric U-shaped multi-scale decoder coupled with same-scale encoder–decoder channel concatenation long skips.

问题：FCN 上采样边界粗，生物医学标注稀缺Question: FCN upsampling lost fine boundary localization, while annotated biomedical datasets were small.

机制：对称 encoder-decoder 与同尺度长跳连联合语义和精确定位Mechanism: Pair a contracting semantic encoder with a symmetric expanding decoder linked by same-scale feature concatenation skips.

U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation · E0 / 待核对

Attention U-Net · 2018

由粗尺度 decoder gating signal 计算空间 attention coefficients，在拼接前逐位置筛选 encoder skip。Computes spatial soft-attention coefficients α ∈ [0,1] from coarse decoder gating signals to filter encoder skips prior to concatenation.

问题：U-Net 跳连把大量背景细节无差别传给 decoder，小器官易被淹没Question: Unfiltered U-Net skip connections pass cluttered background activations to the decoder, degrading small-organ false-positive rates.

机制：用 decoder gating 信号对 skip 特征做空间注意力筛选Mechanism: Gate high-resolution encoder skip features spatially using contextual gating signals from the coarser decoder stage.

Attention U-Net: Learning Where to Look for the Pancreas · E0 / 待核对

nnU-Net · 2021

dataset fingerprint 驱动的自配置、交叉验证与推理后处理闭环，而不是单一新算子。Dataset-fingerprint-driven self-configuration, 5-fold cross-validation ensembling, and post-processing loop rather than a new neural block.

问题：新数据集依赖大量专家手调，论文比较常被 pipeline 差异混淆Question: Segmentation performance was dominated by manual per-dataset tuning, confounding architectural comparisons across papers.

机制：根据数据集 fingerprint 自动配置预处理、网络、采样、训练与推理Mechanism: Automate preprocessing, network topology, patch/batch sizing, sampling, training schedule, and post-processing directly from dataset statistics.

nnU-Net: a self-configuring method for deep learning-based biomedical image segmentation · E0 / 待核对

Swin UNETR (SSL) · 2022

3D shifted-window hierarchy 与旋转、重建、对比联合自监督的 Swin UNETR。3D shifted-window hierarchical Swin encoder coupled to a U-shaped CNN decoder and pretrained with multi-task 3D self-supervision.

问题：平坦 UNETR token 序列昂贵且缺自然多尺度，3D 标注有限Question: Flat UNETR tokens lack multi-scale spatial pyramids and scale poorly to high-resolution 3D volumes under limited labels.

机制：层级 shifted-window Transformer 与 U 形解码器结合，并在 5050 个 CT 上自监督预训练Mechanism: Replace flat ViT with a 3D shifted-window Swin pyramid and pretrain on 5,050 unlabeled volumetric CT scans.

Self-Supervised Pre-Training of Swin Transformers for 3D Medical Image Analysis · E0 / 待核对
