Deep Autoencoder · 2006

逐层 RBM 生成式预训练初始化深层对称 encoder–decoder，再以全局重建误差微调非线性 bottleneck。Layer-by-layer Restricted Boltzmann Machine (RBM) generative pretraining initializing a deep symmetric encoder–decoder fine-tuned via global reconstruction error.

问题：PCA 与手工字典只能表达线性或预设结构Question: Linear PCA could not capture curved data manifolds, while deep non-linear autoencoders failed to optimize from random initialization.

机制：用深层 encoder-decoder 学非线性低维 latent 并重建输入Mechanism: Pretrain stacked RBMs greedily layer-by-layer, then unfold into a symmetric encoder–decoder fine-tuned by backpropagation.

GAN · 2014

生成器与判别器通过同一 value function 对抗优化，以数据驱动判别器替代固定重建损失。Generator and discriminator networks engage in a two-player minimax game, replacing hand-crafted pixel reconstruction losses with a learned adversarial critic.

问题：显式 likelihood 生成模型难训练且样本常过度平滑Question: Pixel-wise L1/L2 and maximum-likelihood generative models averaged over uncertain modes, producing blurry textures.

机制：generator 与 discriminator 进行对抗式分布学习Mechanism: Pit a generator G against an adversarial discriminator D trained to distinguish real samples from synthesized ones.

Generative Adversarial Nets · E0 / 待核对

DDPM · 2020

预设离散高斯前向链，加训练稳定的 epsilon/score 网络，再按学习到的随机逆链逐步采样。Defines a fixed discrete Markov Gaussian forward noising chain and trains a noise-prediction U-Net (ε_θ) across all noise scales for iterative reverse sampling.

问题：GAN min-max 训练不稳、mode coverage 差，VAE 高频模糊Question: GANs suffered from adversarial instability and mode dropping, while likelihood-based flows and VAEs struggled with sample sharpness.

机制：通过逐步高斯加噪并训练网络在各噪声尺度预测噪声来生成数据Mechanism: Destroy data structure gradually via a fixed Gaussian diffusion process and learn the reverse Markov transition via stable multi-scale L2 noise regression.

Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models · E0 / 待核对

PhyDiCT · 2026

在固定预训练三维扩散先验上交替施加投影观测约束与去噪更新；文本报告、几何和预训练权重都是显式条件。A frozen text-conditioned 3D diffusion prior is guided by differentiable projection consistency during inference; reports, geometry and pretrained weights are explicit conditions.

问题：少量投影无法唯一确定患者三维结构；通用生成先验可能与实际观测不一致。Question: Sparse projections leave unresolved depth; a generative prior may be inconsistent with the observations.

机制：把冻结的三维先验与可微成像观测结合，在推理阶段约束候选体。Mechanism: Constrain a frozen 3D prior with the differentiable image-formation model at inference.
