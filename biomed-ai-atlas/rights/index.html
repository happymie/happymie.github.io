实验服务Experiment service

公开实验使用官方预训练 ResNet-18，在浏览器本机计算。首次运行按需下载权重；图片保留在本机。The public experiment runs official pretrained ResNet-18 locally in your browser. Weights download on demand; images stay on your device.

访问统计Visit analytics

图谱记录页面浏览、模型与论文链接点击、交互使用和实验运行点击，用于了解学习内容的使用情况。统计从接入后开始累计，浏览次数并非独立访客人数。记录保留 90 天，详细报表仅供作者查看。The atlas records page views, model and paper link clicks, interactions and experiment run clicks to understand how learning content is used. Counts begin at activation; views are not unique visitors. Records are retained for 90 days, and detailed reports are available only to the author.

统计不接收图片、输入内容或搜索词，不使用追踪 Cookie 或持久访客标识；尊重浏览器的禁止追踪与全局隐私控制设置。Analytics do not receive images, input content or search terms, and use no tracking cookies or persistent visitor identifiers. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control preferences are respected.
