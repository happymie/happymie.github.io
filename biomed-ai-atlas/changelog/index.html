3.1.0 · 让架构、实验与专著连起来A connected atlas, studio and compendium

  • 演化年表按年份收拢，支持年代、家族与关键词筛选。A compact year index with era, family and keyword filters.
  • 卷积核可编辑并保存输出对照；残差梯度可逐层追踪；注意力支持完整矩阵、特征编辑和因果掩码。Editable kernels and pinned output comparisons, layer-by-layer gradient traces, and full attention matrices with editable features and causal masking.
  • 首页与原专著由同一模型注册表同步，保留旧章节并自动追加新模型。The homepage and original compendium share one model registry. Existing chapters stay in place and new models are appended automatically.
  • 公开产物包含页面所需文件与官方公开演示权重；真实模型支持本机推理、特征浏览和遮挡对照。Public artifacts include page dependencies and approved official demo weights. Real inference runs locally with feature exploration and occlusion comparisons.
  • 每日北京时间 01:00 检查新模型，通过来源和完整性核查后同步更新。New models are checked daily at 01:00 Asia/Shanghai and published together after source and completeness checks.
