以下数量均为构造的教学条件。每一步都可以用纸笔或简短程序独立复核。All quantities below are constructed teaching conditions. Verify each step independently by hand or with a short program.

先明确符号、数量与假设Define symbols, quantities, and assumptions
符号Symbol设定Setting含义与边界Meaning and assumptions
N10,000假设对象独立，且测试作用于这组目标人群。Assume independent people tested in the specified target population.
π1%目标人群中疾病比例。Disease prevalence in the target population.
Se90%患病条件下被判阳性的概率。Probability of a positive result conditional on disease.
Sp95%未患病条件下被判阴性的概率。Probability of a negative result conditional on no disease.
  1. 01

    先按真值分人群Partition by actual disease

    一万人中有 100 人患病、9900 人未患病。先按疾病状态计数，可以避免把“患病后测到阳性”误当成“阳性后患病”。Among 10,000 people, 100 have disease and 9,900 do not. Partitioning by actual disease prevents confusing a positive result given disease with disease given a positive result.

    N₊ = 10000×0.01 = 100; N₋ = 9900
  2. 02

    对两组分别应用测试条件Apply test characteristics within each group

    100 名患者中，真阳性 90、假阴性 10。9900 名非患者中，真阴性 9405、假阳性 495；5% 的假阳性率作用在更大的人群上。Among the 100 people with disease, there are 90 true positives and 10 false negatives. Among the 9,900 without disease, there are 9,405 true negatives and 495 false positives. The 5% false-positive rate acts on a much larger group.

    TP=90; FN=10; TN=9405; FP=495
  3. 03

    只在所有阳性结果中计算比例Condition on all positive results

    阳性结果共有 90+495=585 个，其中真正患病的有 90 个。因此阳性预测值为 15.38%，远低于敏感度 90%。There are 90+495=585 positive results, of which 90 have disease. Positive predictive value is 15.38%, much lower than sensitivity of 90%.

    PPV = 90 / 585 = 0.153846…
  4. 04

    写出同一个计算的贝叶斯形式Write the same computation using Bayes’ rule

    分子是“患病且阳性”的联合概率；分母由患病阳性与未患病阳性两条互斥路径相加得到。条件方向不能交换。The numerator is the joint probability of disease and a positive test. The denominator adds the mutually exclusive diseased-positive and non-diseased-positive paths. Conditional directions cannot be swapped.

    P(D|+) = Se·π / (Se·π + (1−Sp)(1−π))
  5. 05

    改变使用环境，而不是篡改测试性能Change the population rather than test characteristics

    若疾病比例提高到 10%，其余条件保持不变，则真阳性 900、假阳性 450，阳性预测值为 66.67%。同一敏感度与特异度下，使用人群改变仍会改变阳性结果的含义。At 10% prevalence with the other conditions unchanged, there are 900 true positives and 450 false positives, yielding PPV 66.67%. Changing the target population changes what a positive result means even when sensitivity and specificity are unchanged.

    PPV(π=0.10) = 900/(900+450) = 2/3
亲手复现Reproduce it yourself

改变一个条件，再检查结果Change one condition and verify the result

固定一万人与敏感度 90%，将患病率设为 0.1%、1%、10%，分别将特异度设为 95% 与 99%，制作六格计数表。Keep 10,000 people and sensitivity 90%. Cross prevalence levels 0.1%, 1%, and 10% with specificity 95% and 99%, producing a six-cell count table.

应该核对什么What to verify

  • 每格都满足 TP+FN+FP+TN=10000；0.1% 情景允许用期望计数的小数。Each cell must satisfy TP+FN+FP+TN=10,000; fractional expected counts are acceptable at 0.1% prevalence.
  • 在 1% 患病率、99% 特异度时，PPV=90/(90+99)=47.62%。At 1% prevalence and 99% specificity, PPV=90/(90+99)=47.62%.
  • 区分目标人群患病率与人为平衡测试集中的阳性比例。Distinguish target-population prevalence from the positive fraction in an artificially balanced test set.

这一课要理解什么What you will understand

能区分敏感度和阳性预测值，用基础患病率计算后验，并识别两类不确定性。Distinguish sensitivity from positive predictive value, calculate a posterior from prevalence and identify two kinds of uncertainty.

条件概率有方向Conditional probability has a direction

P(A|B) 表示已知 B 时 A 的概率，通常不等于 P(B|A)。敏感度问的是“患病者中有多少检测阳性”，阳性预测值问的是“检测阳性者中有多少患病”，分母已经不同。联合概率描述两件事同时发生，边缘概率则把其他可能情形加总。用一张人数表核对分母，比只背缩写更可靠。医学筛查中的病例比例、入组方式与检测阈值改变后，概率的含义和数值都可能变化。P(A|B) is the probability of A given B, and generally differs from P(B|A). Sensitivity asks what proportion of people with disease test positive. Positive predictive value asks what proportion of positive tests correspond to disease. Their denominators are different. A joint probability concerns events occurring together; a marginal probability sums over other possibilities. A table of patient counts makes those denominators visible. In screening, changes in prevalence, recruitment and the decision threshold can change both the interpretation and the values of the reported probabilities.

贝叶斯更新连接先验与证据Bayesian updating connects prior and evidence

贝叶斯公式把先验概率与检测结果连接起来。先验不是随意猜测，而是当前人群与信息下对事件频率或信念的表达。观测到证据后，用似然说明不同假设产生该证据的可能程度，再归一化得到后验。低患病率人群中，即使检测敏感度较高，假阳性人数仍可能超过真阳性人数。因此不能把“敏感度 90%”直接解释成“阳性者有 90% 概率患病”，还必须知道特异度和患病率。Bayes’ rule connects a prior probability with new evidence. A prior expresses the event frequency or belief appropriate to the population and information already available. The likelihood describes how probable the evidence is under each hypothesis; normalization produces the posterior. In a population with low disease prevalence, false positives may outnumber true positives even when sensitivity is high. Consequently, “90% sensitivity” does not mean that a person with a positive result has a 90% probability of disease. Specificity and prevalence are also required to answer that question.

不确定性从哪里来Where uncertainty comes from

数据不确定性来自噪声、模糊图像或标签分歧；模型不确定性与有限训练数据、参数估计和模型设定有关。这种区分有助于决定下一步：重拍检查、补充标注、收集新域数据，还是改进模型。softmax 最大值只是模型内部输出，不能自动测量这两类不确定性；未见过的输入也可能得到很高分数。应结合校准、外部分布检查和具体错误分析，评估概率在目标人群中的实际含义。Data uncertainty arises from noise, ambiguous images or disagreement in labels. Model uncertainty relates to limited training data, parameter estimation and model assumptions. The distinction helps identify a useful next action: acquire a better image, improve labels, collect examples from a new domain or revise the model. The largest softmax value is an internal output, not an automatic measurement of either uncertainty. Unfamiliar inputs can receive high scores. Calibration, distribution checks and analysis of specific errors are needed to understand what predicted probabilities mean in the intended population.

D 表示患病，+ 表示阳性；后验同时取决于敏感度、假阳性率和先验患病率。D denotes disease and + a positive result. The posterior depends on sensitivity, false-positive rate and prior prevalence.

一个纯教学的筛查算例A hypothetical screening calculation

假设 10,000 人中患病率为 1%，敏感度 90%，特异度 95%。100 名患者中约 90 人阳性，9,900 名非患者中约 495 人假阳性。因此阳性预测值为 90÷585，约 15.4%。这些数值仅用于展示计算，不是某个模型的性能。Assume 10,000 people, 1% prevalence, 90% sensitivity and 95% specificity. Approximately 90 of the 100 people with disease test positive, while 495 of the 9,900 people without disease are false positives. Positive predictive value is 90÷585, about 15.4%. These numbers illustrate arithmetic and do not describe a model benchmark.

再想深一步Take the idea further

预测概率不是因果效应，注意力权重或高置信分数也不能代替概率校准。A predicted probability is not a causal effect. Attention weights and high confidence scores do not substitute for calibration.
