  1. 01

    先做点积，而不是直接分配百分比Compute dot products before percentages

    查询与三个键的点积依次为 1、0、1。除以 √2 后得到分数 [0.7071,0,0.7071]；缩放来自键维度，而不是候选数量 3。Dot products with the three keys are 1, 0, and 1. Dividing by √2 gives scores [0.7071,0,0.7071]. Scaling uses key dimension, not the number of candidates.

    s = qKᵀ/√dₖ
  2. 02

    稳定归一化为一组共同竞争的权重Normalize jointly and stably

    对所有分数同时减去最大值再指数化，权重为 [0.4011, 0.1978, 0.4011]，总和为 1。单独对每个分数做 sigmoid 不会得到这组权重。Subtract the shared maximum before exponentiation. Weights are [0.4011, 0.1978, 0.4011], summing to 1. Applying sigmoid independently to scores does not produce these weights.

    a = softmax(s) = [0.401112…,0.197776…,0.401112…]
  3. 03

    用权重汇总值，而不是汇总键Aggregate values, not keys

    输出第一坐标为 2a₁+a₃=1.2033；第二坐标为 2a₂+a₃=0.7967。输出仍是一个二维向量，不是三个注意力权重本身。The first output coordinate is 2a₁+a₃=1.2033; the second is 2a₂+a₃=0.7967. The output is a two-dimensional vector, not the three attention weights themselves.

    o = aV = [1.203336…,0.796664…]
  4. 04

    遮掉一个位置，重新归一化其余位置Mask a position and renormalize

    把第三个位置的分数设为 −∞，其权重变为 0；前两个位置的权重变为 [0.6698,0.3302]。输出约为 [1.3395,0.6605]，不能只删除旧输出中的第三项而不改其余权重。Masking position 3 with −∞ gives it zero weight. The first two weights become [0.6698,0.3302], producing approximately [1.3395,0.6605]. Merely deleting the third term without renormalizing is incorrect.

    a(masked)=[0.669762…,0.330238…,0]
  5. 05

    解释这种机制能证明什么State what the mechanism establishes

    权重描述当前查询如何混合可见值向量；它不是某个医学因素的因果贡献。多头注意力会并行学习多组查询、键和值，然后组合各头输出。Weights describe how this query mixes visible value vectors; they are not causal contributions of medical factors. Multi-head attention learns several query–key–value projections in parallel and combines their outputs.

这一课要理解什么What you will understand

能解释 Q、K、V 的作用，沿形状读懂自注意力，并理解位置编码与计算成本。Explain Q, K and V, track self-attention shapes and understand position information and computational cost.

查询、键和值各做什么What queries, keys and values do

注意力把“向谁取信息”与“取到什么信息”分开。查询 Q 与键 K 比较，得到位置之间的匹配分数；归一化后，用这些权重汇总值 V。自注意力的三者都来自同一序列的可学习投影，交叉注意力则让查询来自一组输入，键和值来自另一组。分数依赖当前内容，因此同一个位置可以在不同样本中选择不同的信息来源。它不是把某个通道永久标成重要，而是一次前向计算中的动态加权。Attention separates where information is retrieved from what information is retrieved. Queries Q are compared with keys K to produce matching scores between positions. After normalization, those weights aggregate values V. In self-attention, all three are learned projections of the same sequence. In cross-attention, queries come from one input while keys and values come from another. The scores depend on current content, so a position can use different information sources for different samples. This is dynamic weighting in a forward pass, not a permanent declaration that one channel is important.

沿着形状理解计算Follow the computation through its shapes

若有 T 个词元，每个查询和键有 d 维，QK 的转置乘积会形成 T×T 的关系矩阵。除以 √d 可以调节点积分数的尺度，再按每个查询所在行做 softmax，得到和为一的权重。多头注意力用多组投影并行汇总信息，随后融合；它不保证每一头自动对应某种医学概念。标准稠密自注意力的关系矩阵随 T 的平方增长，三维图像切成大量词元后成本很高，因此出现窗口、分层或稀疏等设计。For T tokens with d-dimensional queries and keys, multiplying Q by the transpose of K produces a T×T relationship matrix. Dividing by √d adjusts the scale of dot-product scores. Row-wise softmax gives weights that sum to one for each query. Multi-head attention uses several projection groups in parallel and combines their outputs; individual heads are not guaranteed to correspond to medical concepts. Standard dense self-attention stores relationships that grow quadratically with T. Large three-dimensional images can generate many tokens, motivating windowed, hierarchical or sparse alternatives.

Transformer 还有位置与前馈网络A Transformer also needs position and feed-forward computation

仅做内容匹配，模型缺少词元顺序或空间坐标的明确提示，因此通常加入位置编码或位置偏置。Transformer 模块还包含逐词元前馈网络、残差连接和归一化，不能把整个架构等同于一张注意力热图。ViT 把图像切成块并投影为词元，医学分割模型则要把表示还原到空间输出。热图可以帮助检查信息流，但高权重不直接等于病因、临床证据或完整解释；要结合扰动、对照与任务评估阅读。Content matching alone does not explicitly supply sequence order or spatial coordinates, so architectures commonly add positional encodings or biases. A Transformer block also contains token-wise feed-forward computation, residual connections and normalization. The architecture cannot be reduced to an attention heatmap. ViT projects image patches into tokens, while segmentation systems must connect representations back to spatial predictions. Attention maps can help inspect information flow, but a high weight is not automatically a cause, clinical evidence or a complete explanation. Read them alongside perturbations, controls and task-level evaluation.

Q 与 K 决定检索权重，dₖ 是键向量长度，V 提供被汇总的信息；softmax 沿每个查询的候选键归一化。Q and K determine retrieval weights, dₖ is key-vector length and V supplies the aggregated information. Softmax normalizes over candidate keys for each query.

从图像块到关系矩阵From patches to a relationship matrix

把 224×224 图像切为 16×16 的互不重叠图像块，会得到 14×14=196 个词元。若再添加一个分类词元，单头完整自注意力的分数矩阵为 197×197，而不是 224×224。Splitting a 224×224 image into non-overlapping 16×16 patches yields 14×14=196 tokens. Adding a classification token gives 197 tokens, so a full single-head attention score matrix has shape 197×197 rather than 224×224.
