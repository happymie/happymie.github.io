切片独立划分，为何会让患者跨集合？Why independent slice splitting mixes patients across sets

以下数量均为构造的教学条件。每一步都可以用纸笔或简短程序独立复核。All quantities below are constructed teaching conditions. Verify each step independently by hand or with a short program.

先明确符号、数量与假设Define symbols, quantities, and assumptions
符号Symbol设定Setting含义与边界Meaning and assumptions
P2020 名合成患者，每人同样多切片。20 synthetic patients, each with the same number of slices.
m5同一患者的五张切片相关，不是五个独立患者。Five related slices per patient, not five independent patients.
q0.8每张切片独立地进入训练集的概率。Each slice independently enters training with probability 0.8.
1−q0.2这是伯努利划分，不是强制恰好 80 张的固定大小划分。This is a Bernoulli split, not a fixed-size split constrained to exactly 80 images.
  1. 01

    计算同一患者全部进入训练的概率Compute the all-training probability

    五张切片独立抽签，全部进入训练集的概率是 0.8⁵=0.32768。全部进入测试集的概率是 0.2⁵=0.00032。For five independent slice assignments, the probability all enter training is 0.8⁵=0.32768. The probability all enter test is 0.2⁵=0.00032.

    P(all train)=q⁵; P(all test)=(1−q)⁵
  2. 02

    用补集得到跨集合概率Use the complement to find mixing

    既不是全训练，也不是全测试，就意味着同一个患者同时出现在两个集合。跨集合概率是 1−0.32768−0.00032=0.672。Anything other than all-training or all-test places the same patient in both sets. The mixing probability is 1−0.32768−0.00032=0.672.

    P(mixed)=1−q⁵−(1−q)⁵=0.672
  3. 03

    用线性期望得到患者数Use linearity of expectation

    20 名患者中，预计有 13.44 名跨集合。这个数是重复随机划分的平均值；一次实际划分只能出现整数名患者。Among 20 patients, the expected number mixed across sets is 13.44. This is an average over repeated random splits; one realized split contains an integer number of mixed patients.

    E[mixed patients] = 20×0.672 = 13.44
  4. 04

    把划分单位改回患者Move the split unit back to patients

    先选择 16 名患者训练、4 名患者测试，再把每人的五张切片一起放入对应集合，可得到恰好 80 与 20 张切片，患者交集为空。归一化统计量也只在训练患者上估计。Choose 16 patients for training and 4 for test, then keep all five slices with each patient. This gives exactly 80 and 20 slices with no patient overlap. Estimate normalization statistics using training patients only.

    |patient(train) ∩ patient(test)| = 0
  5. 05

    不要把图像数当有效独立样本数Do not substitute image count for independent units

    患者级测试应按患者报告结果或按患者重采样估计区间。五张相似切片会增加观测数量，但不会自动把四个独立测试患者变成二十个。Report patient-level results or resample patients for uncertainty. Five related slices add observations, but do not turn four independent test patients into twenty.

亲手复现Reproduce it yourself

改变一个条件，再检查结果Change one condition and verify the result

生成 20 个患者 ID，每人复制五次；用固定随机种子重复伯努利切片划分 10000 次，再与患者分组划分对照。Generate 20 patient IDs repeated five times each. With a fixed seed, repeat Bernoulli slice splitting 10,000 times and compare with patient-group splitting.

应该核对什么What to verify

  • 混合患者数的重复均值应接近 13.44，不要求每次恰好等于此值。The repeated mean mixed-patient count should approach 13.44; individual splits need not equal it.
  • 分组划分每次患者交集都为空。Patient intersections must be empty in every grouped split.
  • 将固定大小图像划分与伯努利划分分开记录；两者的精确混合概率不同。Record fixed-size and Bernoulli image splits separately; their exact mixing probabilities differ.

这一课要理解什么What you will understand

能按预测场景选择划分单位，并把预处理、调参和最终测试放在正确的数据边界内。Choose split units for the intended prediction setting and place preprocessing, tuning and final testing within the correct data boundaries.

训练、验证、测试各有职责Training, validation and test sets have different jobs

训练集用于拟合参数；验证集用于选择超参数、阈值和停止时间；测试集用于对已经确定的流程进行最终评估。如果反复看测试结果再修改模型，测试集也参与了选择，不再是独立的最终检查。数据较少时，可在开发数据内做交叉验证，每折重新拟合完整流程，同时保留独立测试集。报告时写清患者数、检查数、划分规则和选择过程，不能只写一个随机种子或训练测试比例。The training set fits parameters. The validation set selects hyperparameters, decision thresholds and stopping times. The test set evaluates the completed procedure. Repeatedly inspecting test results and modifying the model turns that test set into part of model selection. With limited data, use cross-validation within development data, refitting the complete procedure in each fold while preserving an independent test set. Report patient counts, examination counts, splitting rules and selection steps. A random seed or a train-to-test ratio alone does not describe a reproducible evaluation design.

预测新患者，就按患者隔离Group by patient when predicting new patients

同一患者的切片、重复检查和随访具有相关性。若把切片随机分组，模型可能在训练中见过测试患者的相邻切片或稳定特征，评估的便不是面对新患者的能力。通常先按患者划分，再提取切片和执行增强；如多个患者共享家系或其他强依赖，也要考虑更高层分组。若目标是未来使用，还应增加时间划分；若目标是迁移到新医院，则需要按机构留出数据。切分方式应回答真实使用问题。Slices, repeat examinations and follow-up studies from one patient are correlated. Randomly splitting slices can expose the model to adjacent images or stable characteristics of a test patient during training. That does not estimate performance on a new patient. Usually split patients before extracting slices or applying augmentation, and consider higher-level grouping when families or other dependencies matter. A future-use setting may also require a temporal split; a new-hospital setting needs a site-held-out evaluation. Choose a split that answers the actual deployment question, rather than whichever split produces the highest score.

泄漏也会藏在预处理里Leakage can hide in preprocessing

标准化的均值与方差、缺失值填补、特征筛选和降维参数，都只能在当前训练折中学习，再应用于验证或测试。先用全体数据挑特征、再交叉验证，仍会泄漏。重采样和数据增强也应留在训练边界内；增强后的同源图像不能跨组。对时间预测，还要检查输入是否在预测时刻已经可用，例如用出院后才写入的字段预测入院风险，会把未来信息带进模型。用流水线固化这些顺序。Learn standardization statistics, imputation values, feature selection and dimensionality reduction only from the current training fold, then apply them to validation or test data. Selecting features on the full dataset before cross-validation still leaks information. Resampling and augmentation also belong inside the training boundary, and transformed versions of the same source image must not cross groups. In temporal prediction, verify that each input was available at the prediction time. A field recorded after discharge cannot fairly predict admission-time risk. A pipeline makes the fitting order explicit and repeatable.

P 表示患者 ID 集合；评估新患者时，三个集合两两不重叠。P denotes a set of patient IDs. For evaluation on new patients, all three sets must be pairwise disjoint.

2,000 张切片，不等于 2,000 位独立患者2,000 slices are not 2,000 independent patients

一个教学数据集有 100 位患者，每人 20 张切片。先把患者分成 70／15／15 位，再把各自的全部切片带入训练／验证／测试。分组之后才做增强，标准化参数只从训练组估计。A teaching dataset contains 100 patients with 20 slices each. Split patients into groups of 70, 15 and 15, then place all slices from each patient into the corresponding training, validation or test set. Apply augmentation afterward and estimate preprocessing statistics using training data alone.

再想深一步Take the idea further

标签分层只保持类别比例，不能自动阻止同一患者、同一次检查或重复图片跨组。Stratification preserves class proportions; it does not automatically prevent patient, examination or duplicate-image overlap.
