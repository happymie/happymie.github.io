以下数量均为构造的教学条件。每一步都可以用纸笔或简短程序独立复核。All quantities below are constructed teaching conditions. Verify each step independently by hand or with a short program.

先明确符号、数量与假设Define symbols, quantities, and assumptions
符号Symbol设定Setting含义与边界Meaning and assumptions
x₀[1,−1]两个数构成的合成干净信号。A synthetic clean signal with two coordinates.
ε[0.5,−0.5]固定噪声实现，便于精确复核；并非声称仅这两个数服从经验高斯分布。A fixed noise realization for exact checking, not an empirical Gaussian claim from two numbers.
ᾱₜ0.64累积信号系数，而不是单一步的 α。The cumulative signal coefficient, not a single-step α.
ε̂[0.4,−0.4]人为给定的有误差噪声估计，另与已知真实噪声对照。A supplied imperfect noise estimate, compared with the known true noise.
  1. 01

    先分清信号与噪声幅度Separate signal and noise amplitudes

    √ᾱₜ=0.8，√(1−ᾱₜ)=0.6。系数的平方和为 1；它们本身相加不是 1。把累积系数误当单步系数，会改变整个噪声水平。√ᾱₜ=0.8 and √(1−ᾱₜ)=0.6. Their squares sum to 1; the amplitudes themselves do not. Confusing cumulative and single-step coefficients changes the noise level.

    xₜ = √ᾱₜ x₀ + √(1−ᾱₜ) ε
  2. 02

    直接生成当前时刻的含噪信号Generate the noisy signal at this time

    第一坐标为 0.8×1+0.6×0.5=1.1，第二坐标为 −1.1。无需逐次模拟此前每一步，就能从 x₀ 直接构造这个时刻的训练输入。The first coordinate is 0.8×1+0.6×0.5=1.1 and the second is −1.1. This constructs a training input at the selected time directly from x₀ without simulating every preceding step.

    xₜ = [1.1,−1.1]
  3. 03

    用已知噪声验证代数逆变换Verify the inverse algebra with known noise

    若把真实 ε 代入，(xₜ−0.6ε)/0.8 恰好还原 [1,−1]。这只是已知噪声的解析校验，不代表真实模型已经能从任意含噪图像准确恢复。Using the true ε, (xₜ−0.6ε)/0.8 exactly recovers [1,−1]. This is an algebraic check with known noise, not evidence that a trained model recovers arbitrary noisy images.

    x̂₀(ε̂=ε) = [1,−1]
  4. 04

    让噪声估计产生明确误差Introduce a specified noise-estimation error

    使用 ε̂=[0.4,−0.4]，估计信号为 [1.075,−1.075]，坐标误差 ±0.075，均方误差 0.005625。这个误差可由缩放系数独立推导。Using ε̂=[0.4,−0.4] gives [1.075,−1.075], coordinate errors ±0.075, and MSE 0.005625. The error is independently determined by the scaling coefficient.

    x̂₀−x₀ = √((1−ᾱₜ)/ᾱₜ)·(ε−ε̂)
  5. 05

    解释时间条件为什么必要Explain why time conditioning matters

    当 ᾱₜ=0.04，噪声估计误差会被约 4.899 倍地放大；相同误差 0.1 对应信号误差约 0.490。因此模型必须知道当前噪声水平，训练目标和采样过程也要与时间参数一致。At ᾱₜ=0.04, noise-estimation error is amplified by approximately 4.899, so an error of 0.1 creates a signal error near 0.490. The model must know the current noise level, and training and sampling must use compatible time parameterizations.

改变一个条件，再检查结果Change one condition and verify the result

固定 x₀ 与 ε，在 ᾱₜ=0.04、0.25、0.64、0.9 下，比较真实噪声与偏差 0.1 的估计噪声两种逆变换。Fix x₀ and ε. At ᾱₜ=0.04, 0.25, 0.64, and 0.9, compare inversion using true noise versus an estimate with error 0.1.

应该核对什么What to verify

  • 使用真实噪声时，每个 ᾱₜ 都应在浮点误差内还原 x₀。True-noise inversion should recover x₀ within floating-point error at every ᾱₜ.
  • 有误差时，MSE 应等于 ((1−ᾱₜ)/ᾱₜ)×0.01。With the specified error, MSE must equal ((1−ᾱₜ)/ᾱₜ)×0.01.
  • 不要把这次解析逆变换称为一次真实扩散采样或模型性能测量。Do not label this algebraic inversion as an actual diffusion sampling run or a model-performance measurement.

能比较自编码器、VAE、GAN 和扩散模型，区分无条件采样与有测量约束的条件生成。Compare autoencoders, VAEs, GANs and diffusion models, and distinguish unconditional sampling from measurement-conditioned generation.

自编码器与变分自编码器Autoencoders and variational autoencoders

普通自编码器先把输入编码为潜在表示，再解码重建输入；瓶颈或其他约束促使表示捕捉结构。但训练过一个重建器，并不保证从任意随机潜变量都能生成合理样本。VAE 进一步定义潜变量先验和概率解码器，用近似后验学习每个输入对应的潜在分布。训练同时兼顾重建与近似后验和先验之间的关系，因而可以从先验采样后解码。潜在维度、分布假设和目标权重都会改变生成细节与表示结构。An ordinary autoencoder encodes an input into a latent representation and decodes it to reconstruct the input. A bottleneck or other constraint encourages structure, but training a reconstructor does not guarantee meaningful samples from arbitrary random latent values. A variational autoencoder additionally defines a latent prior, a probabilistic decoder and an approximate posterior for each input. Training balances reconstruction with the relationship between the approximate posterior and prior, enabling prior samples to be decoded. Latent dimensions, distribution assumptions and objective weights affect both generated detail and representation structure.

GAN 用对抗目标学习生成GANs learn through an adversarial objective

GAN 同时训练生成器和判别器：生成器把随机潜变量变成样本，判别器区分真实样本与生成样本，两者通过不同目标相互推动。判别器判断的是与训练分布的相似性，而不是诊断是否正确。训练还可能出现模式坍塌，即生成结果只覆盖少数样貌，遗漏真实分布中的多样性。因此视觉锐利度只是评价的一部分，还要看覆盖范围、条件符合程度与下游任务影响。架构、损失和训练平衡需要一起理解。A GAN trains a generator and discriminator together. The generator maps random latent variables to samples; the discriminator distinguishes real from generated examples. Their different objectives drive an adversarial learning process. The discriminator learns distributional distinctions, not diagnostic correctness. Training can also suffer from mode collapse, where generated examples cover only a few appearances and omit important diversity. Visual sharpness is therefore only one aspect of evaluation. Examine coverage, agreement with conditioning information and downstream effects, while interpreting architecture, objectives and the balance between the two training processes together.

扩散模型学习逐步去噪Diffusion models learn progressive denoising

扩散训练先按已知噪声日程逐渐扰动真实数据，再让网络根据当前噪声水平学习去噪方向；常见 DDPM 目标是预测加入的噪声。生成时从噪声出发，通过学习到的反向步骤逐渐形成样本，而不是把某个输入简单模糊再还原。训练步数、采样步数和采样器各有不同含义。潜在扩散把主要去噪计算放在压缩表示中，再由解码器回到图像空间，以改变计算成本；压缩也会影响可以保留的细节。Diffusion training perturbs real data according to a known noise schedule and teaches a network a denoising direction at each noise level. A common DDPM objective predicts the added noise. Generation begins with noise and follows learned reverse steps to form a sample; it is not simply blurring and restoring one supplied input. Training steps, sampling steps and the sampling algorithm have distinct meanings. Latent diffusion performs most denoising in a compressed representation before decoding into image space, changing computational cost. That compression also affects which details can be retained.

条件生成要检查条件与结果是否一致Conditioning requires agreement between inputs and outputs

无条件生成学习整体数据分布；条件生成则依赖文本、类别、分割图或实际测量，学习给定条件下可能的输出。医学重建中的低剂量投影或欠采样 MRI 测量提供了比“生成一张像 MRI 的图”更具体的约束。评价需要分别检查外观、解剖与病灶、测量一致性和任务性能。多次采样可以展示不同可能结果，但多样性本身不等于校准的不确定性。把同一患者的真实测量与输出对照，才能知道生成过程改变了哪些信息。Unconditional generation models an overall data distribution. Conditional generation uses text, classes, segmentation maps or measurements to model possible outputs given that information. Low-dose projections or undersampled MRI measurements provide more specific constraints than merely requesting an MRI-like image. Evaluate appearance, anatomy and lesions, measurement consistency and task performance separately. Multiple samples reveal alternative outputs, but diversity alone is not calibrated uncertainty. Comparing the output with the same patient’s actual measurements helps identify which information the generation procedure preserved, inferred or changed.

关键公式The key equation

把机制写成公式THE MECHANISM IN AN EQUATION

xt=αˉt x0+1−αˉt ϵ,ϵ∼N(0,I)x_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon,\qquad\epsilon\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)xt​=αˉt​​x0​+1−αˉt​​ϵ,ϵ∼N(0,I)

这是 DDPM 前向加噪的闭式表达：x₀ 是数据，xₜ 是第 t 个噪声水平的样本，ᾱₜ 控制信号保留量；网络再学习对应的去噪任务。This is the DDPM forward-noising expression: x₀ is data, xₜ is its noisy version at level t and ᾱₜ controls retained signal. The network learns a corresponding denoising task.

同样生成 MRI，任务可能完全不同Generating MRI can mean different tasks

从随机噪声产生一张脑 MRI 外观的图像，是无条件合成；给定该患者的欠采样测量再恢复图像，是条件重建。后者必须核查结果与测量的相容性及病灶保留，不能只凭图像“更清晰”判断成功。Sampling a brain-MRI-like image from random noise is unconditional synthesis. Recovering an image from one patient’s undersampled measurements is conditional reconstruction. For the latter, assess agreement with those measurements and preservation of lesions, rather than judging success only by apparent sharpness.

再想深一步Take the idea further

生成图像的逼真程度、病例多样性和对实际测量的忠实程度是不同属性，需要分别检查。Visual realism, case diversity and fidelity to actual measurements are different properties and require separate evaluation.
