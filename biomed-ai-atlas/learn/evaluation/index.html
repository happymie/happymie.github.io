敏感度与阳性预测值相同吗？Are sensitivity and positive predictive value the same?

查看推理Reveal the reasoning

不同。敏感度以实际阳性为分母，阳性预测值以预测阳性为分母。No. Sensitivity conditions on actual positives; positive predictive value conditions on predicted positives.

  1. 01

    先检查总数与评价单位Check totals and evaluation units

    40+20+10+130=200；阳性比例为 60/200=30%，预测阳性共有 50。若一个患者贡献多张相关图像，这张矩阵的单位需要另外说明。40+20+10+130=200, with actual prevalence 60/200=30% and 50 predicted positives. If a patient contributes multiple related images, the unit represented by this matrix must be stated.

    TP+FN=60; FP+TN=140; TP+FP=50
  2. 02

    分别以真实阳性与真实阴性为分母Condition on actual positives and negatives

    敏感度为 40/60=66.67%，特异度为 130/140=92.86%。这两个分母不同，都不是 200；它们分别回答漏掉多少患者与排除多少非患者。Sensitivity is 40/60=66.67% and specificity is 130/140=92.86%. The denominators differ and neither is 200. They describe finding positives and excluding negatives.

    Se=0.666667; Sp=0.928571
  3. 03

    再以预测阳性为分母Now condition on predicted positives

    精确率为 40/(40+10)=80%。F1 为 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN)=80/110=72.73%；它不是精确率与敏感度的算术平均。Precision is 40/(40+10)=80%. F1 is 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN)=80/110=72.73%; it is not the arithmetic mean of precision and sensitivity.

    Precision=0.8; F1=0.727273
  4. 04

    看总体正确率，同时保留类别信息Inspect accuracy while retaining class information

    总体准确率为 (40+130)/200=85%。若只报告 85%，无法知道阳性敏感度其实只有 66.67%。一个阈值的混淆矩阵也不足以重建完整 ROC 或 PR 曲线。Accuracy is (40+130)/200=85%. Reporting only 85% hides sensitivity of 66.67%. A confusion matrix at one threshold also cannot reconstruct a complete ROC or PR curve.

    Accuracy=0.85
  5. 05

    把校准作为独立问题Treat calibration as a separate question

    另设三个等样本量的概率箱，平均预测为 [0.2,0.6,0.9]，真实阳性率为 [0.1,0.5,0.8]；分箱 ECE 为 0.1。这是另一组构造示例：分类排序、阈值决策和概率校准需要分别检查。In a separate construction, three equally sized bins have mean predictions [0.2,0.6,0.9] and observed rates [0.1,0.5,0.8], giving binned ECE 0.1. Ranking, threshold decisions, and probability calibration require separate checks.

    ECE = (|0.2−0.1|+|0.6−0.5|+|0.9−0.8|)/3 = 0.1

这一课要理解什么What you will understand

能从混淆矩阵计算指标，解释阈值与不平衡的影响，并区分判别能力和校准。Calculate metrics from a confusion matrix, explain thresholds and imbalance, and distinguish discrimination from calibration.

从人数开始理解指标Begin with counts

二分类的混淆矩阵包含真阳性、假阳性、真阴性和假阴性。敏感度关注实际阳性被发现的比例，特异度关注实际阴性被排除的比例，精确率则关注预测阳性中真实阳性的比例。类别极不平衡时，总预测多数类也可能得到很高准确率，因此要保留人数与分母。对医学任务还应先明确是患者级、检查级还是病灶级统计；把同一患者的大量切片当作独立对象，会改变指标与区间估计的含义。A binary confusion matrix contains true positives, false positives, true negatives and false negatives. Sensitivity measures how many actual positives are detected. Specificity measures how many actual negatives are correctly excluded. Precision measures how many predicted positives are true positives. With severe imbalance, always predicting the majority class can achieve high accuracy, so retain counts and denominators. Also specify whether evaluation is at patient, examination or lesion level. Treating many slices from one patient as independent observations changes both the metric interpretation and the validity of uncertainty estimates.

排序、阈值与任务成本Ranking, thresholds and task costs

ROC 曲线在不同阈值下画敏感度与假阳性率，AUROC 总结排序能力；它不指定实际使用阈值。精确率—召回率曲线更直接展示阳性预测质量与检出率，但也受患病率影响。选择阈值需要结合漏诊与误报的代价、后续检查容量和使用人群，在开发数据上确定后再测试。分割还要结合 Dice、边界误差和小病灶漏检等任务指标；不同指标衡量不同损失，不能用一个平均值替代具体的临床目标。A ROC curve plots sensitivity against false-positive rate across thresholds. AUROC summarizes ranking ability without selecting an operating threshold. A precision–recall curve shows the trade-off between positive prediction quality and detection, while depending on prevalence. Choose a threshold using development data, considering missed cases, false alarms, follow-up capacity and the intended population. Segmentation also needs task-appropriate measures such as Dice, boundary error and missed small lesions. Metrics capture different costs; one aggregate number cannot replace a clearly defined clinical objective and an explicit operating point.

分数能排序，也要检查概率是否可信Good ranking does not guarantee calibrated probabilities

校准问的是：在预测概率接近 0.8 的一组样本中，真实阳性比例是否也接近 0.8。可靠性图把预测分组后比较平均概率与实际比例，Brier 分数和对数损失则从不同角度评价概率。温度缩放等后处理需要在独立开发数据上拟合，不能拿测试标签校准后又在同一测试集报最终结果。置信区间应匹配独立采样单位，常按患者重采样；还要分人群和机构检查，并在新的患病率或设备环境下重新评估。Calibration asks whether groups receiving probabilities near 0.8 contain approximately 80% positive outcomes. Reliability diagrams compare average predicted probabilities with observed frequencies; Brier score and log loss evaluate probabilistic predictions in different ways. Post-processing methods such as temperature scaling must be fitted using separate development data, not fitted and finally evaluated on the same test labels. Confidence intervals should respect the independent sampling unit, often through patient-level resampling. Examine population and site subgroups, and reassess probabilities when prevalence, devices or acquisition settings change.

TP、FN、FP 是计数，pᵢ 是阳性概率，yᵢ∈{0,1} 是真实标签；前两项评价阈值后的决策，后一项评价概率。TP, FN and FP are counts; pᵢ is a positive probability and yᵢ∈{0,1} the label. The first two assess thresholded decisions; Brier score assesses probabilities.

99% 准确率也可能没有检出能力99% accuracy can coexist with no positive detection

在一个纯教学的 1,000 人数据集中，有 10 位阳性。若模型全部预测阴性，准确率是 990÷1,000=99%，敏感度却是 0÷10=0%。精确率此时没有预测阳性作分母，应明确所用软件对零分母的处理。In a hypothetical dataset of 1,000 people with 10 positive cases, predicting everyone as negative gives accuracy 990÷1,000=99% but sensitivity 0÷10=0%. Precision has no predicted-positive denominator here, so state how the implementation handles the undefined value.
