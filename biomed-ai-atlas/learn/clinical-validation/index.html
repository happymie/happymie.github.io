从条件到结果，完整推演A complete derivation, from assumptions to result

90/100 的敏感度，包含多少不确定性？How uncertain is sensitivity of 90/100?

以下数量均为构造的教学条件。每一步都可以用纸笔或简短程序独立复核。All quantities below are constructed teaching conditions. Verify each step independently by hand or with a short program.

先明确符号、数量与假设Define symbols, quantities, and assumptions
符号Symbol设定Setting含义与边界Meaning and assumptions
n₊,TP100,90外部中心 100 名参考标准阳性的假设独立患者，检出 90 名。A hypothetical external cohort of 100 independent reference-positive patients, 90 detected.
n₋,TN200,190另有 200 名独立参考阴性患者，正确排除 190 名。Another 200 independent reference-negative patients, 190 correctly excluded.
z1.96采用二项比例的近似 95% Wilson 区间。Uses an approximate 95% Wilson interval for a binomial proportion.
τ预先冻结frozen模型、预处理和阈值在外部测试前冻结。Model, preprocessing, and threshold are frozen before external testing.
  1. 01

    按参考状态分别报告结果Report results by reference status

    敏感度为 90/100=90%，特异度为 190/200=95%。这里是为手算设定的病例与对照数量；其 1/3 阳性比例不能直接当目标使用人群患病率。Sensitivity is 90/100=90% and specificity is 190/200=95%. Case and control counts are constructed for calculation; their positive fraction of 1/3 is not automatically deployment prevalence.

    Se=0.9; Sp=0.95
  2. 02

    选择符合二项比例的区间Choose an interval for a binomial proportion

    令 p̂=0.9，Wilson 分母为 1+z²/n，中心为 (p̂+z²/(2n))/(1+z²/n)。它不是简单地把成功数写成“90±某个百分比”。With p̂=0.9, the Wilson denominator is 1+z²/n and center is (p̂+z²/(2n))/(1+z²/n). This is not a symmetric percentage attached directly to the count 90.

    c = (p̂+z²/(2n))/(1+z²/n)
  3. 03

    算出区间宽度Calculate the interval width

    半宽按 z√(p̂(1−p̂)/n+z²/(4n²))/(1+z²/n) 计算，敏感度区间约为 [82.56%, 94.48%]。点估计相同不意味着精度相同。The half-width is z√(p̂(1−p̂)/n+z²/(4n²))/(1+z²/n), giving sensitivity interval approximately [82.56%, 94.48%]. Equal point estimates do not imply equal precision.

    Wilson₉₅%(90/100) ≈ [0.82563,0.94477]
  4. 04

    提高独立样本数，而不是复制图像Increase independent sample count, not duplicated images

    若换成 1000 名独立阳性患者中检出 900 名，点估计仍是 90%，Wilson 区间会缩窄至约 [87.98%,91.71%]。同一患者复制十次不满足这种独立性假设。Detecting 900 of 1,000 independent positive patients still gives 90%, with Wilson interval narrowing to about [87.98%,91.71%]. Repeating the same patient ten times does not satisfy this independence assumption.

  5. 05

    把临床迁移问题写成预先定义的检查Prespecify checks for clinical transfer

    分别报告设备、中心、年龄等子组的样本数与区间；病例混合改变会影响总体指标。若按中心聚类或按患者重复测量，应使用相应的分组重采样或层次分析，而非套用独立二项区间。Report subgroup counts and intervals for device, site, age, and other prespecified factors. Case-mix shifts affect aggregate metrics. Site clustering or repeated measures require grouped resampling or hierarchical analysis rather than an independent-binomial interval.

亲手复现Reproduce it yourself

改变一个条件，再检查结果Change one condition and verify the result

实现 Wilson 区间，对 9/10、90/100、900/1000 分别计算；然后列出本研究患者与中心层面的依赖关系。Implement Wilson intervals for 9/10, 90/100, and 900/1000. Then document patient-level and site-level dependencies in the proposed study.

应该核对什么What to verify

  • 三者点估计相同，区间随独立样本数增加而缩窄。Point estimates are equal, while intervals narrow with more independent cases.
  • 0/n 与 n/n 的 Wilson 区间仍有非零宽度，不能宣称风险恰为零。Wilson intervals for 0/n and n/n still have nonzero width; zero observed failures does not establish zero risk.
  • 外部结果不用于选择最佳阈值；如需重新校准，应使用新的适配集并重新留出测试。Do not choose a threshold using external test outcomes. Recalibration requires a separate adaptation set and a fresh held-out test.

这一课要理解什么What you will understand

能制定从内部验证到外部、前瞻性评估的路线，并区分预测性能与临床获益。Plan a path from internal validation to external and prospective evaluation, and distinguish prediction performance from clinical benefit.

先固定适用问题与参考标准Fix the intended use and reference standard

医学 AI 的证据从明确用途开始：服务谁、在哪个流程节点、使用哪些当时可获得的信息、输出给谁，以及输出会触发什么行动。参考标准可能来自病理、随访或专家标注，其误差和观察时间都会影响标签。回顾性纳入已经完成检查的人群，未必代表未来真正使用系统的人群。预先记录纳入排除、缺失数据、样本选择和分析方案，再报告模型的完整流程，才能理解一个指标究竟回答了哪一个医学问题。Evidence begins with intended use: who the system serves, where it enters the workflow, which information is available at that moment, who receives the output and what action follows. A reference standard may use pathology, follow-up or expert annotation, each with its own errors and observation window. A retrospective cohort of people who already completed testing may not represent future users. Record eligibility, missing-data handling, sampling and the analysis plan before evaluation, and report the full modeling procedure. Only then is it possible to understand which medical question a performance estimate answers.

外部验证检验可以迁移到哪里External validation tests where performance transfers

内部验证检查同一开发来源下的表现；外部验证使用与开发数据独立、并在机构、时间或人群等方面具有相关差异的数据。更换一个随机种子，不会变成外部验证。测试前应固定模型、预处理和阈值，报告外部队列特征、适用范围与分组表现。若使用外部数据重新校准或微调，应把更新步骤与后续独立评估分开。域偏移不仅是图像颜色改变，也可能是患病率、扫描流程、疾病谱或参考标准改变。Internal validation assesses performance within a development source. External validation uses data independent of model development, with relevant differences in institution, time or population. Changing a random seed does not create external validation. Fix the model, preprocessing and threshold before testing, and describe the external cohort, scope and subgroup results. If external data is used for recalibration or fine-tuning, separate that update from subsequent independent evaluation. Domain shift can involve prevalence, acquisition workflows, disease spectrum or reference standards, not merely image appearance.

从预测表现到真实工作流程From predictions to a real workflow

离线指标说明模型对已有样本的预测质量，不能直接推出使用后能改善结局。前瞻性评估按实际时间顺序收集数据；静默运行可以先观察技术与分布问题，而不把结果交给临床决策。进一步研究还要评估人与系统的协作、误报负担、时间成本和可能的临床获益。上线后保留版本、监测数据漂移和错误、建立升级与回退流程。TRIPOD+AI 帮助完整报告预测模型研究，但遵循报告清单本身不等于完成了有效性或临床效益证明。Offline metrics describe predictions on available examples; they do not directly establish better outcomes after use. Prospective evaluation collects data in actual temporal order. Silent deployment can first examine technical and distribution issues without exposing predictions to clinical decisions. Further studies assess human–system interaction, false-alarm burden, time costs and possible clinical benefit. Maintain versions, monitor drift and errors, and define update and rollback procedures. TRIPOD+AI supports complete reporting of prediction-model research, but following a reporting checklist does not itself establish validity or clinical benefit.

为胸片分诊写一条验证路线Plan validation for radiograph triage

先在开发医院完成患者级内部验证，冻结模型与分诊阈值；再到独立医院检验敏感度、误报量与校准；随后静默前瞻性运行，记录时延、缺失输入和工作量。若研究问题是缩短诊疗时间，还需要针对工作流程与结局另设评价。Complete patient-level internal validation at the development hospital, then freeze the model and triage threshold. Test sensitivity, false-alarm volume and calibration at an independent hospital. Next, run prospectively in silent mode to measure latency, missing inputs and workload. A claim about shorter time to care requires a separate evaluation of workflow and outcomes.

再想深一步Take the idea further

外部验证通过只支持已研究的数据与场景；临床获益、监管要求和其他场景的适用性需要各自的证据。Successful external validation supports the settings studied. Clinical benefit, regulatory requirements and use in other settings each require their own evidence.
