来源与编辑标准Sources and editorial standards

从论文结构到实验结果，每项信息都保留可核查的出处。From paper architectures to experiment results, every entry keeps inspectable sources.

原图与阅读辅助Original figures and reading guides

模型默认展示原论文架构图，并链接作者图注和原文。辅助动态图用于逐步阅读计算机制，与原图并列标注。Model pages open with the original paper figure and link to its caption and source. Animated guides support step-by-step mechanism reading and are labeled separately.

成绩与持续更新Results and ongoing updates

基准成绩记录数据集、划分、指标、单位与评价协议。每日 01:00（北京时间）检查新模型；原图、章节和来源完整后，首页、专著与搜索一起更新。Benchmarks record dataset, split, metric, unit and evaluation protocol. New models are checked daily at 01:00 Asia/Shanghai.
