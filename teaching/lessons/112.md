Decipher-MR 明确采用 8³ patch 的 3D ViT-Base、DINOv2/iBOT 视觉预训练、PubMedBERT MLM，再做器官感知的 MRI-report CLIP 对齐。

3D MRI vision–language foundation model combining an 8³-patch 3D ViT-Base (pretrained via 3D DINOv2/iBOT) with PubMedBERT MLM and organ-aware MRI–report CLIP alignment.

## 1. 输入、输出与学习目标

- 原模型输入：单个 3D MRI series 与同 study 的器官级报告文本 / 3D MRI series volume + paired study-level organ-parsed radiology report text
- 原模型输出：512 维共享 MRI—文本 embedding、冻结视觉特征及轻量下游 decoder 输出 / 512-D shared 3D MRI–text embedding and frozen 3D visual features for lightweight downstream decoders
