# 068 · GradICON

[教学网站](https://happymie.github.io/teaching/#model=68) · [返回覆盖表](../docs/COVERAGE.md) · [原图谱结构与数学](https://happymie.github.io/biomed-ai-atlas/#/model/architecture_model_068_gradicon)

**实现状态：部分机制演示 / Mechanism demo**

通过双向预测与复合映射 Jacobian 的 gradient inverse-consistency 正则获得近似微分同胚。

Enforces diffeomorphic regularity without tuning sensitive multi-scale smoothness weights by penalizing the Frobenius norm of the composed forward–backward Jacobian deviation from identity.

## 1. 输入、输出与学习目标

- 原模型输入：图像 A 与图像 B / Ordered pairs (I^A, I^B) and (I^B, I^A) fed symmetrically to a registration network
- 原模型输出：近似互逆、规则的双向配准映射 Phi_AB 与 Phi_BA / Approximately diffeomorphic, inverse-consistent forward map Φ^AB and backward map Φ^BA
- 分辨原模型的必要计算机制、本地缩小配置和没有覆盖的原训练阶段。
