**实现状态：独立论文结构或方程实现 / Paper port**

通过 J-invariant 遮挡使单幅 noisy image 同时提供输入与 held-out 监督目标。

Formalizes single-image self-supervised denoising via J-invariant functions whose prediction for pixel subset J does not depend on the observed noisy values in J.

## 1. 输入、输出与学习目标

- 原模型输入：单幅含噪图像 y / Single noisy image y (no paired second scan and no clean ground truth)
- 原模型输出：由多次 J-invariant 预测聚合的去噪图像 x_hat / Denoised image x̂ predicted from J-invariant masked neighborhoods
- 分辨原模型的必要计算机制、本地缩小配置和没有覆盖的原训练阶段。

**已知差异 / Limitations**

- Deterministic zero replacement and a compact U-Net; full J-invariant inference needs nine forward passes.
- Synthetic teaching data only; no clinical validity or published benchmark is claimed.
