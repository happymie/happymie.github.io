**实现状态：复用上游实现 / Upstream adapter**

以 GPU、ReLU、dropout 和深卷积组合首次规模化训练 ImageNet 级深 CNN。

First large-scale ImageNet-grade deep CNN trained end-to-end by combining dual-GPU parallelism, non-saturating ReLU activations, dropout regularization, and stacked convolutions.

**已知差异 / Limitations**

- Random initialization and synthetic data; no original benchmark result or pretrained capability is reproduced.
- Torchvision uses its documented AlexNet variant; adaptive pooling 1x1 and classifier width 128 replace the 6x6/4096 head.
