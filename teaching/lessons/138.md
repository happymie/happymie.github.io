**实现状态：原版学习路径，尚无 CPU 适配器 / Source route**

在固定预训练三维扩散先验上交替施加投影观测约束与去噪更新；文本报告、几何和预训练权重都是显式条件。

A frozen text-conditioned 3D diffusion prior is guided by differentiable projection consistency during inference; reports, geometry and pretrained weights are explicit conditions.

## 1. 输入、输出与学习目标

- 原模型输入：稀疏 X 光、采集几何、文本报告条件与预训练 MedSyn 权重 / Sparse X-rays, acquisition geometry, a report condition and pretrained MedSyn weights
- 原模型输出：与观测相容的三维肺部 CT 候选体；仍需验证患者特异性与域外可靠性 / A candidate 3D lung CT volume; patient-specific fidelity and external reliability still require validation
- 分辨原模型的必要计算机制、本地缩小配置和没有覆盖的原训练阶段。
- 从数据、损失、梯度到日志完成可检查的学习记录，保留失败与不确定性。

## 3. 尚未完成的实现

The official PhyDiCT inference source is available and pinned. Its pretrained MedSyn 3D diffusion prior, report embedding, calibrated projection geometry, LPIPS guidance, and CUDA-oriented sampling have not been adapted or executed locally. The synthetic database supports source study only; it is not a PhyDiCT reconstruction experiment.
