W-MSA 与 SW-MSA 交替实现局部高效注意力和跨窗连接，并以 patch merging 形成层次金字塔。

Alternates regular Windowed Multi-Head Self-Attention (W-MSA) with Shifted-Window Attention (SW-MSA) across a patch-merging multi-scale pyramid.
