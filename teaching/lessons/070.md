# 070 · DiffPose

[教学网站](https://happymie.github.io/teaching/#model=70) · [返回覆盖表](../docs/COVERAGE.md) · [原图谱结构与数学](https://happymie.github.io/biomed-ai-atlas/#/model/architecture_model_070_diffpose)

**实现状态：部分机制演示 / Mechanism demo**

合成 DRR 训练的 pose initializer 与可微 X-ray renderer 驱动的 SE(3) 测试时优化闭环结合。

Couples a patient-specific SE(3) pose initializer (trained self-supervised on synthetic DRRs) with test-time gradient optimization through a differentiable X-ray ray-casting renderer.

## 1. 输入、输出与学习目标

- 原模型输入：术前 CT 体 V、术中 2D X-ray y、投影标定参数 / Preoperative 3D CT volume V, intraoperative 2D X-ray fluoroscopy image y, and C-arm intrinsic calibration
- 原模型输出：CT 到 X-ray 的刚体 SE(3) 位姿 T_hat / Sub-millimeter 6-DoF rigid pose T̂ ∈ SE(3) aligning preoperative CT to intraoperative X-ray
- 分辨原模型的必要计算机制、本地缩小配置和没有覆盖的原训练阶段。
- 从数据、损失、梯度到日志完成可检查的学习记录，保留失败与不确定性。

**已知差异 / Limitations**

- Normalized parallel-beam renderer; no calibrated cone-beam DiffDRR, patient-specific published pretraining or geodesic pose loss.
- Synthetic teaching data only; no clinical validity or published benchmark is claimed.
