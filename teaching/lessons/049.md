# 049 · Variational Network

[教学网站](https://happymie.github.io/teaching/#model=49) · [返回覆盖表](../docs/COVERAGE.md) · [原图谱结构与数学](https://happymie.github.io/biomed-ai-atlas/#/model/architecture_model_049_variational_network)

**实现状态：独立论文结构或方程实现 / Paper port**

把多线圈 MRI 的变分梯度下降展开，并联合学习卷积滤波器、势函数与每级更新权重。

Unrolls K steps of gradient descent on a generalized Fields-of-Experts variational energy for multi-coil MRI, jointly learning convolutional filters, non-linear potentials, and data weights.

## 1. 输入、输出与学习目标

- 原模型输入：欠采样多线圈 k-space y、采样掩膜 P 与线圈灵敏度 S / Undersampled multi-coil k-space data y, sampling mask P, and pre-estimated coil sensitivity maps S
- 原模型输出：K 次变分更新后的复数 MRI 图像 x_hat / Complex-valued reconstructed MRI image x^{(K)} after K unrolled variational steps
- 分辨原模型的必要计算机制、本地缩小配置和没有覆盖的原训练阶段。
- 从数据、损失、梯度到日志完成可检查的学习记录，保留失败与不确定性。

**已知差异 / Limitations**

- Small filter bank and three stages; synthetic smooth coil maps are supplied, not learned.
- Synthetic teaching data only; no clinical validity or published benchmark is claimed.
