**实现状态：独立论文结构或方程实现 / Paper port**

RealNVP 以三角 Jacobian 的 affine coupling 获得同时精确可逆、可采样且可算 log-likelihood 的映射。

Constructs an exactly invertible normalizing flow using stacked affine coupling layers whose triangular Jacobians permit O(D) log-determinant and exact likelihood evaluation.

**已知差异 / Limitations**

- Vectorized single-scale coupling, uniform dequantization, small MLPs; no original multiscale image architecture.
- Synthetic teaching data only; no clinical validity or published benchmark is claimed.
