Domain-specific CLIP trained on PMC-15M (15 million biomedical figure–caption pairs from PubMed Central) using a ViT vision tower and PubMedBERT text tower.

- 原模型输入：生物医学图像/论文图与对应 caption / Biomedical image (radiology, pathology, microscopy, chart) + scientific figure caption
- 原模型输出：开放词汇生物医学图文共享嵌入 / Open-vocabulary biomedical multimodal embeddings for retrieval, zero-shot classification, and VQA
- 分辨原模型的必要计算机制、本地缩小配置和没有覆盖的原训练阶段。
