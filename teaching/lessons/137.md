# 137 · ECGFounder

[教学网站](https://happymie.github.io/teaching/#model=137) · [返回覆盖表](../docs/COVERAGE.md) · [原图谱结构与数学](https://happymie.github.io/biomed-ai-atlas/#/model/architecture_model_137_ecgfounder)

**实现状态：复用上游实现 / Upstream adapter**

ECGFounder 是 RegNet/Net1D 风格的 1D CNN foundation model，以超过千万波形和 150 类监督规模为核心，不应误写为通用 Transformer SSL。

1D RegNet-style residual CNN with Squeeze-and-Excitation blocks pretrained via multi-label clinical supervision across >10 million 12-lead ECGs and 150 diagnostic categories.

## 1. 输入、输出与学习目标

- 原模型输入：标准化的 12 导联或适配后的任意单导联 ECG 波形 / Raw multi-lead (12-lead) or lead-masked single-lead 1D ECG voltage waveform tensor
- 原模型输出：150 类诊断概率、通用 ECG embedding 与下游微调预测 / 150-class diagnostic probability vector and general-purpose 1D ECG representation
- 分辨原模型的必要计算机制、本地缩小配置和没有覆盖的原训练阶段。
- 从数据、损失、梯度到日志完成可检查的学习记录，保留失败与不确定性。

## 3. 本地实现与 CPU 配置

The authors' Net1D: SAME-padded grouped 1D bottlenecks, squeeze-excitation, residual downsampling, global time pooling and a task head.


配置直接来自实际注册表。宽度、深度、头数或网格尺寸的改变必须满足结构约束；不要把缩小配置的结果写成原模型结果。

**已知差异 / Limitations**

- Architecture-only exercise: no ten-million-recording pretraining, 150-label diagnostic ontology, lead-dropout pretraining or published checkpoint is included.
- The default reads one synthetic waveform channel; it is not a physiological ECG or a validated single-lead disease predictor.
- Original seven-stage block counts and kernel size 16 are retained, but channels are reduced and the downstream head has three synthetic classes.
- No external weights are loaded. Low compute does not recreate the learned knowledge of ECGFounder.
