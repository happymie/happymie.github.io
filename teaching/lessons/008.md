MONAI VNet residual volumetric stages with 5x5x5 kernels; soft Dice training objective.

- Random initialization and synthetic data; no original benchmark result or pretrained capability is reproduced.
- MONAI implementation with ELU, dropout disabled and two output classes; 32-cube teaching input.
