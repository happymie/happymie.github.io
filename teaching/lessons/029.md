**实现状态：部分机制演示 / Mechanism demo**

以 LVD-142M 数据策展配合 DINO+iBOT+KoLeo 复合自监督，并通过跨尺寸蒸馏形成模型家族。

Combines automated deduplication/retrieval curation (LVD-142M) with a composite DINO + iBOT + KoLeo objective and multi-resolution distillation.

- 原模型输入：经自动去重、检索与平衡构建的大规模无标签图像池 / Large-scale deduplicated and rebalanced unlabeled image corpus

## 3. 本地实现与 CPU 配置

DINO-style global and masked patch distillation with a nearest-neighbor entropy regularizer.

```json
{
  "width": 32,
  "momentum": 0.99,
  "prototypes": 16
}
```

配置直接来自实际注册表。宽度、深度、头数或网格尺寸的改变必须满足结构约束；不要把缩小配置的结果写成原模型结果。

**已知差异 / Limitations**

- Mechanism subset only: local mini objectives do not reproduce DINOv2 data curation, Sinkhorn teacher balancing, untied heads, large training or learned representations.
