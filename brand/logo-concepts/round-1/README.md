# XIAO / 驍 — Logo Concepts, Round 1

## Brief (assumptions)
- **Name:** 驍 / XIAO.
- **Context:** personal technology / advanced technology brand, used on the Biomedical AI Atlas website and future digital products.
- **Audience:** researchers, engineers, collaborators, and international technology audiences.
- **Desired traits:** elegant, precise, distinctive, calm, forward-looking.
- **Constraints:** strong in monochrome; readable at 16–24 px; usable on light and dark surfaces; avoid literal horse illustrations, gradients, metallic effects, generic shields, circuit traces, and ornamental emblems.

## Concepts
- **A — Aperture:** three solid planes form a forward-facing opening. Recommended starting point because it is abstract and architectural. Risk: can read as a generic chevron unless the geometry is made more ownable.
- **B — Monogram:** an asymmetric X with a central cut. Most direct for digital use. Risk: the X-monogram category is crowded; it needs a stronger relationship to 驍 / XIAO before selection.
- **C — Wordmark:** editorial XIAO lettering with an interrupted O. Most typographic and restrained. Risk: the O is currently exploratory live text/shape, and all lettering must be outlined and optically refined before production.

## Status
These are **round-one concepts**, not approved final assets. The files are intentionally monochrome so the concept is judged by silhouette rather than colour. Do not deploy them as the production logo yet.

## Method
Prepared using the workflow described by the public `logo-design` skill:
1. Establish the brief and assumptions.
2. Explore different mark architectures.
3. Judge in monochrome before colour.
4. Review at small sizes and on contrasting backgrounds.
5. Select one direction before producing the complete logo kit.

Source skill: https://github.com/kaankiziltug/logo-design-skill

## Next review
Choose A, B, C, or request a hybrid. After the direction is approved, refine the geometry, test one-colour/reversed and 16/24/32 px contexts, convert every text element to paths, and prepare the production asset kit. Trademark clearance has not been performed.
